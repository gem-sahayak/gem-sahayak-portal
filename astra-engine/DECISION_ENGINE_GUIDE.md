# ASTRA Decision Intelligence Guide (v1.9.0)

Welcome to **ASTRA Decision Intelligence Engine**, calculating recommendation priority, confidence percentages, weighted scores, and ranking lists.

## Features
- **Recommendation Ranking:** Ranks recommendations by weighted score.
- **Confidence Engine:** Calculates confidence % based on evidence count.
- **Priority Engine:** Categorizes priority into CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW tiers.
