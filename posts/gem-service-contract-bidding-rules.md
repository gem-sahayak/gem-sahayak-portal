# GeM Service Contract Bidding Rules: SLA, Manpower & Security Services Guidelines (2026)

Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par services ki procurement din-ba-din badhti ja rahi hai. Government departments ko daily operations chalane ke liye outsourced manpower, security guards, facilities management, sanitation, aur IT outsourcing providers ki zaroorat hoti hai. 

Lekin, goods/products procurement ke mukable **services bidding** thodi zyada complex hoti hai. Isme strict labour laws, Minimum Wages, EPF/ESIC contribution criteria, aur strict Service Level Agreements (SLAs) shamil hote hain. Agar aap ek service provider hain aur in rules ko detail me nahi samajhte, toh technical rejection ke saath-saath caution money ya Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) forfeit hone ka bada risk rehta hai. Is guide me hum inhi critical service contracts, SLAs, aur manpower/security rules ko easy Hinglish me samjhayenge.

## Introduction to GeM Service Contracts

Bharat sarkar ke various ministries aur PSUs har saal outsourced service vendors ke zariye operations maintain karte hain. In operations me sabse common categories **Manpower Outsourcing (Minimum Wage Basis)** aur **Security Guard Services** hain. GeM portal ne in service contracts ko bid karne aur enforce karne ke liye strict automated systems setup kiye hain.

Naye sellers ke liye sabse badi problem ye hoti hai ki wo bidding price sheet calculate karte waqt standard parameters (jaise EPFO/ESIC calculation, monthly bonus, state versus central minimum wages, and minimum service charge cap) me mistakes kar dete hain. ## Prerequisites

Bid submit karne se pehle verify karein ki aapke paas in qualifications ka active status hai ya nahi:
1. **Fully Verified GeM Seller Profile**: Account validation score minimum 100% complete hona chahiye.
2. **Statutory Registrations**: EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund) aur ESIC (Employees' State Insurance) registration certificates active format me.
3. **Valid PSARA License (Security Guard Bids ke liye)**: PSARA (Private Security Agencies Regulation Act) certificate mandatory hai aur ye usi state ka hona chahiye jahan duty points assign kiye jayenge.
4. **Labor Department Registration**: Labour commissioner se contract labour licence validation (where applicable).
5. **Class 3 DSC Token**: Authorized key signatory ke name par active Digital Signature Certificate for signing bid schedules.

## Step-by-Step Guide with flow diagrams/mermaid blocks

GeM Service Contract Bid submit karne ki systematically complete workflow niche batayi gayi hai:



### Step 1: Tender Identification & SLA Analysis
- **Why**: Sahi requirement parameters match karna bohot critical hota hai taaki delivery issues aur bad reviews na aayein.
- **Action**: GeM account login karein. "Bids > List of Bids" section me jayein, "Services" click karein, aur relevant category chunein. Bid document download karein aur uske **Service Level Agreement (SLA)** aur **Additional Terms & Conditions (ATC)** ko read karein.

### Step 2: Minimum Wages Verification
- **Why**: Under-quoting ya wrong sphere select karne se direct disqualification ho sakti hai.
- **Action**: Identify karein ki tender Central Sphere Minimum Wages par hai ya State Sphere par. Current applicable minimum wages rates download karein (Central Labour Commissioner website se). 

### Step 3: Cost-Breakdown Calculation (Financial Bid Preparation)
- **Why**: Galat percentage fill karne par financial bid sheet reject ho jayegi.
- **Action**: Complete cost component structured sheet create karein:
  - **Basic Wages + VDA** (Variable Dearness Allowance)
  - **EPF Contribution** (13% of basic wage limits)
  - **ESIC Contribution** (3.25% of gross wages)
  - **Statutory Bonus** (Usually 8.33% or actual rules)
  - **Administrative Charges / Service Charge** (Strict floor limit: **3.85%** as per GeM rule).
- **Expected Result**: Compliance ready pricing plan block ready hoga.

### Step 4: Document Bundling & Technical Upload
- **Why**: Adhi-adhuri information se technical evaluation status reject ho jata hai.
- **Action**: Mandated checklists ke PDFs banayein (PSARA, EPF registration, CA audit reports). Files ko GeM portal par upload karein aur verify karein. Digital signature validation complete karein.

### Step 5: Post-Bid Technical Evaluation & Representation Check
- **Why**: Agar technical criteria meet karne ke baad bhi buyer disqualify kar de, toh aap voice raise kar sakte hain.
- **Action**: Bidding dashboard par look rakhhein. Jaise hi technical evaluation stage summary open ho, verify karein. Agar non-compliant remarks dikhein jo unjustified hain, toh **48 hours** ke andar detailed explanation representation box me raise karein.

## Structured FAQs

Niche service bidding and SLAs guidelines ke baare me 15 comprehensive questions aur answers diye gaye hain:

### Q1. GeM portal par minimum service charge limit 3.85% kyun set ki gayi hai?
Predatory pricing (jaise ₹0.01 ya 0% service charge quote karna) ko rokne ke liye, aur contractors dwara basic wages me se commission kaatne se workers ko safe rakhne ke liye GeM ne minimum floor service charge **3.85%** declare kiya hai.

### Q2. Kya main Delhi ka PSARA license UP me hone wale security bids ke liye use kar sakta hoon?
Nahi. PSARA license state-specific hota hai. UP state ke deployment area me activity perform karne ke liye aapke paas UP Private Security Agencies Regulation department dwara issued license hona mandatory hai.

### Q3. Minimum Wages rate Central Sphere ka add karna chahiye ya State Sphere ka?
Yeh bid specification par depend karta hai. Buyer bid guidelines (ATC) me clear mention karte hain ki unhe Central minimum wages follow karne hain ya State specific rates. Hamesha bid details scroll karke wage base check karein.

### Q4. Performance Report (PR) aur CRAC me kya relation hai?
Service execution ke baad, buyer online Performance Report generate karta hai jo system standard (SLA met / penalties) reflect karta hai. Is PR sheet verification ke bad hi Consignee Receipt and Acceptance Certificate (CRAC) generate hota hai jo payment process karta hai.

### Q5. Agar technical bid summary open hone par lagta hai ki buyer ne biasness ki hai, toh appeal kaise karein?
Tender status page par **48 hours ka representation appeal window** milta hai. Aap detailed reply documents online box me submit kar sakte hain. Jab tak issue clarify nahi hoga, system aage proceed nahi karega.

### Q6. EPF and ESIC deduction amounts kya buyer end se change kiye ja sakte hain?
Nahi. Statutory rates fixed hote hain. Agar employees central salary threshold ke criteria me aate hain, toh unka EPF (13%) aur ESIC (3.25%) calculation system auto-evaluate karta hai ya specified rules ke compliance rules run hote hain.

### Q7. Kya micro and small enterprises (MSEs) ko PSARA license se exemption milta hai?
Nahi. MSMEs ko EMD fees and experience preferences me relaxations mil sakte hain, par safety regulations aur laws (jaise PSARA Act, 2005) se koi relief nahi milta.

### Q8. Liquidated damages (LD) penalty ka kya rule hai?
Service scope delivery parameters (SLAs) breach hone par buyer standard penalties deduct karta hai, jo total project value ke **10%** tak accumulate ho sakti hain. Beyond this, contract termination process start ho sakta hai.

### Q9. Bidding ke waqt EMD Exemption declare karne ke liye kya upload karein?
Bidders ko registration details verification block me valid **Udyam Registration Certificate** upload karna hota hai, aur bid details window me EMD Exemption options checklist me "Yes" tick karna hota hai.

### Q10. GeM Pool Account (GPA) se payment kab credit hoti hai?
System rules ke according, CRAC certificate clear hone ke baad buyer ko **10 working days** ke andar payment process karni hoti hai. Delays hone par interest charges calculations system evaluate karta hai.

### Q11. Kya security guard bids ke liye local state office detail verify karna mandatory hai?
Agar buyer organization ne state operations support ke liye tender details (ATC clause) me demand kiya hai, toh vendor ko local office proof sheet upload karni padegi.

### Q12. Bonus rules kya manpower pricing schedule me shamil hote hain?
Haan, Payment of Bonus Act ke tehat statutory minimum bonus (usually 8.33% of wages) pricing configurations ka ek required aspect hota hai, jo monthly basis ya yearly calculation basis par include hota hai.

### Q13. Agar minimum wages mid-contract me rise ho jayein, toh kya revised rate milta hai?
Haan. Government update notifications auto-apply hone ke reference par service provider revised claims raise kar sakta hai. Buyer parameters verify karke extra wages budget approve karte hain.

### Q14. Incomplete documents ke case me "Clarification Request" milti hai kya?
Yeh buyer's discretion par depend karta hai. Kuch bids me minor omissions par clarification window dashboard par display hoti hai jahan 2-3 din me proof reload karna hota hai. Iske baad document review change ho jata hai.

### Q15. GeM system par blacklisting se kaise bachein?
Rules compliance strictly run karein: workers ko bank transfer se directly 7th date tak pay karein, regular EPFO/ESIC deposits verify karwayein, aur contract details verify karne ke bad accurate attendance maintain karein.
