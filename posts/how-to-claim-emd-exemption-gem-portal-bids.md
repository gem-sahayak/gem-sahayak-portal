# GeM Portal Bids Par EMD Exemption Kaise Claim Karein: Complete MSME Guide

Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par jab bhi koi MSME seller tender ya bidding process me participate karta hai, toh ek sabse badi samasya hoti hai **Earnest Money Deposit (EMD)** jama karna. EMD ek tarah ka financial security deposit hota hai (jo tender price ka 2% se 5% hota hai) aur bid fill karne ke sath ise submit karna mandatory hota hai. 

Lekin small businesses ke liye multiple active bids me EMD pay karna unka valuable working capital block kar deta hai. Is guide me hum samjhenge ki kaise aap legally **100% EMD Exemption** (choot) claim kar sakte hain bina koi deposit diye.

---

## ⚡ Quick Answer: EMD Se Bachne Ka Tarika
*   Agar aapke paas **Udyam Registration (MSE)** ya **DPIIT Startup Recognition** hai, toh aap EMD exemption ke liye completely eligible hain.
*   Bidding fill karte waqt profile section me **"Exemption Claimed for EMD"** ko **Yes** select karein.
*   Apna **Udyam Certificate PDF** submit/upload karein aur verify karein ki registered product categories match karti hain.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step EMD Exemption Claim Guide

Active GeM bid me participate karte waqt niche diye gaye steps se waiver claim karein:

### Step 1: Bid Document Check Karein
Bid document download karke check karein ki kya buyer ne **EMD Advisory** me "MSE Exemption Allowed" ya "Startup Exemption Allowed" ko **Yes** mark kiya hai.

### Step 2: Bidding Submission Me Option Select Karein
Bidding process ke dauran jab portal aapse payment options poochega:
1. **"Exemption Claimed for EMD"** toggle par click karke **Yes** karein.
2. Form drop-down me se **"Micro & Small Enterprises (MSE)"** select karein.

### Step 3: Verify NIC Category Codes
Apne Udyam certificate me check karein ki **NIC Code (National Industrial Classification)** aur bid product/service description match kar rahe hain ya nahi. Agar mismatch hoga toh government buyer aapko technical phase me disqualify kar dega.

## 💡 Important Rules & Common Rejection Reasons

*   **NIC Code Mismatch:** Agar bid computer desks ki hai aur aapka Udyam fabric manufacturing ka hai, toh EMD claim reject ho jayega.
*   **Validity of Certificate:** Udyam status verify karein ki active aur non-expired ho.
*   **No Bid Security Declaration:** EMD exemption select karne par aapko portal par dynamic "Bid Security Declaration" verify check box check karna mandatory hota hai.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. GeM portal par EMD waiver kya hota hai?
EMD (Earnest Money Deposit) waiver ka matlab hai ki registered MSMEs bina kisi financial security guarantee deposit ke active tenders me compete kar sakte hain.

### 2. Kya Medium Enterprises ko EMD exemption milti hai?
Nahi. Government Public Procurement policy ke under sirf Micro aur Small (MSEs) enterprises ko hi EMD exemption milti hai.

### 3. DPIIT Startups ko EMD exemption kaise milti hai?
Recognized Startups ko DPIIT recognition certificate upload karke EMD waive-off milta hai, agar unka core product category match karta ho.

### 4. Verification failed hone par EMD forfeit ho sakti hai?
Nahi, lekin agar aap bid win karne ke baad document fake submit karte hain ya execute karne se mana karte hain toh warning incident generate hota hai.

### 5. Kya NSIC registered sellers ko EMD waiver milta hai?
Haan. Valid NSIC (National Small Industries Corporation) certificates wale sellers ko bhi GeM bids par exemption milta hai.

### 6. Bid document me "EMD exemption: No" likha ho toh kya karein?
Kuch custom cases me buyers technical limitations ke chalte exemption block kar dete hain. Aise me pre-bid phase me classification clarification generator request daalein.

### 7. Udyam registered firms ko L1 margin kitna milta hai?
MSEs ko L1 price matching range of L1+15% me preference milti hai, jisme wo L1 price match karke orders ka part secure kar sakte hain.

### 8. Exemption status update check karne me kitna samay lagta hai?
Jaise hi check verification checkbox yes karte hain, verification real-time check database se automatically complete ho jati hai.
