## State Procurement Rules on GeM

Most Indian state governments have mandated their local departments to purchase goods and services through the GeM portal. This includes municipal corporations, state universities, and state-run hospitals.

---

## How to Filter State-wise Bids

1.  Open the active bids dashboard.
2.  Input your primary product keyword.
3.  Under the filters section, select the target **State** or **Consignee Location**.
4.  Apply filters to load bids relevant to that region.
