## Search Keyword Best Practices

To optimize your search results:

1.  **Use Synonyms:** Search for both 'Desktop Computer' and 'Personal Computer'.
2.  **Broaden Terms:** If searching for 'IS 1375 Digital Multimeters', try searching for 'Multimeter' first.
3.  **Check Spelling variations:** Portal search indexes sometimes miss matches due to minor spelling discrepancies.

---

## Building a Search Query

Combine terms logically:
*   Instead of `Stainless Steel Waste Bin for Hospital Room`, search for `Waste Bin` or `Dustbin`.
*   Filter by buyer ministry to narrow down the results after using broad terms.
