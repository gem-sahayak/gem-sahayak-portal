## The challenge of manual tender document reading:

Government bids published on the **government e marketplace** are accompanied by detailed PDF specifications. These documents are often 30 to 80 pages long, packed with complex legal clauses, experience requirements, turnover rules, and compliance criteria.

Reading these PDFs manually is time-consuming and increases the risk of missing critical details, which can lead to technical rejection.

---

## What parameters does the AI PDF analyzer extract?

* **EMD & Tender Fees**: Identifies the exact deposit amount, bank guarantee rules, and exemption eligibility.
* **Turnover Requirements**: Finds the average annual turnover required for the past 3 years.
* **Past Experience**: Summarizes the number of similar works or projects the seller must have completed.
* **Key Dates**: Highlights the bid submission deadline, clarification end date, and bid opening time.
