# GeM Portal Udyam Aadhaar Verification Failure: Solve Mismatch Errors Step-by-Step

Agar aap Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par registration kar rahe hain aur aapka **Udyam Aadhaar verification failed** show ho raha hai, toh aap akele nahi hain. Desh ke hazaron MSME sellers ko profile validation ke waqt "Udyam Details Mismatch" ya "Verification Failed" ka error face karna padta hai. Is error ke aane se aapka profile verification block ho jata hai aur aap government bids me participate nahi kar paate.

Is detail guide me hum samjhenge ki ye error kyun aata hai aur ise step-by-step kaise thik karein.

---

## ⚡ Quick Answer: Udyam Mismatch Kaise Solve Karein?
*   Sabse pehle check karein ki aapka **PAN Card Name**, **Udyam Certificate Name**, aur **GeM Account Name** me ek bhi spelling character ya space ka difference na ho.
*   Agar details match nahi karti hain, toh **Udyam portal** par jaakar update request daalein.
*   Update hone ke baad **48 hours (sync time)** wait karein, fir GeM portal par 'Verify' par click karein.

---

## 🛠️ GeM Verification Failed Hone Ke Mukhya Kaaran

Udyam verification fail hone ke piche niche diye gaye 3 main reasons hote hain:

### 1. Name & PAN Mismatch
GeM portal aapke Udyam certificate ki verification directly Income Tax aur Ministry of MSME ke API database se karta hai. Agar aapke PAN Card par firm ka naam `ABC Enterprises Private Limited` hai aur Udyam par aapne `ABC Enterprises` register kar diya hai, toh data matching error aayega.

### 2. Organization Category Mismatch
Aapki business entity type dono portals par exact match honi chahiye.
*   **Proprietorship** (Individual / Single Owner)
*   **Partnership**
*   **Private Limited Company**

Agar GeM par proprietorship select kiya hai aur Udyam par firm type galat hai, toh validation fail ho jayegi.

### 3. Database Sync Lag (48-Hour Sync Rule)
Bahut se MSME sellers Udyam profile update karte hi turant GeM portal par validation click kar dete hain. Ministry of MSME aur GeM database sync hone me kam se kam **24 se 48 ghante** lagte hain.

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Resolution Guide (Kaise Mismatch Thik Karein)

### Step 1: Udyam Certificate Download Karein
Official [Udyam Registration Portal](https://udyamregistration.gov.in) par jaakar apna updated certificate download karein aur verify karein ki details correct hain ya nahi.

### Step 2: Details Update Request Submit Karein
Agar koi discrepancy milti hai:
1. Udyam portal par **"Update Details"** option par click karein.
2. Mobile OTP verify karke mandatory changes (Name/PAN/GST/Entity Type) apply karein.
3. Update verify ho jaane ke baad certificate download kar lein.

### Step 3: Wait and Sync (48 Hours)
Udyam certificate update hone ke baad minimum **48 hours** tak wait karein taaki backend API data refresh ho sake.

### Step 4: GeM Portal Verification Complete Karein
1. GeM Seller Profile Dashboard par login karein.
2. **My Account** -> **Udyam Aadhaar** section me jayein.
3. Apna Udyam Registration Number aur registered mobile number enter karein.
4. **"Verify Udyam"** par click karein. Aapka status green checkmark ke sath successfully verified dikhega!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. GeM portal par Udyam validation failed kyun dikhata hai?
Jab aapke Udyam certificate ke details (PAN Name, spaces, entity type) GeM profile data se mismatch hote hain, toh validation fail ho jati hai.

### 2. Kya naya Udyam Aadhaar update karte hi validation verify ho jayegi?
Nahi, update karne ke baad kam se kam 24-48 hours wait karein taaki Ministry of MSME database GeM API par refresh ho sake.

### 3. Udyam and PAN card name mismatch kaise solve karein?
Udyam registration portal par update request daal kar apne PAN card ke exact format ke hisab se spelling correction submit karein.

### 4. Verification fail hone se kya mera profile suspended ho sakta hai?
Suspension nahi hota, lekin jab tak Udyam verified nahi hoga, tab tak aapka seller profile incomplete rahega aur aap bids me qualify nahi kar payenge.

### 5. Kya registration verify karne ke liye koi fee lagti hai?
Nahi, GeM portal par profile update aur verification process completely free hai.

### 6. GeM portal par "Udyam OTP not received" issue kaise solve karein?
Check karein ki kya aap wahi mobile number enter kar rahe hain jo Udyam Aadhaar registration ke waqt register kiya tha. Agar number band hai toh pehle Udyam portal par use update karein.

### 7. Kya generic product list karne ke liye Udyam validation zaroori hai?
Haan, primary profile active karne ke liye seller business validation mandatory hai. Uske bina generic ya brand catalog approvals process nahi hote.

### 8. Verification successful ho jane ke baad kya EMD waiver automatically active ho jata hai?
Haan, jaise hi Udyam details verify hoti hain, tender bid submission ke time EMD exemption claims automatically unlock ho jate hain.
