## Standardized Classifications

Classification codes simplify search mappings:
*   **CPV Codes:** Used for global and national product categorization.
*   **GeM Category Codes:** Direct category links that map your seller catalog to active buyer demands.

---

## Why Category Mapping Matters

When a buyer creates a bid, they select a category code. The portal then matches this bid against all sellers who have uploaded catalogs in that category. If your category mapping is incorrect, you will not receive bid notifications.

To optimize your catalog before category mapping, refer to our [OEM Catalog Management Guide](/knowledge/catalog-management/complete-guide).
