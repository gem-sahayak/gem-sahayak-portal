# GeM ePBG Bank Guarantee Rules, Format, Online Submission & Refund Process

Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par jab aap kisi sarkari department ka bid ya direct L1 tender jeet jate hain, toh contract secure karne ke liye buyer aapse ek security money ki demand karta hai. Is security money ko technical terms me **Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG)** ya digital format me **ePBG (Electronic Performance Bank Guarantee)** kaha jata hai.

Agar aap ek GeM seller hain, toh ePBG ke rules, standard formats, online submission process aur safe refund procedures ko samajhna aapke security deposit ko safe rakhne ke liye bohot zaroori hai. Chaliye is detailed guide ke zariye poore ePBG system ko aasan Hinglish me samajhte hain.

---

## ⚡ Quick Answer: ePBG Kya Hai Aur Iska Basic Process Kya Hai?
*   **ePBG Purpose:** Yeh ek security check deposit hai jo guarantee deta hai ki seller contract ke rules aur quality standards ke mutabik supply complete karega.
*   **Amount Limit:** Standard rules ke anusaar yeh contract/order value ka **3% se 10%** hota hai.
*   **Submission Deadline:** Contract award hone ke generally **15 days** ke andar submit karna mandatory hota hai.
*   **Verification:** Portal par automatic validation ke liye physical bank guarantee ke sath-sath bank se **SFMS (Structured Financial Messaging System)** message transmission karwana zaroori hai.
*   **Refund:** Contract execution aur warranty complete hone ke baad buyer portal par **release order** approve karta hai aur aapka BG limit release ho jata hai.

## 1. Introduction to ePBG on GeM
Jab bhi koi buyer GeM portal par heavy equipment supply ya services ka order deta hai, toh financial security ke liye GFR (General Financial Rules) Rule 171 ke under **Performance Security** lena mandatory hai. 

ePBG ka basic maqsad yeh hota hai ki agar koi seller contract terminate karta hai, supply me delay karta hai, ya generic products deliver karta hai, toh buyer bina court case ke us bank guarantee ko local bank se cash karwa (forfeit) sake. Purane dino me BG paper format me submit hoti thi, par ab portal security badhane ke liye **ePBG aur SFMS verification** ko integration standards ke sath mandatory kar diya gaya hai.

## 3. Prerequisites for ePBG Submission
Bank guarantee issue karwane se pehle aapke paas niche diye documents aur accounts hone zaroori hain:
1.  **GeM Sanction / Contract Order Copy:** Isme detailed warranty timeline aur required guarantee percentage likhi hoti hai.
2.  **Buyer Bank Account details & SFMS Code:** Buyer ke bank ka IFSC aur unique SFMS format mapping key.
3.  **Active Commercial Bank Limit:** Aapka business account jis bank me ho, wahan BG limit ya fixed deposit collateral ready hona chahiye.

## 4. Required Documents Checklist

| Document Name | Purpose | Format | Verified By |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **GeM Award of Contract (AOC)** | Base document showing value & requirement | PDF from GeM Portal | Issuing Bank |
| **Standard BG Format Annexure** | Specific language template required by Buyer | Word/PDF from Bid Doc | Issuing Bank |
| **SFMS Form / Request Letter** | Form requesting bank to send SFMS advisory | Physical Form / App | Issuing Bank |
| **Stamp Paper** | Legal backing (normally ₹100 or as per state laws) | Non-judicial Stamp Paper | Treasury / Sub-Registrar |
| **Board Resolution / Authority** | Proves authorization to sign BG documents | PDF/Print on Letterhead | Bank Audit Section |
| **Collateral/FD Receipts** | Margin money to secure the guarantee value | Physical/Digital | Bank Branch Manager |

## 5. Eligibility Criteria Table

| Criteria Parameter | Requirement / Eligibility Condition | Compliance Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Authorized Banks** | Public Sector Banks, Scheduled Private Banks, or foreign banks operating under RBI | Co-operative Banks are generally not accepted on GeM. |
| **Minimum Contract Value** | Usually applicable on contracts above ₹5 Lakhs | Buyer can request even below ₹5 Lakhs if Bid Doc permits. |
| **MSE Exemptions** | No blanket exemption for PBG (Unlike EMD) | MSEs must submit ePBG unless specifically exempted in Bid. |
| **Validity Coverage** | Must cover the entire Delivery + Warranty Period + 60 Days buffer | Non-compliance leads to automatic rejection of BG. |

## 6. Step-by-Step ePBG Online Submission Process

Niche diye flowchart se samajhein ki GeM contract jeetne se lekar verification tak ka process kaise chalta hai:



### Detailed Steps:
1.  **Step 1: SFMS details download karein:** GeM seller portal par login karke "Orders" dashboard par jayein aur "ePBG/Performance Security" tab se buyer ke bank ka SFMS code details copy karein.
2.  **Step 2: Bank me apply karein:** Apne bank branch me contact karein aur required security format ke anusaar bank guarantee request form fill karein.
3.  **Step 3: SFMS Advice Transmit Karayein:** Apne bank se strict verification ensure karein ki woh **SFMS Transmission Protocol (MT760)** ke zariye buyer ke bank ko inform karein. Is alert message me GeM Contract Reference number zaroor hona chahiye.
4.  **Step 4: GeM Portal Par Details Enter Karein:** Jab bank physically/digitally BG ready kar de, tab GeM dashboard par *ePBG details page* open karke BG Number, Date, Issue Bank name, Amount, and SFMS Ref ID input karke documents scanned copy upload karein.
5.  **Step 5: Status Tracking:** Submit karne ke baad, status "Pending Approval" dikhayega. Jab buyer verify kar lega, tab yeh status "Accepted" me change ho jayega.

## 8. Warning: Critical ePBG Rules & Compliance Checklist

> [!WARNING]
> **Avoid These Common ePBG Rejection Mistakes:**
> 1. **Incorrect Validity Period:** Agar aapka contract 12 months ka hai, toh aapka ePBG **12 Months + 60 Days** valid hona chahiye. Ek din ki bhi kam validity hone par buyer use reject kar dega.
> 2. **Missing SFMS Transmission:** Physical stamp paper par BG banwana kafi nahi hai. Agar bank ne system par **SFMS alert** transmit nahi kiya, toh GeM portal automatically verification error generate karega.
> 3. **Non-Scheduled Banks:** Co-operative bank ya local societies se issue kiya gaya BG GeM par valid nahi hota. Humesha Scheduled Commercial Bank se hi BG banwayein.
> 4. **Typo in GeM Contract Number:** SFMS transmission text me Contract Number bilkul sahi hona chahiye. Sahi format ke bina cross-referencing fail ho jayegi.

## 9. Comparison: Manual Bank Guarantee vs. Electronic Bank Guarantee (e-BG)

Procurement industry me ab physical guarantees ko replace karke digital systems ko follow kiya ja raha hai. Niche table me compare karein dono options ko:

| Feature Comparison | Manual Bank Guarantee (with SFMS) | Electronic Bank Guarantee (e-BG) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stamp Paper** | Physical Stamp Paper (purchased manually) | Digital e-Stamp (integrated electronically) |
| **Turnaround Time (TAT)** | 3 to 7 working days | 1 to 24 hours (super fast) |
| **Physical Courier** | Yes, physical BG copy buyer office me submit karni padti hai | No, entirely paperless and securely sent online |
| **Fraud Risk** | Moderate (physical verification dependent) | Zero (digitally signed by NeSL and bank) |
| **Verification Method** | Manual review + SFMS notification lookup | Direct API integration on GeM dashboard |
| **Security Deposit Return** | Physical BG returned to seller for cancellation | Automated digital release notification to bank |

## 11. Structured FAQs (15 Detailed Q&As)

### 1. GeM Portal par ePBG ka full form aur meaning kya hai?
ePBG ka full form Electronic Performance Bank Guarantee hai. Jab koi seller portal par tender jeet jata hai, toh order successfully fulfill karne ki surety ke roop me security deposit electronically bank security details submit karke diya jata hai.

### 2. Kya har ek GeM bid me ePBG submit karna mandatory hai?
Nahi. Yeh tabhi mandatory hota hai jab bid guidelines aur tender document (BID PDF) me specifies kiya jaye. Chote value orders (e.g. ₹5 Lakhs se niche) par generally ePBG nahi liya jata.

### 3. Kya MSME / MSE registered companies ko ePBG se koi exemption milti hai?
Nahi, EMD (Earnest Money Deposit) me MSMEs ko exemptions zaroor milti hai par performance bank guarantee (ePBG) me koi direct automatic exemption nahi milti. Bid terms and GFR 2017 ke rules ke anusaar har ek vendor ko security guarantee deposit karna hi hota hai.

### 4. SFMS (Structured Financial Messaging System) verification kya hota hai?
Yeh bank-to-bank electronic messaging protocol hai. Jab aapka bank ePBG issue karta hai, toh woh buyer ke bank ko "MT760" format me digital notification bhejta hai. Jab tak buyer ke bank ko yeh alert nahi milega, woh aapka digital ePBG approve nahi karega.

### 5. ePBG ki validity kitni honi chahiye?
BG ki validity contract completion period se lekar extra **60 days (buffer period)** tak honi chahiye. Agar aapka contract 1 saal ka hai, toh guarantee minimum 1 saal + 60 days tak active rehni chahiye.

### 6. ePBG verification status 'Pending' kab tak rehta hai?
Ek baar details upload karne ke baad, verification process me generic standard time range **3 se 7 business working days** lagta hai. Is doran buyer bank confirmations crosscheck karta hai.

### 7. Kya main ePBG ki jagah FD (Fixed Deposit) receipt jama kar sakta hoon?
Haan, standard rules ke mutabik FDR (Fixed Deposit Receipt) ya DD (Demand Draft) bhi execute kiya ja sakta hai. FDR ko "Pledged to Buyer Designation" karwana padta hai aur details dashboard par enter karni hoti hain.

### 8. Agar time par ePBG submit na kiya jaye, toh kya hota hai?
Agr standard 15 days deadline (ya contract bid criteria timeline) ke andar performance security jama nahi hoti, toh buyer aapka contract terminate kar sakta hai aur default status create karke EMD forfeit kar sakta hai.

### 9. ePBG refund lene ka process kya hota hai?
Apna delivery aur warranty performance time successfully complete hone ke baad, buyer panel se release request initiation standard rules ke tehat approve karta hai. Digital validation ke baad original bank paper wapas courier ho jata hai.

### 10. Agar buyer order complete hone par bhi ePBG release na kare toh kya karein?
Is tarah ke delay cases me aap portal dashboard se official **Incident** create kar sakte hain aur Department hierarchy me higher authorities ko appeal escalate kar sakte hain. GFR rules ke anusaar 30 days se jyada hold karna violations hai.

### 11. NeSL e-BG kya hai?
National E-Governance Services Limited (NeSL) ke cooperation se paperless bank guarantee release ki jaati hai. Isme system APIs digital signatures aur e-stamping integration check karke instantly BG issue kar deti hain.

### 12. Agar contract value extend hoti hai toh ePBG amendment kaise hota hai?
Contract value badhne par aapko bank se request karke **Amendment Bank Guarantee (with MT767 SFMS message)** issue karana hota hai aur modified values GeM portal par submit karni hoti hain.

### 13. Bank guarantee commission cost kitni aati hai?
Commission charges normal standard banks range ke anusaar **1% se 3% per annum** hoti hain jo collateral limits aur margin amount (fixed deposits) par depend karta hai.

### 14. Kya co-operative bank ka ePBG valid hai?
Nahi, GeM procurement policies ke tehat co-operative bank, Gramin bank ya micro societies ke guarantees standard acceptance parameters me include nahi kiye jate. Scheduled Commercial Bank hi verify hote hain.

### 15. Kya BG cancel hone par commission refund hota hai?
Nahi. Ek baar bank ne guarantee status generate kar diya aur validity cycle trigger ho gayi, toh bank ka transaction commission non-refundable category me aata hai.
