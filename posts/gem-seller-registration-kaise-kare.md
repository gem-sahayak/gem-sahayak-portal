# How to Register as Seller on GeM Portal (Complete Guide 2026)

Welcome to the ultimate guide on how to register as a seller on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) in 2026. The GeM portal has evolved significantly with the introduction of GeM v4, streamlining procurement processes and offering immense opportunities for sellers across India. This guide adheres to the latest General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017 and provides a thorough, step-by-step approach to ensure your registration is successful and compliant.

> 📌 **Important Note:** Registration on the GeM portal is entirely free. Beware of third-party agencies demanding exorbitant fees for basic registration.

## Understanding the GeM Portal and Its Importance

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is an online platform for public procurement in India. It aims to enhance transparency, efficiency, and speed in public procurement. For sellers—ranging from small MSMEs to large corporations—GeM opens the door to a massive market of government buyers, including Central and State Government departments, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), and autonomous bodies.

### Why Register on GeM?

*   **Direct Access to Government Buyers:** Direct reach to thousands of government departments.
*   **No Registration Fee:** The platform does not charge any fee for registering as a seller.
*   **Transparency:** Open and fair bidding processes.
*   **Prompt Payment:** Provisions for timely payments from government entities.
*   **MSME Benefits:** Special provisions and preferences for Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs).

> 📖 **GFR Rule Reference:** As per Rule 149 of the General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017, it is mandatory for Ministries and Departments to procure Goods and Services available on GeM through the GeM portal.

## Pre-requisites for GeM Seller Registration 2026

Before diving into the registration process, it's crucial to have all necessary details and documents ready. The GeM v4 portal has introduced stricter validation checks to ensure authenticity.

### Essential Information Required

1.  **Aadhaar Number or PAN Number:** For identity verification of the primary user.
2.  **Active Mobile Number:** Linked to your Aadhaar/PAN for OTP verification.
3.  **Active Email ID:** An active email address (preferably a domain email for business).
4.  **Business Documents:** GSTIN, Udyam Registration (for MSMEs), CIN (for Companies).
5.  **Bank Account Details:** Account number, IFSC code, and branch details.

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Register as Seller on GeM Portal

Follow this comprehensive, step-by-step guide to complete your registration on the GeM portal.

### Step 1: Access the GeM Portal
Navigate to the official GeM portal (gem.gov.in). Click on the **'Sign Up'** button located at the top right corner of the homepage and select **'Seller'**.

### Step 2: Review Terms and Conditions
You will be presented with the GeM Terms and Conditions. It is highly recommended to read these thoroughly as they govern your conduct and liabilities on the platform. Accept the terms to proceed.

### Step 3: Organization Type Selection
Select your organization type from the dropdown menu. Options typically include:
*   Proprietorship
*   Partnership
*   Private Limited Company
*   Public Limited Company
*   Trust / Society
*   Government Entity

### Step 4: Primary User Verification
The Primary User is the individual responsible for managing the GeM account. You must verify the Primary User using either their Aadhaar Number or Personal PAN.

*   **Aadhaar Verification:** Enter the Aadhaar number and the linked mobile number. An OTP will be sent for verification.
*   **PAN Verification:** Enter the PAN and the name as per PAN.

> ⚠️ **Critical Warning:** Ensure that the mobile number linked to the Aadhaar/PAN is active, as all critical OTPs for profile updates and bidding will be sent to this number.

### Step 5: Fill in Organization Details
Once the Primary User is verified, enter the specific details of your organization:
*   Company Name
*   Date of Incorporation
*   Registered Address

### Step 6: Verify Email ID
Enter a valid email address. An OTP will be sent to this email. Enter the OTP to verify your email address. This email will be used for all official correspondence from GeM.

### Step 7: Create Login Credentials
Choose a unique User ID and a strong password for your GeM account. Make sure to securely store these credentials.

## Completing Your Seller Profile

Creating an account is only the first part. To start selling, you must complete your Seller Profile and achieve a 100% profile completion status. The GeM v4 portal requires meticulous detail.

### 1. PAN Validation
Provide your business PAN (or personal PAN for proprietorships) for validation with the Income Tax Database.

### 2. Company Details & Address Verification
Enter your registered office address and any additional billing or shipping addresses. You will need to verify the primary address.

### 3. Bank Account Validation
Enter your bank account details. The GeM portal will initiate a micro-deposit (often Re. 1) to verify the account.

| Field | Description | Importance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Bank Name | Name of the bank | Essential for routing payments |
| Account Number | Exact account number | Must match business name |
| IFSC Code | 11-character code | Crucial for NEFT/RTGS transfers |
| Account Type | Current or Savings | Current account preferred for businesses |

### 4. Tax and E-Invoicing
Provide your GSTIN. The portal will validate it with the GSTN database. Ensure your GST returns are filed up-to-date, as GeM often checks compliance status.

### 5. MSME/Udyam Registration (Optional but Recommended)
If you are an MSME, linking your Udyam Registration number provides significant benefits, including exemption from Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) and preference in procurement.

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Always keep your Udyam and GST certificates updated. Discrepancies between GeM data and official government databases can lead to account suspension.

## Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

*   **Name Mismatch:** The name on the Aadhaar/PAN must exactly match the records. Even a minor spelling difference will cause validation failure.
*   **Inactive Mobile Number:** Ensure the Aadhaar-linked mobile is always accessible.
*   **Incorrect Bank Details:** Double-check IFSC codes and account numbers to prevent payment delays.
*   **Ignoring Profile Updates:** Regularly update your profile, especially when renewing certificates like Udyam or Startup India.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: Is GST mandatory for GeM registration?**
A: Yes, in most cases, GST registration is mandatory for selling goods and services on GeM. Exemptions are rare and apply only to specific categories of unorganized sector sellers.

**Q2: Can I change my Primary User later?**
A: Yes, GeM allows the transfer of the Primary User role, but it requires verification of both the old and new users.

**Q3: How long does the registration process take?**
A: If all documents and OTPs are readily available, the initial sign-up takes less than 15 minutes. However, completing the profile to 100% and getting validations may take 24-48 hours.

**Q4: What happens if my Aadhaar is not linked to a mobile number?**
A: You cannot proceed with Aadhaar-based registration. You must either update your Aadhaar with a mobile number at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra or use the Personal PAN verification method.

**Q5: Is GeM registration valid for a lifetime?**
A: Yes, the basic registration is valid indefinitely. However, you must maintain active compliance (like filing GST) and keep your profile updated.
