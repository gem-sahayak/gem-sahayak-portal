## Introduction

Bharat sarkar ke vibhinna mantralaya, vibhag aur PSU har saal hazaron karod rupaye ke saamaan aur services ki procurement karte hain. Is khareedari ka 100% execution **Government e Marketplace (GeM)** portal ke zariye hota hai.

Agar aap apne business ko expand karna chahte hain aur government contracts me hissedaari lena chahte hain, toh sabse pehla step hai **gem par seller registration kaise kare** ki sahi prakriya ko samajhna. GeM par register hote hi aapka business lakho sarkari buyers ki najar me aa jata hai.

Is exhaustive guide me hum aapko registration ke har ek step, verification nuances, common errors aur profile completion ke best practices ki jaankari denge, taaki aap bina kisi agent ki madad ke apna seller profile khud setup kar sakein.

## Who Should Read This Guide

Yeh guide vishesh roop se in sabhi business categories ke liye taiyar ki gayi hai:

## Prerequisites Checklist

Registration form start karne se pehle ye 5 cheezein zaroor verify kar lein:

- [ ] **Aadhaar Mobile Link**: Authorized person ka mobile number Aadhaar se linked ho (OTP aana mandatory hai).
- [ ] **Active PAN Card**: Business entity ya proprietor ka valid active PAN card.
- [ ] **Udyam Number**: Active Udyam Registration Certificate Number (MSME benefit ke liye).
- [ ] **Active Bank Account**: Business Name / Proprietor Name par active Savings ya Current Account.
- [ ] **Working Email ID**: Business ka official email access jahan OTP aur verification link receive ho sake.

## Step-by-Step Guide: GeM Par Seller Registration Kaise Kare

### Step 1: Official Portal Par Portal Access Karein
- **Why**: Sahi official portal par jana security aur compliance ke liye zaroori hai.
- **Action**: Apne browser me `gem.gov.in` open karein. Top navigation bar me **Register > Seller** par click karein.
- **Expected Result**: GeM Terms and Conditions (GTC) page screen par display hoga.
- **Common Mistake**: Kisi third-party website ya ad link par click kar dena.
- **Pro Tip**: Hamesha URL check karein ki wo `.gov.in` domain par hi ending ho.

### Step 2: Terms and Conditions Review & Accept Karein
- **Why**: GeM GTC guidelines ko agree karna legal requirement hai.
- **Action**: GTC document ko read karein, declaration checkbox ko tick karein aur **Proceed** par click karein.
- **Expected Result**: Organization details selection form open ho jayega.
- **Common Mistake**: बिना T&C padhe proceed karna aur baad me compliance issue face karna.
- **Pro Tip**: GTC Clause 2.1 ko dhyan se padhein jo user account security ko explain karta hai.

### Step 3: Business Constitution Select Karein
- **Why**: Apke business legal structure ke basis par verification documents decide hote hain.
- **Action**: Dropdown se apna constitution chunein (Proprietorship, Partnership, Company, LLP, Trust, Society). Firm ka official naam type karein.
- **Expected Result**: Next section me Aadhaar/PAN verification window load hogi.
- **Common Mistake**: Proprietorship firm hote hue "Company" Option select kar lena.
- **Pro Tip**: Firm ka naam bilkul wahi rakhein jo PAN aur GST certificate par likha hai.

### Step 4: Personal Identity Verification (Aadhaar OTP)
- **Why**: Account owner ki authenticity verify karne ke liye.
- **Action**: Authorized signatory ka Aadhaar Number enter karein. Registered mobile par aaye OTP ko enter karke verify karein.
- **Expected Result**: Screen par "Aadhaar Verified Successfully" ka message aayega.
- **Common Mistake**: Key Manager ka Aadhaar daalne ke bajaye kisi staff member ka Aadhaar daal dena.
- **Pro Tip**: Primary User hamesha firm ke main decision maker ko hi banana chahiye.

### Step 5: PAN Verification & User Credentials Creation
- **Why**: Tax compliance verify karne aur account login ID set karne ke liye.
- **Action**: Business PAN number enter karein. System PAN status verify karega. Iske baad ek Unique User ID aur Strong Password create karein.
- **Expected Result**: User account credentials reserve ho jayenge.
- **Common Mistake**: Weak password banana jo security check pass na kare.
- **Pro Tip**: User ID me kam se kam 6 alphanumeric characters rakhein aur use secure jagah note karein.

### Step 6: Official Email Verification
- **Why**: Official communication aur tender notifications pane ke liye.
- **Action**: Business Email Address daalein. Inbox me aaye verification OTP/Link ko enter karke verify karein.
- **Expected Result**: Email verification complete hoga aur Primary Account active ho jayega.
- **Common Mistake**: Personal spam email ID daal dena jo daily check na hoti ho.
- **Pro Tip**: Dedicated business email ID (`procurement@yourcompany.com`) ka upayog karein.

### Step 7: Profile Completion (Address, Udyam, Bank Account)
- **Why**: 100% complete profile wale sellers hi catalogue list aur bids me participate kar sakte hain.
- **Action**: Dashboard me login karke Company Address, Udyam Registration, GSTIN, aur Bank Account (Account Number + IFSC) details fill karein.
- **Expected Result**: Profile Completion Status 100% show karega aur account selling ke liye ready ho jayega.
- **Common Mistake**: Profile 100% hone se pehle hi catalogue upload karne ki koshish karna.
- **Pro Tip**: Bank account penny-drop test pass hote hi aapka account instant verify ho jata hai.

## Common Errors & Solutions

### 1. "Aadhaar Name Mismatch with PAN"
- **Reason**: Aadhaar card par naam ki spelling PAN card ki spelling se alag hona.
- **Solution**: Registration se pehle UIDAI ya Income Tax portal par spelling correction karayein. Sahi initials ka upayog karein.

### 2. "Penny Drop Verification Failed"
- **Reason**: Bank Account me firm ka naam aur GeM account ka name match na hona, ya galat IFSC code fill karna.
- **Solution**: Active Current Account ki details daalein. Bank Passbook/Cancelled Cheque ke exact Name & IFSC verify karein.

### 3. "GSTIN Already Registered on GeM"
- **Reason**: Aapki firm ka GST number pehle kisi purane account me use ho chuka hai.
- **Solution**: Purane account ko recover karein ya GeM Helpdesk se Primary User ID transfer request raise karein.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Kya GeM seller registration ke liye koi charge Dena padta hai?
Nahi, GeM portal par seller registration bilkul free hai. Government koi registration fee nahi charge karti.

### Q2. Kya Bina GST Number ke GeM par seller account ban sakta hai?
Haan, agar aapka business turnover GST limit se kam hai ya aap exempted goods deal karte hain, toh aap turnover self-declare karke bina GSTIN ke register kar sakte hain.

### Q3. Proprietorship firm ke liye kiska Aadhaar daalna hoga?
Proprietorship firm ke case me business owner (Proprietor) ka Aadhaar number aur Aadhaar-linked mobile number hi daalna hoga.

### Q4. GeM registration me kitna time lagta hai?
Agar aapke paas saare required documents aur active Aadhaar OTP available hain, toh registration 15 se 20 minute me poora ho jata hai.

### Q5. Udyam Registration link karne se kya fayda hota hai?
Udyam link karne se MSME sellers ko tender participation fee me waiver, EMD Exemption, aur purchase preference jaise sarkar dwara diye gaye fayde milte hain.

### Q6. "Penny Drop Failed" error aaye toh kya karein?
Iska matlab hai ki aapke bank account ka naam aur GeM profile name match nahi kar raha. Active bank passbook/cancelled cheque ke according details re-enter karein.

### Q7. Primary User ID change ho sakti hai kya?
Haan, official authorization letter aur GeM Helpdesk portal par request submit karke Primary User transfer kiya ja sakta hai.

### Q8. Registration ke baad pehla order kaise milega?
Registration ke baad profile 100% complete karein, products ko accurate pricing par list karein, aur L1 Direct Purchase ya Tender Bidding me participate karein.

### Q9. Kya SC/ST aur Women Entrepreneurs ko koi special benefit milta hai?
Haan, GeM portal par Special Vendor Filter hota hai jisse sarkari buyers SC/ST aur Women MSMEs se priority procurement kar sakte hain.

### Q10. Kya ek email ID se multiple GeM accounts banaye ja sakte hain?
Nahi, ek official email ID aur PAN number sirf ek hi Primary Seller Account se linked ho sakta hai.

### Q11. Caution Money deposit kab karna hota hai?
Profile completion ke baad jab aap high-value bids me participate karte hain ya products list karte hain, tab dashboard par Caution Money deposit option enable hota hai.

### Q12. GeM seller registration ke baad customer support kaha se milega?
GeM official toll-free helpline number, email support, aur regional GeM Samvaad centers se help le sakte hain.
