GeM portal par **Office, School, aur Institutional Furniture** (Chairs, Workstations, Desks, Storage) high-volume procurement categories me se hai.

> 📌 **Answer Capsule**  
> **In one line:** GeM furniture tenders evaluate material specifications, ergonomic design, and technical certifications specified in the tender PDF.  
> **What matters:** Certifications like BIFMA, ISO 9001/14001, or GreenGuard apply *only where specified in the applicable bid or category requirements*; verify steel gauge, wood density, and OEM authorization.  
> **What can change:** Specific parameters, test standards, and certification mandates vary by category and buyer requirements.

## 1. Technical Evaluation Checkpoints in Furniture Bids

- **Material & Dimensions:** Sheet metal gauge (e.g. CRCA steel), board density (MDF/Particle board grade), aur upholstery fabric GSM.
- **Testing & Quality Certifications:** Lab test reports and certifications *only where mandated in the bid ATC document*.
- **OEM Authorization:** Valid MAF letter for resellers when mandated by buyer terms.
