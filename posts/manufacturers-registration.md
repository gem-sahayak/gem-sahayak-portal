Manufacturing entities register on the Government e-Marketplace under a specific category called **Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs)**. Registering as an OEM allows you to list your own brands, manage distributor networks, and participate in manufacturing tenders.

This guide explains the OEM registration and vendor assessment process.

## The OEM Onboarding Process

### Step 1: Initial Account Signup
Register your primary seller account on the GeM portal. Select your business type and verify your profile details.

### Step 2: Request Vendor Assessment
1. Navigate to the **Vendor Assessment** tab in your dashboard.
2. Select the product categories (HSN/NIC codes) you manufacture.
3. Pay the assessment fee to the Quality Council of India (QCI).
4. *Exemption:* If you have a valid NSIC registration matching your products, input your credentials to apply for the vendor assessment exemption.

### Step 3: Desktop and Physical Audit Verification
*   **Desktop Audit:** Upload documents showing factory licenses, pollution NOCs, machinery lists, and quality certifications.
*   **Video Assessment:** Complete the remote video audit using the QCI assessment app to verify manufacturing machinery and capacity.

---

## Key Benefits of OEM Status

*   **Catalog Control:** You can list your brand catalog and authorize distributors or resellers to sell your products.
*   **Direct Purchase Visibility:** OEM catalogs are labeled on search results, giving buyers confidence to purchase directly.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

*   *Assessment Category Mismatch:* Pay the assessment fee only for the specific categories you manufacture. If your machinery does not match your listed category, QCI will reject the audit.
*   *Incomplete Factory Documentation:* Ensure factory licenses and utility bills are active and in the company name before scheduling the audit.
