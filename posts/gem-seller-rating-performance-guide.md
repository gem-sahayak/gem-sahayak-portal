# GeM Seller Rating & Performance Score Guide

> [!TIP]
> **Quick Answer:** GeM Seller Rating ek transparent grading system hai (1.0 to 5.0). Ye aapki delivery speed, quality acceptance, aur order fulfillment history par base karta hai. High rating = More Orders.

## 1. Introduction
Jaise e-commerce platforms par customer reviews matter karte hain, waise hi GeM par Seller Rating matter karti hai. Kai tenders mein buyers minimum rating (e.g., 4.0 stars) set kar dete hain, jisse low-rated sellers automatically disqualify ho jate hain.

## 11. Structured FAQs
**Q1: How often is the rating updated?**
A: GeM updates the seller rating dynamically based on a rolling period of recent transaction data.

**Q2: Can I manually request a rating review?**
A: No, it is purely algorithmic. You just need to perform better on future orders to pull the average up.
