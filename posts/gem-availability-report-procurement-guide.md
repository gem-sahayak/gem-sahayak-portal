Public procurement compliance me government buyer departments ke liye items buy karne se pehle GeM portal par search record establish karna mandatory hota hai.

> 📌 **Answer Capsule**  
> **In one line:** A GeM Availability Report documents whether a required product or service is listed in standard GeM catalogs under GFR Rule 149.  
> **What matters:** An Availability Report is an official search record; it does NOT automatically constitute a Non-Availability Certificate (NOC); non-availability does not automatically permit outside procurement without proper administrative authorization under GFR rules.  
> **What can change:** Direct purchase thresholds, catalog search workflows, and departmental procurement delegation rules depend on current GFR and portal guidelines.

## 1. GFR Rule 149 Context & Availability Search

General Financial Rules (GFR 2017) ke Rule 149 ke under government buyers items procure karne se pehle GeM availability search perform karte hain to establish audit trail.

---

## 2. Availability Report vs Non-Availability Document

| Feature | GeM Availability Report | Non-Availability Document Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Confirms items exist in GeM catalog | Records absence of exact catalog match |
| **Procurement Path** | Leads to Direct Purchase or GeM Bidding | Custom Bidding on GeM or GFR-governed route |
| **Audit Function** | Proof of compliance with GFR 149 | Supporting documentation for procurement file |
