Targeting specific buyer ministries allows you to customize your bidding quotes to their requirements. This guide explains how to filter tenders by buyer organization.

---

## Buyer Categories on GeM

Government buyers on the portal are grouped into:
*   **Central Ministries:** e.g. Ministry of Communications, Ministry of Defence.
*   **State Departments:** Local state-level administrative bodies.
*   **Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs):** Government-owned enterprises.
*   **Autonomous Bodies:** State-run schools, councils, and research centers.

---

## How to Search by Ministry

*   On the bids page, select the **Ministry/Department** filter dropdown.
*   Input the target ministry name.
*   The dashboard will reload to display only bids published by that ministry.

To understand how these ministries evaluate single bids, refer to our [GeM Single Bid Evaluation Rules](/guides/single-bid-compliance) guide.
