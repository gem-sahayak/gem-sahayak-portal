# GeM Vendor Assessment Process Explained

For sellers looking to establish themselves as Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM), passing the Vendor Assessment is the ultimate milestone. Unlike standard profile completion, the Vendor Assessment is a rigorous, third-party audit designed to verify your manufacturing capabilities, financial stability, and quality control processes.

In 2026, the GeM portal, in collaboration with the Quality Council of India (QCI) and RITES, updated the assessment protocols to ensure only legitimate manufacturers supply to government departments. This guide explains the entire GeM Vendor Assessment process, the criteria evaluated, and how you can prepare to clear it on the first attempt.

## What is GeM Vendor Assessment?

Vendor Assessment is a mandatory evaluation for any seller wanting to publish products under the "OEM" tag in specific, highly regulated categories (like IT hardware, medical equipment, and heavy machinery). It prevents traders or resellers from masquerading as manufacturers, ensuring the government gets original warranties, better pricing, and quality assurance.

> 📖 **GFR Rule Reference:** Under GFR 2017 policies promoting "Make in India," procuring entities give significant preference to domestic OEMs. The Vendor Assessment acts as the official validation of your manufacturing capacity and domestic value addition.

## Exemptions from Vendor Assessment

Not every seller needs an assessment. You are exempt if you fall into one of these categories:
1. **Resellers:** If you are selling authorized products of another OEM.
2. **Central/State PSUs:** Government-owned enterprises are exempt.
3. **NSIC Registered MSMEs:** Micro and Small Enterprises holding a valid NSIC certificate for the specific product categories are exempt.
4. **BIS Licensed:** Sellers with valid BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) licenses for the specific products (though profile mapping is required).

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** If you are an MSME manufacturer, prioritize getting NSIC registration first. It automatically exempts you from the GeM Vendor Assessment fee and process, saving you significant time.

## The 4-Stage Vendor Assessment Process

If you do not qualify for an exemption, you must undergo the standard assessment process. Here is the step-by-step breakdown:

### Step 1: Application and Fee Payment
- Navigate to `My Account > Vendor Assessment` on your GeM dashboard.
- Initiate the assessment for the desired product categories.
- Pay the non-refundable Vendor Assessment fee (approximately ₹11,200 + GST, subject to GeM's current pricing).

### Step 2: Desktop Assessment (Document Review)
Once the fee is paid, a QCI or RITES auditor is assigned to your case. You must upload a comprehensive set of documents for desktop review.
**Required Documents Include:**
- Factory License / DIC Registration.
- Machinery and Plant details (invoices, photos).
- Electricity Bills for the manufacturing unit indicating industrial usage.
- Raw material procurement invoices.
- Quality Management System certificates (e.g., ISO 9001).
- Financial statements (Balance Sheet, P&L) for the last 3 years.

### Step 3: Video Assessment (Virtual Audit)
GeM transitioned heavily to digital audits to speed up the process. 
- The auditor will schedule a live video call via a mobile app.
- You must walk the auditor through your manufacturing facility live.
- They will verify the machinery, raw material inventory, production line, testing equipment, and finished goods storage.
- Geotagging is used to ensure the location matches the address provided in Step 2.

### Step 4: Final Report and OEM Dashboard Activation
- After the virtual audit, the agency submits a comprehensive report to GeM.
- If approved, your profile receives the "OEM" status.
- You gain access to the dedicated OEM Dashboard, allowing you to manage resellers, authorize dealers, and control the cataloging of your proprietary products.

## Evaluation Criteria Checklists

Assessors look for hard evidence across three main pillars. Use this checklist to prepare:

| Evaluation Pillar | What the Auditor Looks For | Evidence Required |
|-------------------|----------------------------|-------------------|
| **Manufacturing Capacity** | Does the facility exist and have the capacity to produce the claimed volumes? | Machinery invoices, factory layout, live video tour of production lines. |
| **Quality Control** | Is there a standardized process to ensure product quality? | In-house testing lab setup, calibration certificates, ISO certifications. |
| **Financial/Operational Health**| Is the business viable and legally compliant? | Industrial electricity bills, PF/ESIC challans for workers, audited financials. |

## Common Reasons for Assessment Rejection

Understanding why applications fail can help you avoid costly mistakes.
1. **Mismatch of Address:** The factory address on your documents does not perfectly match the geotagged location during the video audit.
2. **Traders Posing as OEMs:** Unable to show raw material conversion. Simply assembling finished imported parts often leads to rejection unless sufficient domestic value addition is proven.
3. **Lack of Testing Equipment:** Claiming high-quality manufacturing but lacking basic calibration tools or testing setups during the virtual tour.

> ⚠️ **Critical Warning:** If your Vendor Assessment is rejected, you will lose the application fee. You must rectify the highlighted issues and re-apply, paying the fee again. Thorough preparation is essential.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: How long does the entire Vendor Assessment process take?**
*Answer:* If your documentation is flawless and you schedule the video audit promptly, the process usually takes 15 to 25 working days.

**Q2: Is Vendor Assessment required for service providers?**
*Answer:* No. Vendor Assessment is specifically designed for product manufacturers (OEMs). Service providers undergo a different validation process based on past experience and SLA capabilities.

**Q3: My Vendor Assessment was rejected. Can I appeal?**
*Answer:* Yes, GeM provides a window to submit a representation if you feel the rejection was unjustified. However, you must provide strong documentary evidence to overturn the decision.

**Q4: Do I need a separate assessment for every new product I manufacture?**
*Answer:* Not necessarily. Assessment is generally category-based. However, if you add a radically different product line (e.g., you manufacture furniture and want to start manufacturing computers), a new assessment may be required.

## Key Takeaways

- Vendor Assessment is the gateway to becoming a recognized OEM on GeM, unlocking exclusive bidding rights and reseller management.
- Ensure you check for exemptions (like NSIC or BIS) before paying the assessment fee.
- The process heavily relies on the Desktop document review and the live Video Audit of your factory.
- Transparency regarding manufacturing capacity and quality control is critical for approval.
