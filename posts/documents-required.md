# GeM Seller Registration Documents Required (Complete Checklist)

Registering on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is a transformative step for any business aiming to supply to government departments. However, the process on the GeM v4 portal demands meticulous documentation. Failing to provide accurate and valid documents can lead to registration delays or rejection. This guide provides a comprehensive checklist of all the documents required for GeM seller registration in 2026, categorized by business type.

> 📌 **Important Note:** All documents submitted on the GeM portal are verified against central government databases (like MCA, Income Tax, GSTN) in real-time. Ensure your documents are up-to-date and names match exactly across all records.

## Universal Documents Required for All Entities

Regardless of your business constitution (Proprietorship, Partnership, Company, etc.), certain core documents are universally required to initiate the registration process.

### Primary User Identity Documents
The Primary User is the authorized representative who manages the GeM account.

1.  **Aadhaar Card:** Must be linked to an active mobile number for OTP verification.
2.  **PAN Card:** Personal PAN of the Primary User.
3.  **Active Mobile Number:** For receiving OTPs and notifications.
4.  **Active Email Address:** Official communication email.

> ⚠️ **Critical Warning:** Ensure that the mobile number linked to the Aadhaar card is currently active and accessible. Without it, you cannot complete the Aadhaar-based primary user verification.

## Specific Documents Based on Business Constitution

The GeM portal requires specific business documents based on your legal entity type.

### 1. Proprietorship Firm
For a sole proprietorship, the business and the owner are considered the same entity for tax purposes.

| Document | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| Personal PAN of Proprietor | Identity and tax validation |
| GST Registration Certificate | Business validation and tax compliance |
| Bank Account Details | Current account in the name of the firm |
| Address Proof | Electricity bill, rent agreement, or property tax receipt |

### 2. Partnership Firm
Partnership firms require documentation that establishes the partnership and the authority of the acting partner.

| Document | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| Firm's PAN Card | Tax validation for the firm |
| Partnership Deed | Proof of partnership constitution |
| GST Registration Certificate | Business validation |
| Authorization Letter | Letter authorizing the Primary User, signed by all partners |
| Bank Account Details | Firm's current account details |

### 3. Private Limited / Public Limited Company
Companies registered under the Companies Act have a more rigorous documentation requirement.

| Document | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| Company PAN Card | Tax validation |
| Certificate of Incorporation (CIN) | Proof of legal existence |
| GST Registration Certificate | Business validation |
| Board Resolution | Authorizing the Primary User to manage the GeM account |
| Bank Account Details | Company's current account details |

> 📖 **GFR Rule Reference:** Compliance with documentation ensures that only legitimate businesses participate in public procurement, aligning with the transparency mandates of GFR 2017.

## Additional Documents for Special Categories

Depending on the nature of your business and the benefits you wish to claim, you may need additional documentation.

### MSME / Udyam Registration
If your business qualifies as a Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise (MSME), you should link your Udyam Registration.

*   **Document:** Udyam Registration Certificate.
*   **Benefit:** Exemption from Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) and preference in certain tenders.

### Startup India Recognition
Startups recognized by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) enjoy specific exemptions.

*   **Document:** DIPP / DPIIT Recognition Certificate.
*   **Benefit:** Exemptions from prior experience and turnover criteria in many bids.

### Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) Verification
If you manufacture the products you sell, you must register as an OEM.

*   **Documents:** Trademark Registration Certificate, NSIC Certificate, or specific manufacturing licenses (e.g., FSSAI, Drug License) depending on the product category.

## Common Documentation Errors to Avoid

1.  **Name Mismatches:** The name on the GeM portal must exactly match the name on the PAN and GST certificates. Even slight variations (like missing initials) can cause automated validation failures.
2.  **Expired Documents:** Ensure that any licenses or certificates (like FSSAI or ISO) uploaded are currently valid.
3.  **Illegible Scans:** If manual upload is required, ensure documents are scanned clearly in high resolution.
4.  **Incorrect Bank Details:** A common error is providing savings account details for a corporate entity or mistyping the IFSC code.

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Keep a digital folder containing all these documents in PDF format before you start the registration process on the GeM portal. This will save time and prevent session timeouts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: Can I register on GeM without a GST certificate?**
A: Generally, GST is mandatory. However, specific categories (like artisans or specific unorganized sectors) might have exemptions, but for standard businesses, GSTIN is a strict requirement.

**Q2: Is a current bank account mandatory, or can I use a savings account?**
A: For Proprietorships, a savings account might sometimes be accepted, but for all other business types (Partnerships, Companies), a current account in the name of the business is mandatory.

**Q3: What should the Board Resolution for a Private Limited Company contain?**
A: It must clearly state the name of the authorized person (Primary User), their designation, and explicitly authorize them to register and operate the GeM account on behalf of the company.

**Q4: Do I need physical copies of these documents?**
A: No, GeM is a completely paperless portal. You only need digital copies (usually PDF format) or the relevant numbers (like PAN, GSTIN) for API-based validation.

**Q5: How does GeM verify my Udyam certificate?**
A: The GeM portal has API integration with the Udyam portal. You simply enter your Udyam Registration Number and the registered mobile number to fetch and validate the data automatically.
