# GeM Buyer Seller Dispute Resolution Guide

> [!TIP]
> **Quick Answer:** GeM par disputes resolve karne ke liye Incident Management System (IMS) ka use hota hai. Agar buyer or seller aapas mein issue solve nahi kar paate, toh GeM Admin as a mediator intervene karta hai.

## At a Glance Dashboard
| Action | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| Platform | Incident Management System (IMS) |
| Response Time | 48-72 Hours per reply |
| Mediator | GeM Admin / Escalation Matrix |
| Final Recourse | Arbitration as per Indian Law |



## 1. Introduction
B2G (Business to Government) transactions mein disputes aana normal hai—chahe payment delay ho, unfair rejection ho, ya wrong specs. Is guide mein hum dispute ko professionally aur legally handle karna sikhenge.

## 2. Who Should Read This Guide
- Aggrieved Sellers
- Procurement Managers
- Legal Consultants

## 3. Prerequisites
- An active incident or grounds to raise one
- Solid documentary evidence (emails, photos, challans)

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- Signed delivery proofs
- GeM Contract copy
- Clear timeline of events

## 11. Structured FAQs
**Q1: Can I call the buyer directly?**
A: Yes, mutual resolution is always encouraged before raising an incident.

**Q2: What is the role of GeM Admin?**
A: GeM Admin acts as a neutral party to review evidence and enforce platform rules, like suspending the erring party.
