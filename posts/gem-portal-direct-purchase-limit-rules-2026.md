# GeM Portal Direct Purchase Limit Rules & GFR Guidelines 2026

Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par sell karne wale MSMEs aur government departments ke liye **Direct Purchase Limits** aur guidelines ko samajhna bohot zaroori hai. General Financial Rules (GFR 2017) Rule 149 ke latest rules ke tehat, sarkari vibhago ko bina bidding ya tender ke specific thresholds tak direct product kharidne ki permission hoti hai.

Agar aap ek seller hain, toh is direct purchase limit ka faida uthakar aap bina kisi bid me participate kiye lakhon rupaye ke orders paa sakte hain. Lekin iske liye aapko iske exact limits aur catalog conditions ka pata hona chahiye.

---

## ⚡ Quick Answer: Direct Purchase Slabs Kya Hain?
*   **₹50,000 tak:** Buyer bina kisi price comparison ke kisi bhi seller se direct purchase kar sakta hai.
*   **₹50,000 se ₹10,00,000 tak:** L1 Comparison mandatory hai. Buyer ko kam se kam 3 different OEMs/sellers ke products compare karke sabse saste (L1) product ko order dena hoga.
*   **₹10,00,000 se upar:** Bidding ya Reverse Auction mandatory hai (kuch categories me ₹5,00,000 threshold lagu hota hai).

Procurement slabs aur unke rules ko niche di gayi table se samjhein:

| Order Value (Slab) | Procurement Mode | Comparison Required | Key Compliance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Up to ₹50,000** | Direct Purchase | No | Any seller meeting specifications. |
| **₹50,000 to ₹10,00,000** | L1 Direct Purchase | Yes (Min 3 Sellers) | Comparison must be done on GeM portal. |
| **Above ₹10,00,000** | Bidding / RA | Yes (Open Tender) | Mandatory publishing on `gem.gov.in`. |
| **Any Value (with PAC)** | Proprietary Article | No | Requires Proprietary Article Certificate. |

## 🛠️ GeM Direct Purchase Rules for MSEs and Startups

Government procurement policy local suppliers aur small businesses ko badhava dene ke liye specific benefit preferences deti hai:

### 1. MSE Purchase Preference
Bidding ke dauran agar koi MSE seller L1 price ke **L1 + 15% pricing band** ke andar aata hai, toh use contract ka kam se kam **25% order share** match price par allocate kiya ja sakta hai.

### 2. Direct Placement to MSEs
Sarkari vibhag special project guidelines ke under direct orders MSEs aur recognized Startups ko allot kar sakte hain, jo manufacturing quality and delivery standard meet karte hain.

## 💡 Important Rules & Common Mistakes

*   **Order Splitting is Prohibited:** GFR Rule 149 ke under, bade purchase orders ko bypass karne ke liye unhe ₹50,000 ke chote-chote multiple orders me split karna strictly illegal hai.
*   **GeM Transaction Fee:** Har order execution par rules ke mutabik mandatory transactional charges apply hote hain, unhe pricing calculate karte waqt shamil karein.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. GeM portal par direct purchase limit kitni hai?
Sarkari buyers directly ₹50,000 tak ka product bina kisi price comparison ke kharid sakte hain. ₹50,000 se ₹10 Lakh tak L1 comparison mandatory hai.

### 2. Kya ₹10,00,000 se upar direct order mil sakta hai?
Nahi. High value orders ke liye open bidding ya reverse auction mandatory hai, siwaye un cases ke jahan buyer ke paas valid PAC (Proprietary Article Certificate) ho.

### 3. Direct purchase orders me L1 comparison kaise hota hai?
GeM portal automatically buyer ke parameters ke hisab se top 3 products compare karta hai. In 3 me se jo price me sabse sasta (L1) hota hai, order use milta hai.

### 4. Direct purchase orders par transaction fee kitni lagti hai?
Transaction charges GeM threshold policy ke according calculate hote hain.

### 5. Kya resellers ko OEM authorization ke bina direct purchase order mil sakta hai?
Agar product non-branded (generic) hai ya buyer ne OEM authorization compulsory nahi kiya hai, toh bina MAF ke reseller register ho sakte hain.

### 6. GeM L1 price band rules MSEs ke liye kya hain?
Agar L1 price kisi non-MSE vendor ka hai aur koi MSE vendor L1+15% range me hai, toh use L1 price match karne ka option milta hai.

### 7. Kya caution money deposit karna direct purchase ke liye zaroori hai?
Haan. Dashboard active rakhne aur orders process karne ke liye turnover ke slab ke hisab se caution money deposit karna mandatory hota hai.

### 8. Order check karne ke liye kya dashboard par verification notification aati hai?
Haan. Jab bhi koi buyer aapke catalog product par direct purchase checkout generate karega, aapko seller dashboard par notification aayegi.
