Government e-Marketplace (GeM) portal par jab buyer department ko customized goods, complex services, ya multi-item packages buy karne hote hain, tab **Custom Bid** ya **BOQ Bid** issue kiya jata hai.

> 📌 **Answer Capsule**  
> **In one line:** Custom Bids and BOQ tenders allow procurement of non-standard items on GeM where technical compliance is governed by buyer ATC PDFs and BOQ Excel schedules.  
> **What matters:** Do not alter template structure or formulas unless expressly permitted; review buyer ATC documents thoroughly; ensure technical compliance undertakings are signed.  
> **What can change:** Specific BOQ template structures, evaluation rules, and submission validation checks vary by bid.

## 1. Custom Bid vs Standard Product Bid

| Aspect | Standard Product Bid | Custom Item / BOQ Bid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Catalog Listing** | Pre-existing Catalog Required | Direct Bid Participation (No pre-catalog mandatory) |
| **Technical Specs** | Standard GeM Golden Parameters | Buyer Uploaded ATC Specification PDF |
| **Pricing Input** | System Rate Entry | BOQ Excel Schedule / Custom Rate Breakdown |

---

## 2. Essential Guidelines for BOQ Participation

1. **Thorough ATC PDF Review:** Buyer dwara attached Additional Terms & Conditions (ATC) PDF ko dhyan se padhein.
2. **Template Integrity:** BOQ Excel sheet fill karte waqt formulas aur layout formatting retain karein jab tak tender document rules customization allow na karein.
3. **Signed Technical Undertakings:** Technical compliance sheet aur Manufacturer Authorization (MAF) signed format me upload karein.
