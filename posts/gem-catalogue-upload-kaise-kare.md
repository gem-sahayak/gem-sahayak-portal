# How to Upload Catalogue on GeM: The Ultimate 2026 Tutorial

Navigating the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) successfully hinges on one fundamental skill: mastering product listing. If buyers cannot find your products, or if your listings are incomplete, you will miss out on lucrative tenders. This comprehensive guide answers the critical question: **how to upload catalogue on GeM**, providing a step-by-step walkthrough optimized for the 2026 GeM v4 interface and compliant with GFR 2017 standards.

## Understanding GeM Catalogues

A catalogue on GeM is a digital representation of the goods or services you offer. It contains technical specifications, images, pricing, and compliance details. 

> 📖 **GFR Rule Reference:** Under GFR 2017 Rule 149, buyers use GeM to compare products and ensure they are getting the best value. An accurate, detailed catalogue is essential because it forms the basis of technical evaluation in bids and direct purchases. Misleading catalogues can lead to order cancellation and penal action.

### The Golden Rule of Catalogue Uploading

Before starting, remember the golden rule: **Always match the category parameters precisely.** GeM uses a highly structured category system. If your product doesn't fit the existing parameters perfectly, do not force it. You may face rejection or, worse, supply issues later.

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Upload Catalogue on GeM (Goods)

Follow this meticulous process to ensure a smooth, error-free upload.

### Phase 1: Preparation and Search

1.  **Login and Navigate:** Log in to your GeM Seller Dashboard. Go to the `Catalogue` tab -> `Products` -> `Add New`.
2.  **Search the Right Category:** This is the most crucial step. Use the search bar to find the category that exactly matches your product.
    *   *Example:* Do not upload a 'Gaming Laptop' under a generic 'Desktop PC' category. Find the specific 'Notebook Computers' category.
3.  **Review Golden Parameters:** Once a category is selected, GeM displays 'Golden Parameters'. These are non-negotiable specifications (e.g., Processor Type, RAM). Ensure your product meets these before proceeding.

### Phase 2: General Information & Authorization

1.  **Brand Selection:** Choose your brand from the dropdown.
    *   If you are the OEM, your approved brand will appear.
    *   If you are a reseller, select the brand and enter the **Authorization Code** provided by the OEM.
    *   If you don't have a brand or authorization, select 'Unbranded' (Note: This limits visibility).
2.  **Product Name/Model:** Enter the exact model number of your product. Buyers search by model numbers often.

### Phase 3: Technical Specifications

This phase determines if your product passes technical evaluations.

1.  **Fill the Parameters:** You will see a long list of technical parameters (Core, General, and Additional). Fill them out truthfully based on the manufacturer's datasheet.
2.  **Avoid 'Any' or 'NA' Abuse:** Do not select 'Not Applicable' just to bypass a field unless it truly doesn't apply to your product. Buyers filter by these parameters.
3.  **Upload Datasheet:** GeM highly recommends (and often mandates) uploading a PDF datasheet or brochure from the OEM. This document must corroborate the technical specifications you just entered.

### Phase 4: Pricing and Quantity

GeM strictly monitors pricing to ensure government buyers are not overcharged.

| Pricing Field | Description | Important 2026 Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MRP (Maximum Retail Price)** | The highest price the product can be sold for in the open market. | You must have proof of this MRP (packaging photo or OEM declaration). |
| **Offer Price** | The price you are offering to the government. | **Must be at least 10% lower than the MRP.** GeM enforces this discount to ensure value for money. |
| **Current Stock** | The number of units you currently have available for dispatch. | Keep this accurate. Bidding with zero stock can lead to penalties if you win and fail to supply. |
| **Delivery Locations** | The states/pin codes where you can deliver the product. | Only select areas you can reliably service within the stipulated delivery period. |

### Phase 5: Images and Upload

1.  **Image Guidelines:** Upload clear, high-resolution images of the actual product on a white background.
    *   Do not use images with watermarks, promotional text, or contact details.
    *   Upload multiple angles if possible.
2.  **Review and Submit:** Carefully review all details. Once satisfied, click submit.
3.  **Admin Review:** Your catalogue goes into a 'Pending Approval' state. GeM administrators will review it against the rules.

## Common Reasons for Catalogue Rejection

Understanding why catalogues get rejected is a key part of learning **how to upload catalogue on GeM** effectively.

*   **MRP Discrepancy:** The MRP entered does not match the proof provided (like the box image) or is unrealistically inflated.
*   **Parameter Mismatch:** The technical specifications entered in the form contradict the uploaded PDF datasheet.
*   **Poor Images:** Blurry images, stock photos that don't match the product, or images containing watermarks.
*   **Invalid Authorization:** For resellers, using an expired or incorrect OEM authorization code.

> ⚠️ **Critical Warning:** Repeated rejections for providing false information (like fake MRPs or doctored datasheets) can trigger an incident against your account, leading to temporary suspension.

## What if Your Category Doesn't Exist? (Custom Catalogues/BOQ)

Sometimes, you have a highly specialized product that doesn't fit any existing GeM category.

1.  **Custom Catalogue/Universal Category:** For specific bids, buyers might allow uploading under a 'Custom Catalogue' or 'Universal' category. Here, you define the parameters.
2.  **BOQ (Bill of Quantities) Bids:** In BOQ bids, buyers upload an Excel sheet of requirements. You do not need a pre-existing catalogue; you simply upload a compliant product specific to that bid.
3.  **Category Creation Request:** If it's a standard product missing from GeM, you can submit a request to the GeM admin to create a new category, providing necessary market data and standard specifications.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How long does it take for a catalogue to be approved?
Typically, if all information is accurate, approval takes 24 to 72 working hours. Automated approvals exist for highly trusted OEMs in certain categories.

### Can I edit a catalogue after it is published?
Yes, you can edit certain fields like Offer Price and Stock Quantity instantly. However, changing core technical specifications or MRP may require the catalogue to undergo the approval process again.

### What is the 'Clone Catalogue' feature?
If you have a similar product to one you've already uploaded, you can use 'Clone' to duplicate the entry and just change the differing parameters (like color or size), saving significant time.

### Why is my product not showing up in search even after approval?
It takes a few hours for the search index to update after approval. Also, ensure your stock is greater than zero and your delivery locations cover the buyer's area.
