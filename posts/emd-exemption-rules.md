# EMD Exemption Rules on GeM: Complete Hinglish Guide

Indian Public Procurement standard systems me government MSMEs, startups aur domestic units ko support karne ke liye rules formulate karti hai. Is process me sabse badi help hai **EMD Exemption (Bid Security Waiver)**.

Bina security amount deposit kiye bids me participate kaise karein, iski complete guidelines details ke sath Hinglish me samjhein.

---

## EMD Exemption Kya Hai? (Waiver Meaning)

General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017 ke Rule 170 ke target limits ke under, eligible enterprises ko tenders me bid participation fees ya deposit (Earnest Money Deposit) se chhut milti hai. Iska matlab hai ki aap zero deposit ke sath official bids submit kar sakte hain.

---

## EMD Exemption Kaun Claim Kar Sakta Hai?

GeM (Government e Marketplace) portal rules ke mutabik niche diye gaye categories eligible hain:

1. **Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs):** Valid Udyam Registration profile holds. Medium enterprises isme eligible nahi hain.
2. **DPIIT Startups:** Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade dwara recognized units.
3. **NSIC registered units:** Single Point Registration System members.

---

## Exemption Claim Kaise Karein? (Step-by-Step)

* **Step 1:** Bid participate section open karein.
* **Step 2:** EMD submission step par **"Exemption Claimed for EMD"** option par click karke value **"Yes"** select karein.
* **Step 3:** Dropdown se exemption category (MSE/Startup) choose karein.
* **Step 4:** Udyam registration certificate ya DPIIT recognition PDF submit block me attach karein.
* **Step 5:** Sahi format me signed "Bid Securing Declaration" letter upload karein.

---

## Common Rejection Mismatch

Agar tender items technical specs aur aapke Udyam NIC Codes me similarity nahi hai, toh buyer technical round me verification fail kar sakta hai. Isliye bidding se pehle apne registration documents active verify kar lein.

---

## FAQ

### 1. Kya sabhi buyers ko EMD exemption dena zaroori hai?
Haan. GFR guidelines central department standard units me clear follow hoti hain, except custom specific critical safety bids me jahan buyer ne special relaxation restrict ki ho.
