If your technical bid is rejected incorrectly, you have a formal right to appeal. Submitting a clear, document-backed representation is essential to stay in the tender.

## The 48-Hour Representation Window

Once a buyer changes your bid evaluation status to "Rejected":
*   The portal triggers an email and dashboard notification.
*   A countdown timer starts, giving you **exactly 48 hours** to submit your appeal.
*   Once this timer expires, you cannot appeal.

---

## Structure of a Strong Representation

A successful appeal should include:

1.  **Reference Details:** Bid number, category, and date of submission.
2.  **Disqualification Reason:** The exact text of the rejection reason as listed by the buyer.
3.  **Counter-Arguments:** Explain clearly how your uploaded documents satisfy the criteria.
4.  **Evidence Links:** Refer to specific pages of your uploaded technical bid PDF.
