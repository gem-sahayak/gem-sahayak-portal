# Technical Evaluation Process on GeM: Complete Hinglish Guide

Tender lifecycle me **Technical Evaluation** sabse important step hota hai. Is stage par buyer is baat ki verification karta hai ki bidder tender ki specs aur guidelines ko satisfy karta hai ya nahi.

---

## Technical Scrutiny Rules

* **Verification of Credentials:** Bidder ke turnover certificates, past experience records, aur credentials ki scrutiny ki jati hai.
* **Exemption Proof Validation:** Agar bidder ne EMD ya experience turnover waiver claim kiya hai, toh valid certificates (Udyam/DPIIT) validation check block check hota hai.
* **Product Specifications Matching:** Catalog details aur technical parameter checklist metrics cross-verify ki jati hain.
