# GeM Incident Management: Show-Cause Notice Reply Format aur Suspension Appeal Guide

> **Quick Answer:**
> Agar aapko GeM Portal par koi **Show-Cause Notice (SCN)** mila hai, toh bina panic kiye **5 days** (ya specified timeline) ke andar uska factual reply dena hoga. Reply ka format bilkul clear hona chahiye: Notice Reference, Clause-by-Clause explanation, aur solid evidence (invoices, delivery proofs, system issues). Agar aapka account suspension (1 mahine se lekar permanent blacklisting tak) ho gaya hai, toh aap official representation portal ya designated email ke through executive authority ko **Suspension Appeal** submit kar sakte hain. Free templates aur details neeche check karein.

## Introduction

GeM portal (Government e Marketplace) par business karna badhiya opportunity hai, lekin yahan compliance rules bohot strict hain. Jab kisi buyer dwara complaint ki jati hai, ya delivery delay hoti hai, toh GeM ka automatic system ya Incident Management Division (IMD) seller ko **Show-Cause Notice (SCN)** bhejta hai. Agar iska reply time par na diya jaye ya reply un-satisfactory ho, toh seller account temporarily ban ya suspend ho sakta hai. Is guide mein hum detail mein dekhenge ki notice ka response kaise likhein aur suspend hone par appeal kaise karein.

## Prerequisites

- Active login access to the **GeM Seller Account**.
- SCN Reference Number (jo email ya portal notification dashboard mein diya gaya ho).
- Detail information of the contract/purchase order (PO) in dispute.
- Supporting logs like delivery challan, email exchange with the buyer, or technical ticket IDs.

## Step-by-Step Guide with Flow Diagram

Show-Cause Notice ka reply draft karne aur appeal submit karne ke liye is sequence ko follow karein:



### Step 1: Notice ko Dashboard Par Verify Karein
SABSE PEHLE, GeM portal par login karein. Incident Management tab par click karke actual warning status aur "Incident ID" check karein. Email validation par hi rely na karein, portal dashboard hi ultimate authority hai.

### Step 2: Main Reason/Deviation Code ko Samjhein
Pata karein ki incident kis category mein fall karta hai:
- **Delivery Delay (L1):** Logistics delays ya material shortage.
- **Product Quality Discrepancies:** Delivered goods bid specifications se match nahi ho rahe.
- **Unethical Bidding:** Non-compliance or fake documents.

### Step 3: Formal Reply Draft Karein (Company Letterhead)
Draft humesha formal English mein likhein. Neeche diya gaya standardized format use karein:

```text
[COMPANY LETTERHEAD]

Date: DD/MM/YYYY

To,
The Incident Management Division,
Government e Marketplace (GeM),
New Delhi.

Subject: Written Representation / Reply to Show Cause Notice (Incident ID: [Enter Incident ID])

Ref: Contract/PO Number: [Enter Contract No] Dated [Enter Date]

Respected Sir/Madam,

This is with reference to the Show Cause Notice issued via GeM Portal on [Date] regarding the alleged deviation: [Brief Description of Deviation].

We wish to submit the following facts for your kind consideration:
1. Ground of Delay/Dispute: [Detail explanation of reason - e.g., Logistics strike/Site not ready/Force Majeure].
2. Communications with Buyer: We had proactively updated the buyer regarding this issue vide email dated [Date] (Copy attached as Annexure A).
3. Correction Steps Taken: [Explain what you did to solve the problem, e.g., dispatched through alternative courier].

Therefore, we request you to kindly condone this delay/deviation and not initiate any adverse action like suspension/account blocking. We assure you of our best services.

Thanking You,

For [Company Name],
[Authorized Signatory Seal & Signature]
Designation: Owner/Director
```

### Step 4: Documents Upload Karein
Letter ko PDF mein convert karein. Apne saare proofs (Annexures) ko sequence-wise compile karke upload button par press karein. Har document ka file size 5MB se kam hona chahiye.

### Step 5: Follow up and Appeal (If Rejected)
Agar GeM portal se response reject ho jata hai aur account suspend ho jata hai, toh aapko official registration email par appellate process ka link milega. Apni petition draft karke portal support appeal mechanism ke through upload karein.

## Warning Callout Box

> [!WARNING]
> **Important Compliance Alert:** Never ignore any warning or deviation alert on the GeM portal. Auto-suspension algorithm active hota hai. Agar aapne 5 days ke time window ke andar response submit nahi kiya, toh system account ko **automatically suspend** kar dega. Is suspension se bidding transactions lock ho jate hain aur chal rahe orders par payment hold ho sakti hai.

## Comparison Table: Formal Appeal vs Informal Email

| Feature/Parameter | Official Representation (GeM Portal) | Informal Email / Helpdesk Support Ticket |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Official Validity** | 100% legally valid under GeM Incident Management Policy. | Not considered as formal reply to SCN. |
| **System Tracking** | Time-stamped and tracked directly against the Incident ID. | Can get lost or delay in matching with Incident ID. |
| **Automatic Status Block** | Stops the auto-suspension timer. | Does not stop the automatic suspension counter. |
| **Evidence Attachment** | Structured upload section for PDF proofs. | Email attachments size limits; might not be indexed. |
| **Auditable Record** | Visible to both Buyer, Seller, and GeM Admin. | Limited visibility, slow response time. |

## Structured FAQs (15 Detailed Q&As)

### Q1: GeM portal par Show-Cause Notice (SCN) kyun aata hai?
**Answer:** Show-Cause Notice tab aata hai jab buyer koi formal complaint register karta hai (jaise delivery delays, poor product quality, non-supply of items) ya system automatic deviations catch karta hai. GeM administration aapse aupcharik explanation mangti hai ki aap par disciplinary action kyun na liya jaye.

### Q2: Deviation alert aur Show-Cause Notice mein kya farq hai?
**Answer:** Deviation alert ek initial warning notification hai jismein 5 days ke andar action ya correction manga jata hai. Show-Cause Notice (SCN) tab issue hota hai jab deviation warning ka reply nahi milta ya issue zyada bada hota hai (e.g. fake documents upload karna). SCN ek formal legal notice hai jo suspension se pehle ka aakhri mauka hota hai.

### Q3: GeM notice ka reply submit karne ki standard time limit kya hoti hai?
**Answer:** GeM rules ke anusaar, deviation aur SCN notice ka reply dene ke liye aapko notification date se **5 calendar days** milte hain. Time limits strict hain, aur iske baad automatic action initiate hota hai.

### Q4: Agar hum reply submit na karein toh kya hoga?
**Answer:** Agar aap 5 days ke andar reply submit nahi karte hain, toh GeM system automatically action le leta hai. Isme aapki product catalog block hona aur 10 din se lekar 2 saal tak ke liye profile suspension shamil hai.

### Q5: Show-cause reply letter draft karte waqt standard format kya hona chahiye?
**Answer:** Letter humesha formal hona chahiye. Company letterhead par standard reference number, Incident ID, contract details likhein. Paragraph wise ground of defence clear karein aur proof attachments ko reference number (Annexure A, B, etc.) ke saath list karein.

### Q6: Kya seller buyer se compromise karke incident close karwa sakta hai?
**Answer:** Haan. Agar buyer ke saath reconciliation ho jaye aur buyer incident close karne ke liye ready ho jaye, toh unhein portal dashboard par incident withdraw/close option use karna hoga. Phir bhi, record verification ke liye seller ko portal par iska proof submit karna hota hai.

### Q7: "Force Majeure" clause kya hota hai aur isse reply mein kaise use karein?
**Answer:** Force Majeure matlab aisi paristhitiyan jo aapke control se bahar hain (jaise flood, lockdown, local riots). Agar aisi problem thi, toh reply letter mein "Force Majeure" cite karein aur sarkari advisory ya weather department ki warning certificate proof ke roop mein attach karein.

### Q8: GeM portal par account suspend hone ki duration kitni hoti hai?
**Answer:** Suspension severity level ke basis par hota hai. L1 (Minor delays) ke liye warning ya 10-30 days suspension ho sakti hai. Repeated offenses ya severe fraud cases (L3) mein 6 months se lekar **2 saal** tak account suspend ya blacklist ho sakta hai.

### Q9: Account suspension ke dauran kya hum active bids mein participate kar sakte hain?
**Answer:** Nahi. Account suspend hote hi aapki bidding access freeze ho jati hai. Aap nayi bids create ya submit nahi kar sakte aur na hi dynamic pricing/reverse auction mein enter kar sakte hain.

### Q10: Suspension ke baad appeal file karne ki process kya hai?
**Answer:** Suspension order milne ke baad aapko official representation dashboard ya designated GeM Appellate email par **Appeal** file karni hoti hai. Isme aapko solid mitigations batane hote hain ki aage aisi mistakes nahi hongi aur past issue ko puri tarah resolve kiya gaya hai.

### Q11: Appellate Authority ke samne appeal file karne ki time limit kya hoti hai?
**Answer:** Formal Suspension Order release hone ke **15 days** ke andar aapko appeal file karni chahiye. Iske baad appeal evaluate hone mein time lag sakta hai.

### Q12: Kya hum suspension ke dauran ongoing contracts ke orders deliver kar sakte hain?
**Answer:** Haan, general cases mein purane and ongoing orders ko complete karna obligatory hota hai, jab tak ki buyer specifically contract terminate na karde. New bids block hoti hain par existing delivery processing chalti rehti hai.

### Q13: Dispute Resolution Committee (DRC) kya kaam karti hai?
**Answer:** DRC buyer aur seller ke beech physical ya technical dispute ko resolve karne ke liye banayi gayi committee hoti hai. Agar direct consensus nahi banta, toh matter DRC ke administrative review ke liye bheja jata hai.

### Q14: Portal par 'Show Cause Reply' option open nahi ho raha, kya karein?
**Answer:** Aisa tab hota hai jab timeline expire ho gayi ho ya system technical error ho. Turant GeM helpdesk standard mail (helpdesk-gem[at]gov[dot]in) par screenshots aur draft reply send karein aur screen capture attach karein.

### Q15: Suspension cancel hone par kya hamara security deposit wapas mil jata hai?
**Answer:** Haan, agar appeal accept ho jati hai aur account reactivate ho jata hai, aur buyer dwara koi penalty execute nahi ki gayi thi, toh security deposit/EMD block clear ho jata hai.
