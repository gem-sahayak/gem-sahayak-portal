## Navigating the Product Category Tree

The GeM portal organizes items into a structured tree:
1.  **Level 1 Category:** Broad sectors (e.g. Office Equipment).
2.  **Level 2 Category:** Standard groups (e.g. Computers & Peripherals).
3.  **Level 3 Category:** Specific items (e.g. Entry Level Desktop Computers).

---

## Searching by Category

*   Use the **Category** input field on the search dashboard.
*   Start typing your product category name and select the matching dropdown suggestion.
*   This ensures you see all tenders targeting that specific category, regardless of different keyword combinations.
