## The Daily Monitoring Routine

Follow this simple morning routine:

1.  **Run Saved Searches:** Load your pre-configured search queries.
2.  **Review Corrigendums:** Check if any active bids you are preparing have been modified.
3.  **Check Clarification Status:** Track the buyer's responses to your pre-bid queries.
4.  **Update Your Tracker:** Log bid numbers, values, and deadlines in a local spreadsheet.

## Monitoring Post-Submission Bids

After submitting a bid:
*   Check the technical evaluation status regularly.
*   Be ready to draft representations within the **48-hour window** if disqualified.

To learn how to draft these appeals, check our guide on [Writing Technical Representations for Disqualified Bids](/guides/representation-writing).
