Indian public procurement landscape me **GeM (Government e-Marketplace)** aur **CPPP (Central Public Procurement Portal - eProcure.gov.in)** do major procurement platforms hain.

> 📌 **Answer Capsule**  
> **In one line:** GeM is an end-to-end B2G marketplace for products and services, whereas CPPP is the central public e-procurement portal for major works and department tenders.  
> **What matters:** GeM provides direct purchase and bidding workflows under GFR 149; CPPP integrates central/state e-tendering portals; contractors often monitor both to capture full procurement scope.  
> **What can change:** Direct purchase thresholds, DSC compatibility across portals, and regional e-procurement integrations depend on current rules and Certifying Authorities.

## 1. System Ecosystem & Direct Purchase Context

Under GFR Rule 149, government buyers GeM portal dwara items procure karne ke liye guided hote hain as per applicable procurement thresholds.

CPPP (eProcure) nationwide e-tendering infrastructure provide karta hai jahan central ministries, public sector undertakings (PSUs), aur state governments infrastructure projects aur specialized works contracts publish karte hain.

## 2. GeM vs CPPP Comparison Table

| Parameter | GeM (Government e-Marketplace) | CPPP (eProcure.gov.in) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Scope** | Products, Services & Standard Bids | Works Contracts, Infrastructure & Custom Tenders |
| **Procurement Modes** | Direct Purchase, L1 Bidding, Custom Bids | e-Tendering, RFP, RFQ, e-Publishing |
| **Direct Purchase Option** | Yes (As per applicable GFR 149 rules) | No (Pure Tendering Portal) |
| **Target Suppliers** | Product Manufacturers, Resellers, Service Providers | Civil Contractors, EPC Firms, Consultants |
