## Introduction

GeM (Government e Marketplace) portal par business badhane aur orders gain karne ke liye aapki credibility sabse badi assets hai. Buyers jab bhi koi products direct purchase karte hain ya bid evaluate karte hain, toh unhe har seller ka **Seller Rating** aur **Performance Dashboard** check karne ki suvidha milti hai.

GeM portal ek internal scoring model use karta hai jo seller ki delivery history, quality checks, buyer complaints aur operational timelines ko aggregate karta hai. Agar aapka delivery score decrease hota hai ya aapki ratings down hoti hain, toh aapki products search results me neeche chali jayengi aur regular orders milna band ho jayenge. Iske alawa, warning notices ya incident management warnings ko ignore karne se aapko platform se suspension/block bhi mil sakta hai.

Is guide me hum aasan Hinglish me samjhayenge ki aap apne performance indicators ko kaise monitor kar sakte hain aur alert flags aane par kaise action lena chahiye.

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Track Performance Metrics

Apne GeM Dashboard par performance check karne aur dashboard ratings maintain karne ke liye step-by-step process follow karein:

### Step 1: Performance Section Navigate Karein
- **Action**: GeM official home `gem.gov.in` par credentials ke sath login karein. Top navigation menu me dashboard panel select karne ke baad `Performance` section par click karein.
- **Details**: Screen open hote hi aapko live chart aur statistics dikhenge.
- **Expected Outcome**: Yahan rating overview, incident notices count, aur dispatch percentages dynamically load honge.

### Step 2: Rating Statistics Trace Karein
- **Action**: `Rating Details` option select karein.
- **Details**: Aapki composite rating display hogi. Yeh rating 3 major parameters par evaluate hoti hai:
  1. **Delivery Rating**: Kya orders specified timeline ke andar deliver ho rahe hain?
  2. **Product Quality Rating**: Kya buyers ne products specifications ya deviations ke regards feedback diya hai?
  3. **Operational Discipline**: Kya show cause replies aur order acceptances time par ho rahe hain?
- **Pro Tip**: Negative ratings details par click karke direct order ID match karein aur buyer feedback response check karein.



### Step 3: Dispatch Performance Percentage Calculation Verify Karein
- **Action**: `Dispatch Performance` section par hover karein.
- **Details**: GeM calculate karta hai ki aapne active contracts ki delivery date meet ki hai ya nahi.
  - Formula: `(Total On-Time Dispatches / Total Orders) * 100`.
  - Is calculation me dynamic grace days and extension periods include hote hain agar buyer ne approval diya ho.
- **Pro Tip**: Delivery delays se bachne ke liye logistics information update karte hi portal par Courier/LR details manually update karein.

### Step 4: Warning Notices & Incident Alerts Track Karein
- **Action**: Check `Incident Tracker` and `Notices` window.
- **Details**: Agar buyer ne delivery deviation, defective specifications ya late compliance ke liye incident generate kiya hai, toh red alert notifications generate honge.
  - Alert notification detail verify karein (Order ID, Incident Code, and Reason).
  - System clock real-time check karein jo reply time limit show karti hai.

## Understanding Seller Rating Statistics

GeM portal par seller rating ek crucial dynamic parameters hai. Yeh standard parameters par construct hoti hai:

1. **Buyer Feedback Index**: Buyers purchase cycle complete hone ke baad seller performance ko star rating system (1 to 5) par feedback rate karte hain.
2. **Quality Conformance Index**: Agar product parameters testing standards me pass hote hain, toh score green mark hota hai. Reject items count negative flags push karte hain.
3. **Admin Actions Penalties**: System rules violation alerts count rating stats me negative indexing ke sath adjust kiye jate hain.

---

## Dispatch Performance Percentages & SLA

On-time delivery rules strictly monitor kiye jate hain.
- **SLA Timeline**: Contract award hone ke time jo delivery timeframe mention hota hai, use meet karna compulsory hota hai.
- **Calculation Window**: Yeh statistics sliding window model (generally last 3 to 6 months contracts) par evaluate hotey hain.
- **Impact of Delay**: Delay dispatches par buyers Liquidated Damages (LD) charge laga sakte hain. Iske sath hi, dispatch percentage threshold 90% se low jaane par bid submission capabilities block ho sakti hain.

---

## Managing Warning Notices & Incident Alerts

Incident management module automated tracking execute karta hai:
- **Show Cause Alert**: Red warning symbol popup dashboard notification ke roop me appear hoga.
- **Escalation Levels**:
  - *Mild (Level 1)*: Buyer and seller local dispute level (Resolved via Mutual Settlement).
  - *Severe (Level 2)*: System algorithm restrictions implementation.
  - *Critical (Level 3)*: GeM admin authority checks and platform blacklist action.
- **Action Path**: Hamesha proper templates use karke technical reason details ke sath digital response document verify karein aur portal par submit karein.
