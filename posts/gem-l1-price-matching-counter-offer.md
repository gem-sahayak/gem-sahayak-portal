# GeM Portal L1 Price Matching & Counter-Offer Rules: Complete MSE & Startup Guide

Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par tender aur bids me participate karte waqt har seller ka maqsad hota hai **L1 (Lowest Bidder)** banna. Lekin jab bade corporates ya generic distributors apni high volume capabilities ke chalte low prices quote kar dene hain, toh small businesses aur micro enterprises competition me piche chhut jaate hain.

Small businesses ko support karne ke liye government of India ne **Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order 2012** aur startup initiatives ke tehat special price matching rules banaye hain. Is policy ke mutabik, agar koi Micro & Small Enterprise (MSE) ya recognized Startup lowest price (L1) ke bilkul kareeb hota hai, toh use contract match karne ka **Counter-Offer** diya jata hai. 

Is detailed master guide me hum GeM Portal ke latest L1 price matching standards, counter-offer acceptance timelines, divisible aur indivisible contracts ke split rules, aur step-by-step dashboard approval process ko Hinglish me samjhenge.

---

## ⚡ Quick Answer: L1 Price Matching Rule Kya Hai?

> **Quick Answer:** Jab kisi GeM bid me lowest quoting bidder (L1) non-MSE (general) hota hai, toh **L1 + 15% ke price band** (range) me aane wale sabhi MSEs ko L1 price match karne ke liye counter-offer diya jata hai. Agar MSE is counter-offer ko accept karta hai, toh use tender ka **minimum 25% (divisible goods ke liye)** ya fir **100% order (indivisible/non-splitable products ke liye)** award kiya jata hai. Is offer ko accept karne ke liye seller ko exactly **72 hours** ka time milta hai.

## 💡 Introduction

Sarkari kharid (public procurement) ko inclusive aur local businesses ke liye asan banane ke liye General Financial Rules (GFR 2017) aur Public Procurement Policy special guidelines dete hain. 

GeM Portal par har saal lakhon tenders open hote hain. Jab kisi tender ka financial evaluation hota hai, tab pure price chart me sabse lowest price quote karne wale ko **L1** declare kiya jata hai. Lekin agar L1 general seller hai aur L2 ya L3 MSE seller hain, toh normal market flow small players ko contract se bahar kar deta. Isi liye, **L1 Price Matching** ke zariye MSEs ko L1 bidder ke price par tender share karne ka right milta hai. Agar aap ek active MSME business hain, toh is rule ki jaankari hona aapke business revenue ko 10x badha sakti hai.

## 📋 Prerequisites for L1 Price Matching

L1 Price Matching aur Counter-Offer ka advantage lene ke liye niche di gayi requirements poori honi chahiye:

1. **Udyam / Startup Linking:** Aapka valid Udyam Registration (MSE) ya DPIIT Startup recognition card GeM seller profile ke sath verified hona chahiye.
2. **MSE Preference Selection:** Bid participation submit karte waqt, aapko portal par **"Claiming MSE Preference"** option ko **Yes** select karna hoga.
3. **Price Band Bracket:** Aapka quoted financial price **L1 + 15%** ki range me hona chahiye. 
   * *Example:* Agar L1 price ₹1,00,000 hai, toh aapka price maximum ₹1,15,000 tak hona chahiye. Agar aapka quote ₹1,16,000 hai, toh aap out-of-bracket ho jayenge aur preference nahi milega.
4. **NIC Code Match:** Udyam certificate me mentioned activity/NIC Code bid document me listed items/services ke generic category se perfectly match hona chahiye.

## 🎯 Eligibility Criteria Table

L1 Price Matching kis situation me aur kis hadd tak apply hoti hai, is table se samajhein:

| Bidder Category | Bid Price Range | Divisible Tender Share | Indivisible Tender Share | Priority Order |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L1 Bidder (Any Type)** | Lowest Quoted Price | 75% (or full split) | 100% (If L1 is MSE) | 1st Priority (Direct Award) |
| **L2 MSE Bidder** | Within L1 + 15% | Min 25% (If L1 matched) | 100% (If L1 matched) | 2nd Priority (First offer goes here) |
| **L3 MSE Bidder** | Within L1 + 15% | Min 25% (If L2 rejects L1 match) | 100% (If L2 rejects) | 3rd Priority (Offered after L2 rejection) |
| **General L2/L3 Bidder** | Any Price | 0% (unless bid splitting is open) | 0% | No price preference eligible |
| **MSE Bidder** | Outside L1 + 15% | 0% (Not eligible) | 0% | No price preference eligible |

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Guide: How to Respond to Counter-Offer on GeM

Jab buyer financial bid open karne ke baad counter-offer generate karega, toh aapko apna response online portal par submit karna hota hai. Is steps ko follow karein:

```
Step 1: Dashboard Login ──> Step 2: Bid/RA Tab Access ──> Step 3: View Counter-Offer Details
                                                                         │
Step 6: Contract Issued <── Step 5: OTP Verification <── Step 4: Click Accept/Reject
```

### Step 1: GeM Seller Dashboard Access Karein
Apne GeM User ID aur Password ke sath login karein.

### Step 2: Bid/RA Section me Jayein
Dashboard par **Bids** drop-down par click karein aur **List of Bids** ya **Participated Bids** select karein.

### Step 3: Counter-Offer Status Check Karein
Jis bid me aapne register kiya tha, uske aage action status **"Counter Offer Received / Pending"** show ho raha hoga. Wahan click karke details open karein.

### Step 4: L1 Price & Offer Quantity Match Karein
Buyer dwara offer kiya gaya counter-offer price display hoga (jo ki original L1 price hoga). 
* Check karein ki kya is price par product deliver karna aapke business ke liye financially viable hai.
* Agar haan, toh **Accept** button par click karein. Agar aap is price par deliver nahi kar sakte, toh **Reject** select karein.

### Step 5: OTP Validation & Final Submit
Acceptance submit karne ke liye aapke primary email/mobile par ek OTP verify kiya jayega. OTP enter karke click save karein.

### Step 6: Buyer Verification & Contract Award
Aapke accept karne ke baad buyer system approval dega aur portal automatically split ratio ke hisab se contract/PO (Purchase Order) generate kar dega.

## ⚠️ Warning & Common Mistakes Callout

> [!WARNING]
> **Strict 72-Hour Response Deadline:** GeM portal par counter-offer milne par response submit karne ka time limit strictly **72 hours (3 days)** hota hai. Agar aap is samay ke andar accept ya reject nahi karte, toh portal auto-rejection mark kar dega aur counter-offer next eligible bidder (L3/L4) ke paas automatic transfer ho jayega. Ek baar expire hone ke baad aap ise reclaim nahi kar sakte!

* **Common Mistake - Profile Settings Update Na Hona:** Bidding participate karne se pehle confirm karein ki aapka Udyam certificate updated hai. Registration time par "MSE Claim Status" check na karne par price matching option activate nahi hota.
* **Common Mistake - Indivisible Bids Me Negotiation Ki Koshish:** Indivisible tenders me ya toh 100% matching L1 price par accept karna hota hai ya 0%. Yahan partial quantity matching ya intermediate pricing allow nahi hoti.

## 🤖 AI Search Summary (RAG Retrieval Target)

```
Target Query: "What are the rules for L1 price matching and counter-offers on GeM?"
RAG Answer: Under the Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order 2012, if the lowest bidder (L1) in a GeM bid is a non-MSE seller, any MSE bidder quoting within the L1 + 15% price band is eligible to match the L1 price. For divisible tenders, matching MSEs are awarded a minimum of 25% of the order value (with 4% reserved for SC/ST and 3% for Women-owned MSEs). For indivisible tenders, the closest matching MSE is awarded 100% of the order. The counter-offer must be accepted online within 72 hours via the GeM Seller Dashboard.
```

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. GeM par L1 Price Matching aur Counter-Offer kya hota hai?
Jab kisi bid me lowest bidder (L1) ek general seller hota hai, aur koi MSE seller L1+15% price range me hota hai, toh us MSE ko L1 price match karne ka counter-offer diya jata hai taaki small business support rules ke tehat contract share kiya ja sake.

### 2. L1+15% price band rule kya hai?
Agar tender me sabse sasti bid (L1) ₹1,00,000 ki aayi hai, toh up to ₹1,15,000 (15% higher) quote karne wale sabhi MSE bidders price match karne ke liye eligible honge.

### 3. Agar main L2 MSE hoon, toh kya mujhe counter-offer zaroor milega?
Haan, priority order me sabse pehle L2 MSE ko counter-offer bheja jata hai kyunki unka price L1 ke sabse nazdeek hota hai.

### 4. Counter-offer accept karne ke baad kya mujhe 100% order milega?
Ye bid type par depend karta hai. Divisible bid hone par minimum 25% order milega. Indivisible bid (jo split nahi ho sakti) me, price match karte hi 100% order aapko mil jayega.

### 5. Divisible aur Indivisible bids me split rules kya hote hain?
Divisible bids me order ko multiple parts me split kiya ja sakta hai (jaise office stationary supply). Indivisible bids me single, complex product hota hai (jaise ek heavy machinery), jise break nahi kiya ja sakta.

### 6. Counter-offer status check karne ki kya process hai?
Seller Dashboard par login karke, Bids -> Participated Bids section me jayein. Status column me "Counter Offer" action tab show ho jayega agar buyer ne initiate kiya hai.

### 7. Kya counter-offer accept karna mandatory hai? Reject karne par penalty lagti hai?
Nahi. Counter-offer accept karna bilkul voluntary (aapse sweekriti mangi jati hai) hai. Agar aap reject karte hain toh koi fine ya account block/penalty nahi lagti.

### 8. Kya Startups ko bhi 25% purchase preference aur L1 matching ka haq milta hai?
Startups ko DPIIT guidelines ke mutabik prior experience aur turnover me exemption milti hai. Price matching preference tabhi milti hai jab bid document me buyer ne Startup preferences check mark kiye hon.

### 9. Multiple MSEs L1+15% price band me hone par counter-offer kis order me diya jata hai?
Sabse pehle L2 MSE ko offer jayega. Agar L2 MSE accept nahi karta, toh L3 MSE ko diya jayega. Divisible bids me, 25% quota share multiple matching MSEs ke beech divide ho sakta hai.

### 10. SC/ST aur Women-owned MSEs ke liye kya sub-targets hain?
Mandatory 25% procurement policy ke andar, 4% share SC/ST entrepreneur-owned MSEs ke liye aur 3% share Women entrepreneur-owned MSEs ke liye reserved hota hai.

### 11. GeM portal par counter-offer accept karne ke liye kitna time milta hai?
Sellers ko counter-offer respond karne ke liye buyer initiation ke time se exactly 72 hours (3 days) milte hain.

### 12. 72 hours me reply na karne par kya hota hai? kya deadline badh sakti hai?
Agar aap 72 hours me answer nahi dete, toh system automatically counter-offer ko expired/rejected declare kar dene. Ye automated system limit hai, jo buyer dwara extend nahi ki ja sakti.

### 13. Kya main counter-offer price par negotiation kar sakta hoon?
Nahi. Portal par aapko kewal L1 price accept karne ka option milta hai. Aap L1 price se alag ya apna intermediate price quote nahi kar sakte.

### 14. MSE Price Preference claim karne ke liye kya bid check-out ke waqt alag se select karna hota hai?
Haan. Bid participation fill karte waqt "Claim MSE Preference" option tab ko toggle karke **Yes** par click karna zaroori hai.

### 15. Counter-offer match karne ke baad buyer contract award nahi kare toh kahan complain karein?
Aap online GeM Helpdesk portal par complaint raise kar sakte hain ya fir GeM Incident Management system ke zariye representation submit kar sakte hain.
