## The Onboarding Timeline Breakdown

### 1. Basic Registration & Account Creation
*   **Time Needed:** 15 to 30 minutes.
*   **Process:** Selecting organization type, entering Aadhaar details, and verifying mobile OTP.

### 2. Profile Verification & Database Syncing
*   **Time Needed:** 24 to 48 hours.
*   **Process:** The portal verifies PAN credentials and Udyam details.

### 3. Caution Money Deposit & Bank Verification
*   **Time Needed:** 24 to 72 hours.
*   **Process:** Confirming bank account details and caution money deposits.

### 4. QCI Vendor Assessment (Manufacturers Only)
*   **Time Needed:** 7 to 15 business days.
*   **Process:** Arranging documents, factory verification, and final QCI audit checks.

---

## Tips to Avoid Verification Delays

1.  **Check spelling across all documents:** Ensure details on PAN, Udyam, and GST match exactly (including spacing and full stops).
2.  **Verify Bank Accounts:** Ensure your caution money is deposited using net banking from your registered business bank account. Do not pay using third-party accounts.
