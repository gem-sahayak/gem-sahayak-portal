# GeM Product Approval Time: Category Manager Timelines & Standard vs Custom Approvals

Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par tender participate karne ya buyer se direct purchase order pane ke liye aapke products ka catalog active aur approved hona sabse pehli requirement hai. Lekin kai baar naye sellers jab catalogue upload karte hain, toh unhe ye samajh nahi aata ki unka product kab live hoga. 

Kya isme Category Manager (CM) ka manual intervention lagta hai? Standard aur custom items approval timelines me kya difference hai? Is detailed guide me hum GeM Portal ke latest 2026 rules, Category Manager SLAs, aur standard vs custom category approvals ke timelines ko 70/30 Hinglish me step-by-step samjhenge.

---

## ⚡ Quick Answer: GeM Product Approval Timelines Kya Hain?

> **Quick Answer:** GeM portal par product approval ka timeline product type aur category par depend karta hai. **Standard Category Uploads** (jo portal ke taxonomy me pehle se registered hain) general auto-validation aur quick review ke baad **24 se 48 hours (1-2 working days)** me approve ho jate hain. Wahin, **Custom Category / New Item Requests** (jinke liye manual Category Manager evaluation chahiye hota hai) approve hone me **7 se 15 working days** ka samay leti hain.

## 💡 Introduction

GeM Portal par har saal lakhon items upload kiye jaate hain. Lekin General Financial Rules (GFR 2017 Rule 149) ke strict guidelines ke tehat, government buyers sirf wahi catalog order kar sakte hain jo standardized aur verified ho. In catalogs ki accuracy aur specification authenticity ko monitor karne ke liye GeM ne dedicated **Category Managers** ko block-level control diya hai.

Jab koi seller apna product upload karta hai, tab system parameters use check karte hain. Standard items auto-runs aur simple checks se pass ho jate hain, jabki customized requests manual desk review par jati hain. Timelines aur validation criteria ko theek se na samajhne ki wajah se sellers ke bids cancel ho jate hain, kyunki tender opening tak unka catalog live hi nahi ho pata.

## 📋 Prerequisites Before Uploading Catalog

Product approval process start karne se pehle ye criteria ensure kar lein:

1. **Complete GeM Profile:** Caution Money deposited hona chahiye aur profile score green target par ho.
2. **Valid Brand Approval:** Agar branded item bech rahe hain, toh brand request approve ho chuki ho.
3. **Correct HSN Directory Proof:** Exact GST aur HSN codes system database se check kar liye gaye hon.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Guide: Product Approval Process

GeM Portal par product approval pane ke liye 5 simple steps follow karein:

### Step 1: Login & Catalogue Upload
Apne credentials se `gem.gov.in` par login karein. **Catalogue -> Products -> Add New Product** select karein. Product category, brand name aur generic details enter karein.

### Step 2: Specification Matching
System specs template load karein. Auto-parameters ko correct specs sheet se match karein. 

* *Golden Parameters:* Ye compulsory hote hain aur isi par approval directly filter hoti hai.
* *Non-Golden Parameters:* Additional features information ke liye hote hain.

### Step 3: Status Tracking ("Pending for Approval")
Sellers dashboard me status display monitor karein. Jab status *"Pending for Approval"* ho, iska matlab hai catalog review queues me line-up hai.

### Step 4: Category Manager Allocation
Agar product custom request hai ya system parameters map nahi ho rahe, toh category system automatically request ko ek specific **Category Manager (CM)** desk par routing kar deta hai.

### Step 5: Verification & Catalog Live
Parameters successful verify hone par status change hokar *"Active"* ho jayega, jiske baad product listing public search me direct show hone lagegi.

## ⚖️ Key Differences: Standard vs Custom Category Approvals

1. **System Automation:** Standard catalog approvals maximum software logic validation check par process hote hain. Lekin Custom Item Approvals strictly manually design specification criteria checks par evaluate kiye jaate hain.
2. **Document Strictness:** Standard products me sirf normal images aur product features required hote hain. Custom Category Requests me manufacturer test reports (NABL/BIS) attach karna mandatory hai.
3. **Rejection Handling:** Standard rejections local admin level par query raise hone se fast fix hote hain. Custom rejections me Category Manager direct request close kar deta hai aur user ko naye sire se form bharne ki advice milti hai.

---

## ⚠️ Common Reasons for Approval Delays

Sellers aksar unga-punga data upload karte hain jisse process suspend ho jata hai:
* **Mismatched HSN Code:** Tax mapping file agar HSN portal directory se match na kare.
* **Vague Technical Specifications:** Dimensions ya technical ratings clear tarike se sheet me update na hona.
* **Unclear Images:** Product pictures blank, watermarked ya low quality hone se design check verify nahi ho pata.
* **Brand Authorization Slip Fail:** Reseller agar fake MAF ya incomplete authority letter system me upload kare.

---

## 💡 Expert Tips to Fast-Track Approval

* **Upload During Mid-Week:** Tuesday aur Wednesday ko upload karein. Weekend requests pending queue me chali jati hain jisse check delayed ho jata hai.
* **Follow Generic Technical Terms:** Brand name specifications parameters section me na likhein. Ise non-generic criteria bolkar CM reject kar dete hain.
* **Generate System-Friendly PDF:** Aapka technical catalog clean aur searchable text PDF hona chahiye (Scanned image PDF scan hone me time leti hai).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. GeM portal par standard product upload approval me kitna time lagta hai?
Standard categories me upload hone wale branded products general auto-run queries aur category manager review se 24-48 hours (1-2 working days) me approve ho jate hain.

### 2. Custom Category create karne ka normal SLA/timeline kya hai?
Nayi category request ya custom specifications approval me Category Manager level verification ke tehat 7 se 15 working days lagte hain.

### 3. Product approval delay hone ka main reason kya hota hai?
Incomplete specs sheet upload karna, restricted or mismatched HSN/GST percentage validation errors aur OEM authorization (MAF) verification issues sabse common reasons hain.

### 4. Category Manager (CM) kaun hota hai?
Category Manager GeM ke government-appointed technical officials hote hain jo system taxonomy, fair specifications aur vendor validations ko check aur control karte hain.

### 5. Kya resellers ko standard catalog me OEM permission chahiye?
Branded categories me upload ke liye brand code confirmation zaroori hai. Generic categories me resellers bina OEM approval ke invoice dikha kar register ho sakte hain.

### 6. Mera request 15 din se status dashboard me 'Pending' hai, kya karoon?
Agar SLA period exceed ho chuka hai, toh helpdesk panel me ja kar ek official Ticket register karein aur Category ID/Reference Number trace karwayein.

### 7. Kya generic category me product approval instant hota hai?
Nahi, Generic categories me administrative verification required hoti hai taaki spamming na ho, jisme typically 2-3 working days ka samay lagta hai.

### 8. Custom Category request reject hone par ticket kaise close hota hai?
Category Manager comments section me clear rejection reason likhta hai. Wo request automatically auto-close ho jati hai, aur aapko fresh request raise karni padti hai.

### 9. Restricted items (jaise Security Systems) me kitna approval time lagta hai?
In cases me extra verification (Government clearance and special security standard certifications) lagti hai, isliye isme timeline 10-20 working days tak ja sakta hai.

### 10. Kya Category Manager reject karne se pehle clarification maang sakta hai?
Yes. Kuch categories me status "Clarification Required" show hota hai. Seller ko next 3 se 5 working days ke andar online updates submit karne hote hain.

### 11. HSN directory and tax rate change hone par system verification me delay kyu hota hai?
GeM system internal tax registry update karta hai. Agar upload me rate mismatched hai toh database error show hone par catalog automatically queue me freeze ho jata hai.

### 12. Direct purchase catalogue approvals ke rules strict hain ya general?
Direct purchase guidelines are identical. Rules simple aur standard rehte hain jo ki normal catalog parameters ke rules pe run hote hain.

### 13. Kya standard uploads ko fast-track karne ke liye helpdesk call support effective hai?
Call support tabhi effective hai jab request SLA standard time limit se zyada time se pending status me atki ho.

### 14. Restricted GST rates custom category creation time ko badha dete hain?
Haan, kyunki finance teams standard GST slabs mapping review karti hain. Sahi tax document attach karne par verification fast-track ho jata hai.

### 15. Kya automatic tools upload error reduce kar sakte hain?
Yes. SahayakAI tools jaise AI Catalog Dashboard aur HSN Finder tools parameters verification pehle se hi check kar dete hain, jisse human error aur rejection risk minimize ho jata hai.
