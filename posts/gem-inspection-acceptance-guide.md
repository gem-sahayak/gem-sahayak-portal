# GeM Inspection & Acceptance Guide (CRAC Rules)

> [!TIP]
> **Quick Answer:** Consignee ke paas goods receive hone ke baad 10 din ka time hota hai inspection karne ke liye. Agar sab theek hai toh CRAC (Acceptance) generate hota hai, warna reject hota hai.

## At a Glance Dashboard
| Feature | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| Inspection Window | 10 Days post delivery |
| Result Document | CRAC (Acceptance or Rejection) |
| Replacement | Allowed for rejected quantity |
| Third Party Inspection | Applicable for certain high-value bids |



## 1. Introduction
Goods bhejna hi kaafi nahi hai, unka buyer ki expectations aur bid parameters par khara utarna zaroori hai. Is guide mein hum inspection aur acceptance ki complete process discuss karenge.

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- QC Pass Certificate

## 5. Eligibility Criteria Table
| Scenario | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| Goods match specifications | Buyer issues accepted CRAC |
| Goods damaged/wrong | Buyer issues rejected CRAC |

## 11. Structured FAQs
**Q1: How much time does the buyer have to inspect?**
A: 10 days from the generation of PRC.

**Q2: What is Auto-CRAC?**
A: If the buyer does nothing for 10 days, the system automatically accepts the goods.
