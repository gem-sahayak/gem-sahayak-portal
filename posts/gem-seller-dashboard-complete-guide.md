# GeM Seller Dashboard Complete Guide

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) seller dashboard is the mission control center for your public procurement business. With the rollout of GeM v4 and continuous updates in 2026, the dashboard has evolved into a highly sophisticated, data-rich interface. Understanding how to navigate and utilize every feature is essential for maximizing your success on the portal.

In this comprehensive guide, we will break down the GeM Seller Dashboard, explaining every critical tab, metric, and tool available to you.

## Overview of the GeM v4 Dashboard Interface

When you log into your 100% complete GeM account, you are greeted by the main dashboard. It provides a real-time snapshot of your business health on the platform, categorized into distinct actionable zones.

> 📖 **GFR Rule Reference:** The dashboard’s architecture is designed to enforce the transparency and efficiency mandates of GFR 2017 (Rule 149), ensuring all transactions, bids, and communications are logged and trackable.

## Core Modules of the Seller Dashboard

Let's dissect the primary modules you will interact with daily.

### 1. The Summary Ribbon (Top Metrics)
At the very top of your dashboard, you will find vital statistics:
- **Total Orders Value:** The cumulative financial value of all orders received.
- **Active Bids/RA:** The number of ongoing Bids or Reverse Auctions you have participated in.
- **Seller Rating:** A critical score out of 5, based on delivery compliance, quality, and buyer feedback.

> ⚠️ **Critical Warning:** Your Seller Rating directly impacts your visibility. A rating below 3.0 may trigger red flags in buyer evaluation stages and can lead to suspension from high-value bids.

### 2. Catalog Management Module
This is where you build your digital storefront.
- **Add New Offering:** Upload new products or services.
- **Published Catalogs:** View, edit, or update stock levels and prices of currently live items.
- **Pending/Rejected Catalogs:** Track items awaiting admin approval or fix issues highlighted by the GeM quality team.

### 3. Bid / RA Participation Module
Government buyers primarily procure through bidding.
- **List of Bids:** A searchable database of all active tenders relevant to your categories.
- **Participated Bids:** Track the status (Technical Evaluation, Financial Evaluation, Awarded) of bids you have entered.
- **Bid Representation:** If you feel a bid’s technical specifications are unfairly restrictive, you can file a representation here before the bid closes.

### 4. Order Management Module
Once you win a contract, execution happens here.
- **Direct Purchases:** Small value orders (usually under ₹5 Lakhs) that require immediate acceptance.
- **Contracts:** Formal agreements generated post-bidding.
- **Invoice Generation:** GeM requires e-invoicing integrated directly through the dashboard.
- **Delivery Tracking:** Upload Proof of Dispatch and track Consignee Receipt and Acceptance Certificate (CRAC) generation.

### 5. Incident Management
GeM maintains strict discipline through its Incident Management policy.
- **Raised against you:** If a buyer reports delayed delivery or poor quality, an incident is raised. You must respond within the stipulated time frame to avoid account suspension.
- **Raised by you:** If a buyer delays payment beyond the mandated timeline, you can raise an incident to escalate the issue.

## Step-by-Step: Processing an Order on the Dashboard

To illustrate the dashboard's utility, here is the lifecycle of processing a GeM order:

**Step 1: Order Acceptance**
Navigate to `Orders > Pending`. Review the contract terms. Click 'Accept' within the deadline (usually 3-5 days).

**Step 2: Dispatch and E-Way Bill**
Prepare the goods. Navigate to the specific order, enter dispatch details (Transporter name, tracking number), and upload the E-Way bill if applicable.

**Step 3: Generating the Invoice**
Use the `Generate Invoice` button. Ensure the invoice date matches the dispatch date. The system automatically fetches GST details.

**Step 4: Monitoring CRAC**
Track the dashboard to see when the buyer generates the Consignee Receipt and Acceptance Certificate (CRAC). This is the official acknowledgment of delivery.

**Step 5: Payment Tracking**
Once CRAC is generated, monitor the `Payments` tab. Under GeM rules, payment must be processed within 10 days of CRAC generation.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: How do I assign an employee to manage my dashboard?**
*Answer:* You can use the 'Secondary User Registration' feature (found under Account Settings) to create sub-accounts with restricted permissions for your employees.

**Q2: Why is my Seller Rating dropping even though I delivered on time?**
*Answer:* Ratings are also affected by catalog rejections, ignoring direct purchase orders, and buyer-specific subjective feedback. Check the 'Rating Breakdown' for specifics.

**Q3: Can I integrate the GeM dashboard with my ERP system?**
*Answer:* Yes, GeM offers API integrations for high-volume sellers, allowing direct synchronization of inventory and orders with systems like SAP or Tally.

**Q4: Where do I find the EMD return status on the dashboard?**
*Answer:* Navigate to `Bids > EMD/ePBG Status` to track the refund process of your Earnest Money Deposits for completed bids.
