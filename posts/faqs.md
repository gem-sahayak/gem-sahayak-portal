## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is there an official registration fee for GeM?
No. Registration for both buyers and sellers is free of official fees.

### 2. Can sole proprietors register without a separate company PAN?
Yes. Sole proprietorships can register using the personal PAN details of the owner.

### 3. How do MSMEs qualify for bidding fee waivers?
Sellers must link a valid Udyam certificate during registration.

### 4. What is caution money and who must pay it?
Caution money is a mandatory deposit based on turnover. Registered MSEs holding Udyam certificates are exempt.

---

## Best Practices to Avoid Registration Blocks

1.  **Check spelling across all documents:** Ensure details on PAN, Udyam, and GST match exactly (including spacing and full stops).
2.  **Verify Bank Accounts:** Ensure your caution money is deposited using net banking from your registered business bank account. Do not pay using third-party accounts.
