# GeM Payment Cycle Complete Guide: Timeline & Process

> [!TIP]
> **Quick Answer:** GeM par payment ka time Consignee Receipt and Acceptance Certificate (CRAC) par depend karta hai. Item receive hone ke 10 din ke andar CRAC banna chahiye, aur uske baad 10 din ke andar payment process honi chahiye.

## At a Glance Dashboard
| Milestone | Timeline |
| :--- | :--- |
| Delivery to PRC | Instant to 48 hours |
| PRC to CRAC | Maximum 10 Days (Auto-CRAC on day 10) |
| CRAC to Payment | Maximum 10 Days |



## 1. Introduction
GeM platform par payment ko lekar sellers ke man mein kai sawaal hote hain. Naye rules ke mutabik, payment process ko highly automated aur time-bound kar diya gaya hai.

## 2. Who Should Read This Guide
- Sellers awaiting payment
- Accounts & Finance teams
- New GeM Vendors

## 3. Prerequisites
- Dispatched Order
- Correctly updated dispatch details on GeM
- Active Bank Account updated on GeM

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- E-Way Bill / Lorry Receipt
- GST Invoice
- Delivery Challan with consignee stamp/sign

## 5. Eligibility Criteria Table
| Step | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Generate Invoice | Seller |
| Generate PRC | Consignee (Buyer) |
| Generate CRAC | Consignee (Buyer) |

A: Provisional Receipt Certificate, indicating goods have physically reached the consignee.

**Q2: What to do if payment is delayed?**
A: You can escalate via GeM Helpdesk or raise an incident against the buyer.
