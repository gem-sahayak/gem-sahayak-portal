## Why Automate Search Alerts?

Active bids have short participation windows (often only 10 to 14 days). If you check the portal only once a week, you may not have enough time to prepare technical documents and secure EMD bank guarantees.

---

## Setting Up Saved Searches

*   Log into your seller account.
*   Apply your standard category, value, and location filters on the bid search page.
*   Save the query to access it with a single click in the future.
