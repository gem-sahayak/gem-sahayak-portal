# GeM Portal Par Active Bids aur Tenders Kaise Search Karein: Step-by-Step Hindi Guide

Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par daily hazaron naye tenders aur active bids publish hote hain. Lekin small business owners aur MSME sellers ke liye sabse badi problem ye hoti hai ki apne business se relevant tenders ko sahi time par kaise dhoondhein. Galat search patterns ke karan bohot se sellers acche opportunities miss kar dete hain.

Is guide me hum dekhenge ki kaise aap GeM portal par bina kisi help ke active bids dhoondh sakte hain aur unke documents download kar sakte hain.

---

## ⚡ Quick Answer: Active Bids Dhoondhne Ka Sahi Tarika
*   Official **BidPlus sub-portal** ([https://bidplus.gem.gov.in](https://bidplus.gem.gov.in)) par jayein.
*   **Bids** -> **Active Bids** select karein.
*   Apna category keyword ya exact **Bid Number** (e.g. `GEM/2026/B/7712479`) enter karke filter lagayein.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Tender Search Guide

Apni niche category ke active tenders search karne ke liye in steps ko follow karein:

### Step 1: Open BidPlus Sub-Portal
GeM portal sabhi active bids aur documents ko public portal par host karta hai. Bids search karne ke liye login credentials ki zaroorat nahi hai. Directly open karein:
[https://bidplus.gem.gov.in](https://bidplus.gem.gov.in)

### Step 2: Search Box Me Keyword Enter Karein
Main search console par click karke apne product ka naam (e.g. `Office Chair`, `Computer Printing Paper`) enter karein.
*   **Pro Tip:** Bohat lambe sentence likhne se bachein. Sirf generic keywords enter karein.

### Step 3: Exact Bid Number Use Karein
Agar aapko kisi tender ki exact details pata hain, toh directly full bid number format search karein:
*   Format: `GEM/2026/B/XXXXXXX` (Bina spaces ke check karein).

### Step 4: Technical Document Download Karein
Search result card me right-hand side par click karke **"Bid Document"** link par jayein. Isse ek PDF download hogi jisme turnover, experiences, aur mandatory certificates ki guidelines shamil hoti hain.

## 💡 Important Rules & Common Mistakes to Avoid

*   **Spaces around Slashes:** Search bar me `GEM / 2026 / B / ...` jaise spaces add na karein, isse zero results aayenge.
*   **Checking Bid End Dates:** Hamesha bid submission end date aur time check karein. Deadline ke baad portal updates verify nahi karta.
*   **PAC (Proprietary Article Certificate):** Agar kisi bid card par PAC likha hai, toh wo sirf ek particular manufacturer ke liye reserved hai, use skip karein.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Kya GeM portal par active tenders search karne ke liye login zaroori hai?
Nahi. GeM portal ke BidPlus sub-portal par active bids ko public access diya gaya hai. Koi bhi user bina login ke tenders search kar sakta hai.

### 2. Search result me "No bids found" dikhane par kya karein?
Verify karein ki kya spelling sahi hai. Keyword ko small/generic rakhein (jaise `Desktop Computer` ke badle `Computer` type karein).

### 3. Service bids aur Product bids search karne ka tarika alag hai?
Nahi, tarika same hai, par search results me product and services tab separated hote hain, sahi tab choose karein.

### 4. Bid Document PDF download nahi ho raha, kya karein?
Aksar portal maintenance ke waqt server issues hote hain, browser cache clear karke page refresh karein.

### 5. Custom bid search karne ke liye kya keywords dalein?
Custom bids specific department requirements ke liye hote hain, inke name standard se thoda unique ho sakte hain, use specific department filter.

### 6. GeM par bids search aur participation free hai?
Haan. Active bids search karna aur document download karna completely free hai, halanki bidding me participate karte waqt dynamic limits caution money apply ho sakti hain.

### 7. Competitor bids kaise track karein?
"Bid Finalization" tab par click karke final ho chuki bids aur unke technical qualifiers ki list check kar sakte hain.
