## What is the biggest challenge for sellers and MSMEs on the Gem Portal?

Sellers on the **government e-marketplace (Gem India)** often miss high-value active bids because the portal is dynamic and search filters are reset regularly. Checking `gem.gov.in` manually multiple times a day is tedious, time-consuming, and prone to missing deadlines.

Many high-value tenders have short response windows of just 7 to 10 days. If you do not check the portal daily, you risk losing valuable business opportunities to competitors who track these updates in real-time.
