After creating your basic credentials, you must complete your seller profile. The GeM portal uses a profile progress bar that must hit 100% before your account is activated for bidding or catalog listing.

This guide provides the complete checklist to set up your profile.

## Step-by-Step Profile Completion Checklist

To complete your seller profile, log in to your dashboard and verify the following sections:

### 1. PAN & Company Registration Validation
*   Confirm your business PAN is verified.
*   For companies, verify that Director Identification Numbers (DIN) are linked.

### 2. Office Address & GSTIN Details
*   Input your registered office address matching your GST registration.
*   Verify your GSTIN details to enable tax calculations on your orders.

### 3. Bank Account Mapping
*   Enter your business bank account details (IFSC code, bank name, account number).
*   Complete the penny drop verification test to confirm your account is active.

### 4. Caution Money Deposit (CMD)
*   Deposit the caution money amount based on your turnover slab.
*   *Exemption:* MSMEs with valid Udyam certificates can apply for the CMD waiver.

---

## Best Practices for Profile Setup

1.  **Double check spelling across documents:** Ensure details on PAN, Udyam, and GST match exactly (including spacing and full stops).
2.  **Verify Bank Accounts:** Ensure your caution money is deposited using net banking from your registered business bank account. Do not pay using third-party accounts.
