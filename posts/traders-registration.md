Traders, wholesalers, distributors, and dealers register on the Government e-Marketplace as **Resellers**. Resellers sell branded products or generic items manufactured by OEMs to government departments.

This guide explains how traders can register and pair their catalogs.

## The Reseller Onboarding Process

### Step 1: Initial Account Registration
Create your primary seller account on the GeM portal. Select your business type (Proprietorship, Partnership, Company) and verify your profile details.

### Step 2: Catalog Listing Options
As a reseller, you have two options to list products:
*   **Generic Listing:** Sell unbranded goods that do not require brand authorization.
*   **Branded Listing:** Sell products under an OEM brand. You must request a **Brand Link Authorization Code** from the manufacturer to pair with their catalog.

### Step 3: Product Verification Uploads
Ensure your pricing does not exceed the Maximum Retail Price (MRP). Upload proof of purchase, invoices, or OEM authorization letters to verify supply source compliance.

---

## Key Rules for Traders

1.  **Skip Vendor Assessment:** Resellers are exempt from the ₹11,200 QCI vendor assessment required of manufacturers.
2.  **Verify Brand Authorization Codes:** Always coordinate with your OEM partner to receive the brand link authorization code before listing.
