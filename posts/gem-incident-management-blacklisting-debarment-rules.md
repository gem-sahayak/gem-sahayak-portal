# GeM Portal Incident Management Policy: Complete Guide to Show Cause Notices, Watchlist, Debarment & Account Unfreezing (2026 Rules)

Government e-Marketplace (GeM) India ka official public procurement platform hai jahan har saal lakhon crore rupaye ke sarkari tenders aur direct orders process hote hain. Portal ki integrity, transparency aur compliance ko maintain rakhne ke liye Ministry of Commerce & Industry ne **GeM Incident Management Policy (IMP)** ko lagu kiya hai.

Ek GeM seller ke liye Incident Management Policy ko samajhna utna hi zaroori hai jitna bidding rules ko samajhna. Ek chhoti si mistake — jaise CRAC delay, delivery timeline breach, catalog parameter mismatch, ya document verification error — aapke account ko **Watchlist**, **Suspension**, ya **Permanent Debarment (Blacklisting)** mein daal sakti hai. Is master guide mein hum 2026 ke updated Incident Policy Rules, Show Cause Notice replies, legal rebuttals, aur account unfreezing process ko step-by-step detail mein samjhenge.

## ⚡ 1. Executive Summary & Core Definitions

GeM Incident Management Policy (IMP) ek structured, semi-automated compliance mechanism hai jo buyer aur seller dono ke platform behavior ko monitor karta hai.

### Key Definitions for AI & Human Retrieval:
*   **Incident (घटना):** GeM Portal par kisi order, bid, catalog, ya transaction ke dauran rules ka violation, supply delay, rejection, ya fraud alert.
*   **Show Cause Notice - SCN (कारण बताओ नोटिस):** GeM Portal dwara seller ko bheja gaya official notice jisme violation ka reason explain karne ke liye 5–10 din ka time diya jata hai.
*   **Watchlist (वॉचलिस्ट):** Seller profile par temporary restriction lagana jisse naye bids mein participate karna ya direct orders receive karna block ho jata hai.
*   **Debarment / Blacklisting (ब्लैकलिस्टिंग):** Seller ke PAN/GSTIN ko GeM aur baaki sabhi Indian Public Procurement portals (CPPP, IREPS, State Tenders) se 1 se 3 saal tak ke liye ban karna.
*   **Incident Management Committee (IMC):** GeM ki dedicated legal aur technical authority jo disputed incidents aur appeals ko review karti hai.

## ⚖️ 4. GeM Incident Classification & Severity Matrix (2026)

GeM Policy ke tehat incidents ko 3 main severity categories mein divide kiya gaya hai:

| Severity Level | Violation Type | Response Window | Action Taken on Non-Reply | Penalty / Penalty Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1 (Mild Violation)** | Minor catalog mismatch, delivery delay under 10 days, delayed GST invoice upload. | 10 Calendar Days | Warning letter & System Flagging | Watchlist placement for 10–15 days. |
| **Level 2 (Serious Violation)** | Non-delivery of order, delivery delay over 30 days, supply of wrong specification item, refused order accept. | 7 Calendar Days | Account Suspension & Watchlist | Watchlist for 30–60 days + Buyer ePBG deduction. |
| **Level 3 (Severe Violation)** | Fake document submission (GST/PAN/Udyam), fake OEM Authorization, counterfeit goods, fraud, collusion. | 5 Calendar Days | Immediate PAN Freezing & Debarment Initiation | Blacklisting / Debarment for 1 to 3 Years across GFR. |

## 🚫 5. Types of Incidents: Seller vs Buyer Deviations

Incident keval sellers par nahi, buyers par bhi raise kiye ja sakte hain. 

### Common Seller Deviations (विक्रेता उल्लंघन):
1.  **Order Cancellation / Non-Acceptance:** Order aane ke baad 10 din ke andar accept na karna ya cancel kar dena.
2.  **Delayed Delivery:** Direct Purchase ya Bid mein committed delivery date ke baad goods supply karna.
3.  **Specification Mismatch:** Catalog mein kuch aur mention karna aur actual delivery mein inferior item bhejna.
4.  **Fake OEM Authorization:** Fraudulent Manufacturer Authorization Form (MAF) upload karke bid win karna.
5.  **Direct Purchase Misuse:** Prices ko artificial level par manipulate karke GFR limits breach karna.

### Common Buyer Deviations (खरीदार उल्लंघन):
1.  **Delayed CRAC Generation:** Consignee Receipt Certificate 10 din ke andar generate na karna.
2.  **Delayed Payment Release:** CRAC generate hone ke 10 din baad bhi payment hold par rakhna.
3.  **Unjustified Rejection:** Inspection without valid lab test report ke goods reject kar dena.
4.  **Restrictive Bidding Conditions:** Specific brand ko favor karne ke liye restrictive specs add karna.

## 🔄 6. Step-by-Step Incident Escalation Lifecycle

GeM Portal par incident ka lifecycle 5 key stages se hokar guzarta hai:

```
[Stage 1: Incident Raised] 
          ↓
[Stage 2: Auto-System Notice (SCN Issued)]
          ↓
[Stage 3: Seller/Buyer Response Window (5-10 Days)]
          ↓
[Stage 4: Evaluation by GeM / IMC Committee]
          ↓
[Stage 5: Final Decision (Closed / Warning / Watchlist / Debarment)]
```

### Stage 1: Incident Creation
Buyer ya System GeM portal ke "Incident Management System" tab par jakar specific Order ID ya Bid Number ke against incident log karta hai.

### Stage 2: SCN Notification
Seller ke registered email, mobile SMS, aur GeM Dashboard Inbox par automated **Show Cause Notice** deliver hota hai.

### Stage 3: Defense Submission
Seller ko mandatory evidence (Invoice, Dispatch Receipts, Courier Proof, Email communications) ke saath official response submit karna hota hai.

### Stage 4: Review & Moderation
GeM Portal Officer ya **Incident Management Committee (IMC)** dono sides ke proof review karti hai.

### Stage 5: Final Action & Closing
Agar seller ka proof valid milta hai, incident **Closed** mark kar diya jata hai. Failure hone par severity ke basis par Watchlist ya Debarment penalty active hoti hai.

## 📝 7. Show Cause Notice (SCN) Drafting Framework

Show Cause Notice ka reply dete waqt emotional language ka nahi, balki legal aur factual language ka upayog karna zaroori hai.

### SCN Reply Format Template:

```text
To,
The Incident Management Officer / Competent Authority,
Government e-Marketplace (GeM), New Delhi.

Subject: Official Representation against Incident ID: [INCIDENT_NUMBER] for Order No: [ORDER_NUMBER]

Respected Sir/Madam,

We are in receipt of the Show Cause Notice dated [DATE] regarding alleged deviation in Order No: [ORDER_NUMBER]. We respectfully submit our point-wise clarification as under:

1. Factual Background:
   Order No [ORDER_NUMBER] was placed on [ORDER_DATE] for supply of [ITEM_NAME]. The stipulated delivery date was [DELIVERY_DATE].

2. Cause of Delay / Clarification:
   The delay of [NUMBER] days occurred due to [REASON - e.g., Buyer site non-readiness / Transporter Strike / Natural Calamity], which was promptly communicated to the Buyer via GeM Messenger on [DATE].

3. Documentary Evidence:
   We attach the following verifiable proofs:
   - Annexure A: Proof of Dispatch (LR Copy / Courier Receipt)
   - Annexure B: GeM Chat Communication Screenshots
   - Annexure C: Transporter Tracking History

4. Prayer / Relief Sought:
   In light of the above facts, it is evident that there was no intentional breach or willful default on our part. We request your good office to kindly drop the incident and close the case without any adverse entry on our profile.

Thanking You,
Yours Faithfully,
For [COMPANY_NAME]
Authorized Signatory (PAN: [COMPANY_PAN])
```

## 🔒 8. Watchlist, Account Freezing & Penalties Explained

### Watchlist Penalties:
*   **10 Days Watchlist:** First-time mild delivery delay or minor catalog deviation.
*   **30 Days Watchlist:** Repeat delivery delay or order rejection after acceptance.
*   **60 Days Watchlist:** Refusal to supply order after winning a bid.

### Impact of Watchlist on Seller Business:
1.  **No New Bidding:** Aap naye tenders mein participate nahi kar sakte.
2.  **No Direct Orders:** Direct Purchase catalog search se aapki listings hidden ho jaati hain.
3.  **Ongoing Orders Unaffected:** Jo orders pehle se accept ho chuke hain, unhe aap normal tareeqe se fulfill kar sakte hain.

## ⛔ 9. Debarment & Blacklisting under GFR Rule 151 / 175

Central Government General Financial Rules (GFR 2017) Rule 151 ke tehat sarkari vibhag kisi seller ko 3 saal tak ke liye sabhi public procurement se debar kar sakte hain.

### Mandatory Grounds for Blacklisting:
1.  **Fake / Forged Documents:** Fake GST Certificate, fake ISO, fake Turnover Certificates, ya forged OEM Authorization upload karna.
2.  **Corrupt / Fraudulent Practices:** Collusive bidding, cartel formation, ya fake pricing structure.
3.  **National Security Breach:** Telecom ya Defence equipment mein restricted country details hide karna (Public Procurement Land Border Order compliance breach).
4.  **Repeat Severe Violations:** Ek hi saal mein 3 se zyaada Severe Incidents mein guilty paya jaana.

## 💰 10. Financial Impact: EMD / ePBG Forfeiture Rules

Jab koi Severe ya Serious Incident validate hota hai:

1.  **Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) Forfeiture:** Bid participate karte waqt jama ki gayi EMD ko Ministry account mein confiscate kar liya jata hai.
2.  **Electronic Performance Bank Guarantee (ePBG) Forfeiture:** Order deliver na hone par ya premature contract termination par buyer department bank ko instruction dekar ePBG encash kara leta hai.
3.  **Recovery from Outstanding Bills:** Agar seller ke purane payments pending hain, toh buyer department penalty amount purane bills se deduct kar sakta hai.

## 🏢 11. Real-World Case Studies & Practical Scenarios

### Case Study 1: The Delayed Delivery Dispute
*   **Scenario:** Seller A ko 20 Laptops Supply ka order mila. Delivery timeline 15 din thi. Local logistics issue ki wajah se delivery 22nd day par hui. Buyer ne Incident raise kar diya.
*   **Action Taken:** Seller ne Proof of Dispatch (LR copy shows item dispatched on Day 12) aur Transporter Delay letter submit kiya.
*   **Outcome:** IMC ne delivery delay ko "Unintentional Logistics Delay" maana. Minor warning ke saath incident close ho gaya. No Watchlist applied.

### Case Study 2: Fake OEM Certificate Upload
*   **Scenario:** Seller B ne local market se Printer khareed kar bid jeetne ke liye fake OEM Authorization Certificate banakar upload kiya. OEM ne GeM Portal par authenticity dispute raised kar diya.
*   **Action Taken:** GeM Incident Committee ne verification ke liye OEM contact kiya. MAF fake nikla.
*   **Outcome:** Seller B ka PAN 2 saal ke liye **Blacklist / Debarred** kar diya gaya aur ePBG forfeit ho gaya.

## 🛡️ 12. How to Handle Fake or Arbitrary Buyer Incidents

Kabhi-kabhi buyers budget finish hone par ya internal delay hide karne ke liye seller par galat incident raise kar dete hain.

### Defense Strategy:
1.  **Communication Trail:** GeM Portal par hamesha GeM Chat / Messenger ke dwara hi conversation karein. External WhatsApp/Calls court ya IMC mein valid proof nahi maane jaate.
2.  **Raise Counter-Incident:** Agar Buyer CRAC delay kar raha hai, toh GeM Seller Dashboard se buyer ke against **"CRAC Not Generated Within 10 Days"** ka counter-incident raise karein.
3.  **Formal Representation:** Ministry Nodal Officer ko official letter likhein aur proof attach karein.

## 📋 16. Compliance Checklist for GeM Sellers

Before accepting any order or submitting a bid, verify this 10-point checklist:

- [ ] Product Catalog Specs match 100% with physical inventory.
- [ ] OEM Authorization Letter (MAF) is authentic and verified with OEM.
- [ ] Committed delivery timeline accounts for buffer logistics delay (+5 days).
- [ ] GST / Udyam credentials on GeM profile exactly match tax documents.
- [ ] Price quoted is inclusive of all freight, GST, and unloading charges.
- [ ] GeM Chat Messenger is used for all delivery date extension requests.
- [ ] Proof of Delivery (POD) / LR Copy is scanned and stored for 3 years.
- [ ] Inspection report copy is attached before dispatch (for L1 tenders).
- [ ] SCN Email alerts are active on registered Mobile & Email.
- [ ] SahayakAI Extension is installed for real-time bid & incident tracking.

## ⚠️ 17. Top 10 Common Mistakes Sellers Make

1.  **Ignoring SCN Notifications:** Emails ko junk/spam folder mein miss kar dena aur 10-day window expire hone dena.
2.  **Off-Record Agreements:** Phone par buyer se delivery extend karne ki baat karna bina GeM Portal par update kiye.
3.  **Uploading Generic Rebuttals:** Formal evidence ke bina bas "Sorry, we will deliver soon" likhkar reply dena.
4.  **Fake Tracking Numbers:** Logistics delay hide karne ke liye galat courier tracking number enter karna.
5.  **Cancelling Order After Winning:** Market price rise hone par order accept karne se refuse kar dena.
6.  **Using Invalid Brand Names:** Generic products par reputed brand names set kar dena.
7.  **Over-Promising Delivery Time:** 2 din mein delivery ka claim karna jab actual supply 15 din leti ho.
8.  **Not Raising Counter-Incidents:** Buyer ke payment/CRAC delay par chup rehna.
9.  **Submitting Unnotarized Deeds:** Partnership ya JV documents bina notary/registration ke upload karna.
10. **Re-Registering with New GST:** Blacklist hone par same PAN se doosri firm banakar register karne ki koshish karna (Auto-flagged by AI system).

## ❓ 20. Frequently Asked Questions (50 Comprehensive FAQs)

### Q1: GeM Portal Incident Management Policy kya hai?
**Answer:** GeM Incident Management Policy (IMP) ek legal aur administrative rulebook hai jo GeM portal par buyers aur sellers ke violations, order delays, product defects, aur fraudulent activities par actions aur penalties prescribe karti hai.

### Q2: Show Cause Notice (SCN) kya hota hai?
**Answer:** SCN ek formal notice hota hai jo GeM System dwara seller ko bhej kar pucha jata hai ki unke khilaf violation ke liye disciplinary action kyu na liya jaye.

### Q3: SCN ka reply kitne dino mein dena mandatory hai?
**Answer:** Severity ke hisaab se 5 se 10 calendar days ke andar reply dena mandatory hota hai.

### Q4: Agar main SCN ka reply na doon toh kya hoga?
**Answer:** Reply na dene par GeM system ise "Implied Admission of Guilt" maanta hai aur automatically Watchlist ya Account Suspension penalty apply kar deta hai.

### Q5: Watchlist status kya hota hai?
**Answer:** Watchlist ek temporary penalty state hai jisme seller 10 se 60 dino tak kisi bhi naye bid mein participate nahi kar sakta.

### Q6: Kya Watchlist period mein main purane order fulfill kar sakta hoon?
**Answer:** Haan, Watchlist period mein pehle se accept kiye gaye orders ko fulfill karne par koi restriction nahi hoti.

### Q7: GeM Account Suspension aur Watchlist mein kya farak hai?
**Answer:** Watchlist mein profile visibility aur bidding restricted hoti hai, jabki Suspension mein seller portal par koi action nahi le sakta aur critical breaches par investigation hoti hai.

### Q8: Debarment / Blacklisting kya hota hai?
**Answer:** Debarment ka matlab hai seller ke PAN card aur firm ko GeM aur sabhi Public Procurement portals se 1 se 3 saal ke liye ban kar dena.

### Q9: GFR Rule 151 kya hai?
**Answer:** General Financial Rules 2017 ka Rule 151 government departments ko power deta hai ki wo fake documents, corruption, ya contract breach karne wale sellers ko debar kar sakein.

### Q10: Blacklisting ka effect CPPP portal par bhi padega?
**Answer:** Haan, GFR Rule 151 ke under hone wala debarment CPPP (eProcure), Railways (IREPS), aur State Tenders sabhi par lagu hota hai.

### Q11: Kya buyer galat incident raise kar sakta hai?
**Answer:** Unfortunate cases mein buyer arbitrary incident raise kar sakta hai, lekin seller evidentiary proof (POD, chats, invoices) dekar ise IMC se reject karva sakta hai.

### Q12: Fake buyer incident ko cancel kaise karayein?
**Answer:** SCN reply mein factual documentation (Dispatch Proof, Chat history, Dispatch Invoice) attach karke GeM Incident Committee ko escalation request bhejein.

### Q13: Delivery delay par kitni penalty lagti hai?
**Answer:** Delayed delivery par Liquidated Damages (LD) 0.5% per week (up to 10% maximum) lagta hai, aur incident severity ke basis par 10-30 days watchlist ho sakti hai.

### Q14: Order Accept na karne par kya penalty hai?
**Answer:** Bid jeetne ya Direct order aane ke baad refuse karne par Seller Profile ko 30 se 60 days ke liye Watchlist mein daal diya jata hai aur EMD forfeit ho sakta hai.

### Q15: Fake OEM Authorization Form (MAF) upload karne par kya hoga?
**Answer:** Fraudulent MAF upload karna Level 3 Severe Violation hai. Isme immediate account freeze aur 1 se 3 saal ka debarment hota hai.

### Q16: CRAC delay ke liye buyer par incident kaise lagayein?
**Answer:** Seller Dashboard ➔ Orders ➔ Select Order ➔ Incident Management ➔ "CRAC Delay Beyond 10 Days" select karke incident file karein.

### Q17: Buyer Payment Release nahi kar raha, kya incident raise kar sakte hain?
**Answer:** Haan, CRAC generate hone ke 10 din baad bhi payment na milne par seller GeM Portal par Buyer Payment Delay incident log kar sakta hai.

### Q18: Incident Management Committee (IMC) kaun hai?
**Answer:** IMC GeM Portal ki decision-making body hai jisme Senior Government Officers aur Technical Experts shaamil hote hain jo disputes aur appeals ko resolve karte hain.

### Q19: Appeal kitne din ke andar submit karni hoti hai?
**Answer:** Disciplinary order pass hone ke 15 calendar days ke andar IMC Appellate Authority ko appeal submit karni hoti hai.

### Q20: Appeal submit karne ke baad kitne din mein decision aata hai?
**Answer:** IMC Appeal decision aam taur par 30 working days ke andar process karti hai.

### Q21: Kya main phone communication ko proof ke roop mein de sakta hoon?
**Answer:** Nahi, GeM Policy ke tehat sirf GeM Messenger chats, official emails, aur signed physical documents hi valid legal proof mane jate hain.

### Q22: Product specification mismatch incident kya hai?
**Answer:** Catalog mein high-grade spec mention karke delivery mein inferior grade product supply karne par buyer Level 2 Incident raise kar sakta hai.

### Q23: ePBG Forfeiture kab hota hai?
**Answer:** Serious delivery failure, contract cancellation, ya severe audit breach hone par buyer department ePBG bank guarantee encash kara sakta hai.

### Q24: EMD Forfeiture se kaise bachein?
**Answer:** Bid conditions ko dhyan se padhein, eligibility parameters verify karein, aur bina capacity ke bid mein quote na karein.

### Q25: GST Verification Failure par incident lagta hai kya?
**Answer:** GST status inactive hone par system automatically profile flag kar deta hai aur seller ko GST restore karne ka 15-day notice deta hai.

### Q26: Kya Watchlist period khatam hone par account auto-unfreeze ho jata hai?
**Answer:** Haan, 10/30/60 days ka period complete hone ke baad system automatically watchlist restriction remove kar deta hai.

### Q27: Unfreeze hone ke baad rating par kya effect padta hai?
**Answer:** Incident validate hone par Seller Reliability Rating drop hoti hai, jise naye successful orders timely deliver karke improve kiya ja sakta hai.

### Q28: Brand Name misuse karne par kya action hota hai?
**Answer:** Unbranded items par trademarked brand names write karne par Brand Owner infringement Incident raise kar sakta hai, jisse catalog delete aur account suspend ho sakta hai.

### Q29: Catalog upload Error incident se alag hai kya?
**Answer:** Haan, Catalog upload errors technical validation issues hote hain, jabki Incidents contractual ya policy violations hote hain.

### Q30: SCN ka response SahayakAI tool se generate kar sakte hain?
**Answer:** Haan, [SahayakAI Rejection & Incident Reply Generator](/tools) se aap formal legal aur evidence-backed response formats Instant generate kar sakte hain.

### Q31: Joint Venture (JV) bidding mein incident aane par kiski responsibility hoti hai?
**Answer:** JV/Consortium ke case mein Lead Partner aur Member Partners dono par Joint and Several Liability lagu hoti hai.

### Q32: Restrictive Buyer Specification par incident kaise lagayein?
**Answer:** Pre-bid stage par "Custom Specification Favoring Single Brand" ka incident raise kiya ja sakta hai.

### Q33: Delivery period extension request kab deni chahiye?
**Answer:** Delivery date expire hone se kam se kam 5 din pehle GeM Dashboard se Delivery Period Extension apply karna chahiye.

### Q34: What is Liquidated Damages (LD) on GeM?
**Answer:** LD delayed delivery ka financial compensation hai jo 0.5% per week of delayed goods value calculate hota hai.

### Q35: Force Majeure clause Incident mein kaise help karta hai?
**Answer:** Natural disasters, floods, war, ya government lock-down ke case mein Force Majeure proof attach karne par delivery delay incident waive off ho jata hai.

### Q36: Transporter strike kya valid excuse hai?
**Answer:** Haan, agar Registered Transporter Union Certificate aur Strike Notification copy submit ki jaye toh IMC delay penalty exempt kar sakti hai.

### Q37: Product Inspection test report fail hone par kya karein?
**Answer:** Inspection report mismatch hone par NABL accredited government lab se re-testing request submit karein.

### Q38: SCN mein Annexure attach karne ka sahi format kya hai?
**Answer:** Sabhi documents ko single OCR-readable PDF file mein combine karein aur line-wise annexure indexing karein.

### Q39: Kya GeM Incident Status Publicly visible hota hai?
**Answer:** Rating badges visible hote hain, lekin exact incident details only Buyer, Seller, aur GeM Officers ke paas visible hote hain.

### Q40: Blacklist seller naya GeM Account bana sakta hai?
**Answer:** Nahi, GeM System PAN, Aadhaar, GSTIN, aur Bank Account Number cross-verify karke duplicate registrations ko block kar deta hai.

### Q41: Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs) ko Incident Rules mein koi relaxation milta hai?
**Answer:** Procedural delays mein MSMEs ko 1 extra extension request allowed hoti hai, lekin Fraud ya Fake Document violations mein ZERO tolerance hoti hai.

### Q42: Incident Management System ka official URL kya hai?
**Answer:** Direct portal section: `https://gem.gov.in/landing/index/incidents_policy`.

### Q43: Representation aur Appeal mein kya difference hai?
**Answer:** Representation SCN ka initial reply hota hai; Appeal Incident Officer ke adverse decision ke baad Higher Authority (IMC) ko di jati hai.

### Q44: Wrong Order Value Bidding par incident lag sakta hai?
**Answer:** Systemic pricing error (e.g. ₹10,000 ki jagah ₹10 quote kar dena) par order reject karne par Level 1 Incident consider hota hai.

### Q45: OEM Assessment Fail hone par GeM Account par kya effect padega?
**Answer:** OEM Panel Status reject ho jata hai aur vendor category downgrade ho sakti hai, lekin direct selling account suspend nahi hota.

### Q46: Buyer Inspection Officer bribe maange toh kya karein?
**Answer:** GeM Vigilance Cell aur Anti-Corruption Bureau ko evidence ke saath complaint submit karein.

### Q47: GeM Account Watchlist hone par payout rukta hai kya?
**Answer:** Nahi, pehle se generate ho chuke CRAC invoices ki payment buyer ko 10 din mein karni hi padegi.

### Q48: Product cloning violation incident kya hota hai?
**Answer:** Kisi doosre seller ki exact proprietary product image aur specs copy-paste karke catalog publish karna.

### Q49: SCN ka response mail dwara bhejna valid hai?
**Answer:** Nahi, response ONLY GeM Dashboard ke Incident Portal Section ke through submit karna mandatory hai.

### Q50: SahayakAI extension Incident Management mein kaise help karta hai?
**Answer:** SahayakAI Extension aapke GeM Dashboard ko real-time monitor karta hai aur SCN notice, CRAC deadlines, aur risk alerts WhatsApp par Instant bhejta hai.
