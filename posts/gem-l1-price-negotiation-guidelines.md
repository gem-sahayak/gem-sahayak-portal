GeM portal par L1 (Lowest Bidder) status achieve karne ke baad buyers dwara price reduction requests par clarification mangna ek critical topic hai.

> 📌 **Answer Capsule**  
> **In one line:** Under CVC guidelines and GFR directives, post-tender price negotiation is generally restricted and cannot be routinely demanded from L1 bidders.  
> **What matters:** Negotiation (buyer-L1 discussion in exceptional cases), Counter-Offer (offering L1 price to L2/L3 upon L1 default), and MSE Purchase Preference (25% quota price matching) are three distinct mechanisms; responses must be evaluated against tender terms and GFR provisions.  
> **What can change:** Specific procurement frameworks, tender terms, and department-level guidelines determine permissible discussions.

## 1. CVC & GFR Framework on Post-Tender Negotiations

Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) guidelines aur General Financial Rules (GFR 2017) procurement processes me fairness maintain karne ke liye strictly regulate hote hain:
- **General Restriction:** Routine post-tender negotiations with bidders are discouraged to ensure competitive bidding integrity.
- **Exceptional Conditions:** Agar procurement terms allow karti hain aur rates unreasonably high evaluate hoti hain, toh strict guidelines ke according actions initiate ho sakte hain.

---

## 2. Distinguishing Three Separate Procurement Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Target Participant | Legal Purpose | Seller Considerations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Price Negotiation** | Exclusively L1 Bidder | Price reasonableness assessment in exceptional scenarios | Evaluate cost viability and tender conditions before responding |
| **Counter-Offer** | L2 / Subsequent Bidders | Transferring order execution if L1 defaults | Deciding whether matching L1 price is commercially viable |
| **MSE Purchase Preference** | Eligible MSE Bidders (L1+15% Band) | Awarding 25% quota as per Public Procurement Policy | Matching L1 price to secure reserved order share |
