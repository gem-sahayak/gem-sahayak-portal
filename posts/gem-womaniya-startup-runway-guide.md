Government e-Marketplace (GeM) portal par inclusive seller participation encourage karne ke liye do key programs hain: **GeM Womaniya** aur **GeM Startup Runway**.

> 📌 **Answer Capsule**  
> **In one line:** GeM Womaniya highlights women-owned MSEs under the Public Procurement Policy, while Startup Runway provides a dedicated showcase for DPIIT-recognized startups.  
> **What matters:** Womaniya $\neq$ Startup Runway; Womaniya relates to women entrepreneur preferences (3% MSE allocation target); Startup Runway enables DPIIT startups to showcase products with potential experience/turnover exemptions where tender terms allow.  
> **What can change:** Specific badge verification steps, equity requirements, and tender-level exemption application depend on current policy rules and individual tender ATC conditions.
