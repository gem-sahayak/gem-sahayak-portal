## Introduction

Bharat sarkar har saal lakhon karod rupaye ke goods aur services ki khareedari **Government e Marketplace (GeM)** portal ke zariye karti hai. Agar aap ek MSME, startup, manufacturer, ya trader hain, toh GeM portal par register hona aapke business ke liye sarkari orders pane ka sabse bada zariya hai.

Bahut se naye sellers ko lagta hai ki GeM registration bahut mushkil ya kharchila kaam hai. Lekin sach ye hai ki GeM portal par registration **100% Free** hai aur ise aap ghar baithe 20 minute me poora kar sakte hain.

Is comprehensive guide me hum aapko **gem registration kaise kare** ki poori prakriya simple Hinglish me samjhayenge, taaki aap bina kisi agent ya fees ke apna seller account khud setup kar sakein.

---

## GeM Registration Shuru Karne Se Pehle Ki Tayyari

Registration form bharne se pehle niche diye gaye prerequisites tayyar rakhein. Isse aapka session timeout nahi hoga aur registration ek baar me poora ho jayega.

- **Aadhaar Card**: Mobile number se linked hona zaroori hai (OTP verification ke liye).
- **PAN Card**: Business ka ya Proprietor ka Active PAN.
- **Udyam Registration**: MSME benefits aur EMD exemption lene ke liye.
- **GSTIN**: Agar aapke business par GST lagu hota hai.
- **Bank Account**: Active savings ya current account ki details (IFSC & Account Number).
- **Email ID & Mobile**: Official business email jo active ho.

## Step-by-Step Solution: GeM Registration Kaise Kare

<div class="step-card">
  <h3>Step 1: Official Portal Par Jayein Aur Registration Option Chunein</h3>
  <p>Sabse pehle apne browser me GeM portal open karein. Top right corner me "Register" button par click karein aur <strong>"Seller"</strong> option ko chunein.</p>
</div>

<div class="step-card">
  <h3>Step 2: Terms and Conditions Accept Karein</h3>
  <p>GeM Portal ke Terms and Conditions (GTC) ka page khulega. Inhein dhyan se padhein, checkbox ko tick karein aur "Proceed" par click karein.</p>
</div>

<div class="step-card">
  <h3>Step 3: Organization Details Aur Business Type Chunein</h3>
  <p>Apne business ka constitution select karein:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Proprietorship</li>
    <li>Partnership</li>
    <li>Company (Private Limited / Public Limited)</li>
    <li>Trust / Society / Firm</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Apni firm ka official naam enter karein jo PAN aur GST record me darj hai.</p>
</div>

<div class="step-card">
  <h3>Step 4: Personal Identity Verification (Aadhaar / PAN)</h3>
  <p>Primary User (Authorized Person) ka Aadhaar Number aur Aadhaar-linked mobile number daalein. Send OTP par click karke received OTP enter karein. Uske baad PAN details verify karein.</p>
</div>

<div class="step-card">
  <h3>Step 5: User ID Aur Password Create Karein</h3>
  <p>Ek unique User ID aur strong Password set karein. User ID me kam se kam 6 characters hone chahiye. Credentials ko surakshit jagah note kar lein.</p>
</div>

<div class="step-card">
  <h3>Step 6: Email Verification Poora Karein</h3>
  <p>Apna primary business Email Address enter karein. Aapke email par ek verification link aur OTP aayega. Link par click karke email verify karein.</p>
</div>

<div class="step-card">
  <h3>Step 7: Business Profile Aur Udyam Integration Complete Karein</h3>
  <p>Dashboard me login karke profile section me jayein. Apne Udyam Registration Number, GSTIN, Company Address aur Bank Account details update karein.</p>
</div>

## Registration Ke Waqt Hone Wali Common Mistakes

1. **Name Mismatch Error**: PAN, Aadhaar aur GST me business ya owner ka naam alag hona.
2. **Unlinked Mobile Number**: Aadhaar se mobile number link na hona, jisse OTP nahi milta.
3. **Wrong Business Type Selection**: Proprietorship firm ke case me Company select kar lena.
4. **Incorrect IFSC Code**: Bank details me galat IFSC daalne se penny drop verification fail hona.
5. **Incomplete Profile**: Basic registration ke baad Profile Completion (Company, Bank, Office Address) na karna.

## Official Procurement Rules & Compliance

- **Rule 149 of GFR 2017**: General Financial Rules ke mutabiq sabhi sarkari vibhag GeM portal se hi khareedari karne ke liye baadhya hain.
- **MSME Policy 2012**: GeM par registered MSME sellers ko EMD (Earnest Money Deposit) me chhoot aur 25% procurement reservation ka labh milta hai.
- **Caution Money Deposit**: High-value bidding ke liye sellers ko profile completion ke baad GeM portal par nirdharit Caution Money deposit karna hota hai.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Kya GeM registration ke liye koi official fee lagti hai?
Nahi, GeM portal par seller registration 100% free hai. Government iske liye koi initial fee nahi leti.

### Q2. Kya Bina GST Number ke GeM seller registration ho sakta hai?
Haan, agar aapka business GST exemption limit ke andar aata hai ya aap exempted categories me deal karte hain, toh aap bina GSTIN ke bhi register kar sakte hain.

### Q3. GeM registration ke liye Udyam certificate zaroori hai kya?
Mandatory nahi hai, lekin Udyam certificate link karne se aapko MSME tender fee waiver, EMD exemption aur purchase preference milti hai.

### Q4. Registration me kitna time lagta hai?
Agar aapke saare documents tayyar hain aur Aadhaar OTP turant mil jata hai, toh registration 15 se 20 minute me poora ho jata hai.

### Q5. Aadhaar OTP not received problem ko kaise solve karein?
Yadi OTP nahi aa raha hai, toh check karein ki aapka mobile number Aadhaar database me updated hai ya nahi. Aap Unique Identification Authority (UIDAI) portal par ise verify kar sakte hain.

### Q6. Primary User aur Secondary User me kya farq hai?
Primary User business owner ya authorized signatory hota hai jo main account manage karta hai. Secondary User employees hote hain jinhein specific roles (jaise bidding, cataloging) assign kiye jate hain.

### Q7. Kya ek PAN number se multiple GeM accounts banaye ja sakte hain?
Nahi, ek PAN card par sirf ek hi primary seller account banaya ja sakta hai.

### Q8. Registration ke baad pehla step kya hota hai?
Registration ke baad sabse pehle apni Profile 100% complete karein, apni Brand/Product Catalogue upload karein, aur relevant tenders search karna shuru karein.
