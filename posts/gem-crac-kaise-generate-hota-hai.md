## Introduction

Dosto, Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par order deliver karna hi half battle win karna hai. Half battle win karne ke baad payments tabhi release hoti hain jab system me **CRAC (Consignee Receipt and Acceptance Certificate)** generate hota hai. 

Agar aap ek GeM seller hain, toh aapko pata hoga ki delivery ke baad sabse badi tension payment ki hoti hai. Aur payment aane ka direct link CRAC se hota hai. Lekin kya aapko pata hai ki CRAC kaise generate hota hai? Consignee ki verification rules kya hain? Aur agar buyer/consignee idle baitha rahe, toh 10 days ke default timeline me auto-generate hone ke kya rules hain?

Is detailed guide me hum CRAC ki poori technical aur regulatory details ko simple Hinglish me samjhenge taaki aapka working capital system me block na ho.

## Prerequisites Before CRAC Generation

CRAC generate hone se pehle niche diye gaye requirements complete hona zaroori hain:
1. **Successful Delivery Update on Portal**: Seller ko dispatch history update karke portal par invoice aur LR (Lorry Receipt) / consignment details upload karni hogi.
2. **Physical Delivery Receipt**: Goods physically consignee ke location par reach hone chahiye.
3. **PRC Generation**: Consignee ko physical verification ke baad 48 hours ke andar Provisional Receipt Certificate (PRC) generate karna chahiye.

## CRAC Eligibility & Status Criteria Table

Consignee ke response aur actions ke base par system me status badalte hain. Yeh criteria table dekhein:

| Delivery State | Consignee Verification Status | SLA Window | System Action & Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goods physically delivered & PRC generated | Manually approved | Within 10 Days | Manual CRAC generated, billing path open |
| Goods physically delivered & PRC generated | Manually rejected (Quality/Quantity issue) | Within 10 Days | Rejection Certificate generated, dispute raised |
| Goods physically delivered & PRC generated | Idle (No action taken by Consignee) | After 10 Days | **Auto-CRAC generated** by system on 11th Day |
| Dispatch updated but physical goods not received | Pending PRC | Not applicable | Auto-CRAC trigger cannot start; status shows pending |

## Step-by-Step Guide: GeM CRAC Generation Process

Agar aapko delivery complete hone ke baad CRAC certificate check karna hai toh niche diye gaye steps ko dhyan se follow karein:

### Step 1: Shipment Dispatch Aur Portal Update (Seller Action)
* **Why**: System trigger set karne ke liye delivery log update karna pehla milestone hai.
* **Action**: Goods dispatch karne ke baad, GeM Seller portal par login karein, **Shipment** tab me details enter karein, invoice copy, dispatch tracking number, and LR copy upload karke 'Generate Shipment' par click karein.
* **Pro Tip**: Delivery proof ke roop me hamesha clear scanned copy hi upload karein taaki technical rejection na ho.

### Step 2: Provisional Receipt Certificate (PRC) Generation (Consignee Action)
* **Why**: Consignee delivery accept karta hai.
* **Action**: Goods deliver hote hi consignee dashboard me login karega. Consignee ko **48 hours** ke andar Provisional Receipt Certificate (PRC) download/generate karna hota hai jo physical package receipt verify karta hai.

### Step 3: Quality Check & Inspection Window
* **Why**: Quality & quantity matching ensure karne ke liye.
* **Action**: Rules ke mutabik consignee ke paas inspection ke liye **10 calendar days** hote hain. Is window me consignee inspect karega ki specifications standard guidelines ke mutabik hain ya nahi.

### Step 4: Final Acceptance & Manual CRAC Generation (Consignee Action)
* **Why**: Final confirmation certificate generate karne ke liye.
* **Action**: Verification successful hone par consignee portal par digital validation (OTP/DSC) ke through **CRAC** approve karta hai. Iss manual approval ke baad CRAC PDF seller dashboard par reflection dene lagta hai.

### Step 5: System Auto-CRAC Trigger (Auto-Generate Mechanism)
* **Why**: Payments hold hone se rokne ke liye.
* **Action**: Agar consignee 10 days tak passive/idle rehta hai aur na rejection update karta hai aur na hi manual approval, toh **11th day** ko portal automatically auto-generate mechanism trigger kar deta hai. Is system-generated document ko **Auto-CRAC** kaha jata hai jo fully valid agreement document hota hai.

## ⚠️ Important Warning & SLA Compliance

> [!WARNING]
> **Delivery Update Kiye Bina Goods Deliver Karne Ki Bhul Na Karein!**
> Kai sellers physical goods toh dispatch kar dete hain par portal par **"Generate Shipment"** karna bhul jate hain ya der se karte hain. Yaad rakhein, 10-day auto-CRAC system tabhi start hoga jab portal par dispatch data update hoga aur PRC generate hogi. Agar portal update zero hai, toh system auto-CRAC schedule trigger nahi karega aur aapki payment infinite block stage me ja sakti hai!

## Comparison Table: Manual CRAC vs Auto-CRAC

| Feature | Manual CRAC Generation | Auto-CRAC (System-Generated) |
|---|---|---|
| **Trigger Mechanism** | Consignee manually logs in and verifies quality/quantity online. | System automatically generates CRAC if consignee is idle. |
| **Timeline** | Anytime within 10 days of PRC generation. | Exactly on the 11th day after PRC/delivery update. |
| **Inspection Status** | Consignee confirms that quality/quantity matches specs. | Assumed accepted (System assumes no issues since no rejection filed). |
| **Seller Payment Process** | Can be started immediately after manual CRAC is generated. | Can be started on the 11th day after system auto-generates it. |
| **Risk of Delay** | High, if consignee forgets to log in and approve. | Low, since system acts as a safety net for the seller. |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. GeM me CRAC ka full form kya hota hai?
CRAC ka full form 'Consignee Receipt and Acceptance Certificate' hota hai. Yeh document confirm karta hai ki buyer ko goods mil chuke hain aur unki quality & quantity dono check kar li gayi hai.

### Q2. CRAC generate hone ki default timeline kya hai?
GeM GTC ke mutabik, physical goods delivery ke baad Consignee ko 10 calendar days ke andar CRAC generate karna hota hai.

### Q3. Auto-CRAC kya hota hai aur yeh kab generate hota hai?
Agar Consignee delivery update hone ke 10 calendar days ke andar manual action (acceptance ya rejection) nahi leta, toh system 11th day par automatic CRAC generate kar deta hai. Ise Auto-CRAC kehte hain.

### Q4. Kya Auto-CRAC tabhi generate hoga jab PRC (Provisional Receipt Certificate) ban chuka ho?
Haan, consignee dwara physical receipt par PRC generate hone ke baad hi 10-day inspection period shuru hota hai. Agar consignee PRC generate nahi karta, toh system dispatch update ke timeline se rules check karta hai.

### Q5. CRAC aur PRC (Provisional Receipt Certificate) me kya difference hai?
PRC sirf physical receipt of goods confirm karta hai (delivery ke 48 hours ke andar). CRAC final quality aur quantity acceptance ko confirm karta hai (delivery ke 10 days ke andar).

### Q6. Kya buyer bina verification ke manual CRAC generate kar sakta hai?
Haan, consignee physically verify karne ke baad digital verification (OTP/DSC) ke through dashboard par click karke manually CRAC generate kar sakta hai.

### Q7. Agar consignee material reject kar de, toh kya CRAC generate hoga?
Nahi, rejection ke case me Rejection Certificate generate hota hai, CRAC nahi. Seller ko rejection report and incident details dashboard par dikh jayengi.

### Q8. Auto-CRAC rule ka main objective kya hai?
Auto-CRAC ka main objective sellers ko timely payment dilwana hai, taaki consignee bina kisi valid reason ke material clearance aur bills ko hold na kar sake.

### Q9. Kya CRAC generate hone ke baad payment automatic ho jata hai?
Nahi, CRAC generate hone ke baad seller ko online bill generate karke buyer ko submit karna padta hai. Bill submission ke 10 days ke andar buyer ko payment process karni hoti hai.

### Q10. Agar buyer auto-generate CRAC ko block karna chahe, toh kya rules hain?
Buyer auto-CRAC ko block nahi kar sakta jab tak ki woh portal par proper reason ke sath rejection process na shuru kare. Idle rehne par system automatic CRAC generate kar dega.

### Q11. Service contracts me CRAC kaise generate hota hai?
Services me CRAC ki jagah JCC (Joint Consensus Certificate) ya Service CRAC generate hota hai. Isme system billing period aur service completion reports (SCR) check karta hai.

### Q12. Kya small values (under ₹25,000) ke orders me bhi 10 days ka rule lagta hai?
Haan, 10 days auto-CRAC rule sabhi value levels (Direct Purchase, L1 Comparison, aur Bids/RAs) par equally apply hota hai.

### Q13. Rejection certificate generate hone ke baad seller kya kar sakta hai?
Seller product replace kar sakta hai ya buyer ke technical rejection reason ke khilaf representation write/incident handle kar sakta hai.

### Q14. Kya Auto-CRAC generate hone par audit check me objection ho sakta hai?
Nahi, Auto-CRAC ek official system-generated document hai jise Ministry of Finance aur GeM GTC dwara standard business rule ke roop me valid mana gaya hai.

### Q15. Portal par CRAC status kaise check karein?
Seller dashboard me 'Orders' tab par click karein, order number search karein, aur fulfillment section me 'PRC' and 'CRAC' columns me check karein.
