## Introduction to GeM Bidding Guidelines

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) functions under the General Terms and Conditions (GTC) and Special Terms and Conditions (SSTC) of the portal. In 2026, government bidding on GeM is entirely digital, ensuring transparent transaction paths between buyer ministries and qualified sellers.

To successfully participate, a seller must have a verified profile matching 100% completion metrics. Bids generally consist of a Technical Bid (qualifying criteria, documents, and product specs) and a Financial Bid (pricing parameters).

---

## Types of Bid Evaluations on GeM

Bids published by government buyers are evaluated using standard methods:

1. **L1 Evaluation (Lowest Bidder):** The contract is awarded to the lowest-priced bidder among those who qualify technically.
2. **Reverse Auction (RA):** An online interactive bidding phase where technically qualified sellers lower their prices in real-time.
3. **High-Value Technical Evaluation:** Used for complex services where technical scoring decides final bid matching matrices.

| Evaluation Type | Main Parameter | Best Fit For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L1 Bidding** | Lowest Price | Standard Goods (e.g. Desktops, Chairs) |
| **Reverse Auction (RA)** | Active Price Decrements | Dynamic Bulk Tenders |
| **Technical Scoring** | Quality-Cost Based (QCBS) | Complex Consulting Services |

## Key Bidding Stages & Timelines

The lifecycle of a GeM bid progresses through strict phases:

*   **Pre-Bid Phase:** Sellers review the bid documents and submit pre-bid queries if specifications seem restrictive.
*   **Bid Submission:** Uploading technical documents, caution money receipts, and financial schedules.
*   **Technical Bid Opening:** The buyer reviews the document checklist and accepts/disqualifies sellers.
*   **Representation Window:** Disqualified sellers have a **48-hour window** to submit technical appeals.
*   **Financial Opening & Award:** The system opens financial bids of qualified sellers, establishes the L1, and initiates price matching or RA.

## General Financial Rules (GFR) Compliance

Under GFR Rule 144, public procurement processes must ensure efficiency, economy, and fair competition. GeM is designed to enforce this policy natively. However, sellers must ensure they:
*   Link their valid Udyam certificates to claim MSE purchase preferences.
*   Validate compliance with local content rules (Make In India or MII guidelines).
*   Avoid bid splitting tactics that attempt to bypass direct purchase thresholds.
