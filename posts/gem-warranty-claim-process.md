# GeM Warranty Claim Process: Ticket Creation, OEM Response Timelines & Refund Rules

Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par jab koi department heavy equipment, IT products, ya office appliances procure karta hai, toh buyer ki priority product durability aur maintenance assurance hoti hai. Har sarkari purchase order standard terms aur warranty conditions ke sath generate hota hai. 

Lekin kya ho agar delivery ke kuch mahino baad hi product kaam karna band kar de? Tab as a buyer, aapko product warranty process ko initiate karna hota hai, aur as a seller/OEM, aapko time-bound manner me technical resolution provide karna padta hai. Chaliye is comprehensive guide ke zariye poore online GeM Warranty Claim, ticket dashboard creation rules, response SLA timelines aur refund policies ko detailed Hinglish me samajhte hain.

---

## ⚡ Quick Answer: GeM Warranty Claim Rule Kya Hai?
*   **Ticket Creation:** Buyer GeM portal dashboard me logged-in state me warranty support ticket raise kar sakta hai.
*   **OEM Response Timeline (SLA):** Ticket generate hone ke baad, Seller/OEM ko **48 hours** me initial response dena hota hai aur general items ke liye **15 days** ke andar product repair ya replacement resolve karna compulsory hai.
*   **SLA Breach Penalty:** Agar OEM specified SLA time window me response ya service nahi deta, to system auto-escalate karke incident raise karta hai, product catalog suspend ho sakta hai, aur seller account temporary block ya L1 exclusion face kar sakta hai.
*   **Refund Options:** Warranty resolution na milne par buyer **Refund Option** select kar sakta hai aur payment release nahi hoti. Agar delivery ho chuki hai, to security money (ePBG) withhold or forfeit ki ja sakti hai.

## 1. Introduction to GeM Warranty Claims
Sarkari procurement me hardware, machinery, aur critical equipment purchase karte waqt, quality standards ensure karne ke liye rigid guidelines hoti hain. General Financial Rules (GFR) ke tehat, equipment delivery and installation ho jane ke baad continuous runtime check kiya jata hai. 

GeM Portal ne manual follow-ups ko eliminate karne ke liye ek integrated online system lagaya hai jise **Warranty Management Module** kehte hain. Is platform par agar machine me manufacturing defect aata hai ya technical breakdown hota hai, toh Buyer portal par ticket generate karta hai. Is centralized registry se OEM/Seller response record hota hai taaki unka portal performance dynamic audit ho sake.

## 3. Prerequisites for Creating a Warranty Ticket
Warranty claim initiate karne ke liye aapke pass niche likhi criteria clear honi chahiye:
1.  **Valid Order with Active Warranty:** Contract/Bid parameters ke anusaar warranty expiry date pass nahi honi chahiye.
2.  **Consignee Receipt and Acceptance Certificate (CRAC):** Delivery complete ho chuki ho aur material state standard acceptance sheet sign ho chuki ho.
3.  **Active OEM/Seller Profile:** Seller profile system par registered status me active hona chahiye.

## 5. OEM SLA & Eligibility Criteria Table

| Criteria Parameter | Requirement / Eligibility Condition | Compliance Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Active Warranty Period** | The claim must be raised within the warranty timeline defined in Bid/Contract | Claims raised post-warranty expiry are legally invalid |
| **Product Condition** | Defect must due to manufacturing failure, not user physical damage | Wear and tear or water/voltage damages are evaluated on a case-to-case basis |
| **OEM Status on Portal** | OEM must be active or have active representative on GeM | Even if OEM is suspended, the reseller shares joint-liability |
| **Registered Buyer** | Only the primary consignee or authorized buyer officer can raise claims | End-users cannot directly raise claims without buyer approval |

## 6. Step-by-Step Warranty Ticket Creation & Resolution Process

Niche diye flowchart se samajhein ki GeM contract support ticket flow se lekar status resolution tak kaise handle hota hai:



### Detailed Steps:
1.  **Step 1: Ticket Initiate Karna:** Buyer portal dashboard me log-in karein aur 'Contract' segment se specific purchase order select karein.
2.  **Step 2: Warranty Claim Form Open Karein:** Order page par item details section me "Warranty Claim / Ticket Creation" option select karein.
3.  **Step 3: Defect Parameters Specification:** Serial Number, Invoice reference number aur defect description enter karein.
4.  **Step 4: Attachments Upload Karein:** Diagnostic reports, error logs aur defect snapshots add karein taaki OEM initial verify kar sake.
5.  **Step 5: SLA Tracking & Follow-up:** Submit button click karne par system alert notification generator trigger ho jayega. Dashboard me dynamic counter SLA timeline reflect hone lagegi.
6.  **Step 6: Resolution Verification:** Jab OEM product repair ya component swap kar le, to onsite verification sheet upload karke ticket status 'Resolved' request karein. Buyer check karke status approve ya escalate karega.

## 8. Warning: Critical Warranty SLA Timelines & Compliance Rules

> [!WARNING]
> **Important SLA & Account Suspension Warnings for Sellers/OEMs:**
> 1. **48-Hour Response SLA:** Aggar ticket open hone ke 48 hours ke andar response register nahi kiya, to system automatically alert trigger karta hai. Multiple failures par automatic "Incident" log ho jata hai.
> 2. **15-Day Completion SLA:** Hardware ya critical equipment ko maximum 15 calendar days ke andar repair ya replace karna hoga. Specific categories (jaise healthcare devices, servers) ke liye yeh time aur bhi short (ATC terms ke mutabik) ho sakta hai.
> 3. **Joint and Several Liability:** GeM rules ke anusaar, reseller aur OEM dono warranty fulfill karne ke liye jointly responsible hote hain. Agar OEM response nahi deta, to reseller ke dashboard par restriction lag sakti hai.
> 4. **De-registration Risk:** Defective products ki replace na karne par permanent red-listing ya debarment ho sakta hai as per Incident Management policy.

## 9. Comparison: Warranty Repair vs. Buyer Refund/Replacement

Agar product me defect aata hai to standard guidelines ke tehat teen dynamic solutions offer kiye jate hain:

| Feature / Parameter | Warranty Repair (Service) | Product Replacement (Swap) | Buyer Refund / Forfeiture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Applicability Condition** | Component error, small firmware bugs, minor replacement | Major motherboard failures, recurrent system failures | OEM unable to repair or replace within SLA timeline |
| **Asset Retained** | Original physical asset remains with Buyer | Original asset returned; new identical model provided | Asset returned to seller; payment refunded or held |
| **SLA Limit** | Max 15 Days | Max 15-30 Days (depending on stock) | Immediate action post incident escalation |
| **ePBG / Payment Impact** | No impact on release if done successfully | ePBG released post validation of replacement | ePBG forfeited; pending payments canceled |
| **Rating Score Penalty** | Zero penalty if resolved within SLA | Minimal rating impact | Permanent account suspension and negative rating |

## 11. Structured FAQs (15 Detailed Q&As)

### 1. GeM portal par warranty ticket raise karne ki time limit kya hai?
Warranty claim ticket humesha contract me specified warranty period (e.g., 1 year, 3 years) active hone ke dauran hi raise kiya ja sakta hai. Warranty duration complete hone ke baad portal par claims submit karne ka provision nahi rehta.

### 2. Agar seller aur OEM dono warranty claim resolve nahi kar rahe, toh buyer kya kare?
Aise case me buyer portal par standard "Incident Management" system ke zariye incident log kar sakta hai. GeM authorities check karke seller/OEM ke profile ko suspend aur block kar sakti hain.

### 3. Warranty issue me OEM response SLA timeline kya hoti hai?
Ticket raise hote hi, OEM ya Seller ko strictly **48 hours** ke andar ticket ko acknowledge aur respond karna hota hai. Failure to do so system guidelines ke against compliance breach maana jata hai.

### 4. Replacement ya repair complete karne ke liye kitna time diya jata hai?
Standard terms ke mutabik resolution deadline **15 days** hoti hai. Delivery and installation place remote hone par or specific contract conditions me is timeframe ko alter bhi kiya ja sakta hai.

### 5. Kya main online delivery certificate (CRAC) approve hone se pehle warranty claim create kar sakta hoon?
Nahi. Jab tak Consignee Receipt and Acceptance Certificate (CRAC) generate nahi hota, tab tak product formally accepted nahi hota. Product transition state me direct replacement or return options choose kiye ja sakte hain, warranty claim nahi.

### 6. Agar repairable defect nahi hai, toh kya OEM ko naya product dena hoga?
Haan, agar tech engineer verify karta hai ki defect non-repairable manufacturing flaw hai, toh OEM/Seller ko same model ya higher specification ka brand-new product as replacement provide karna padta hai.

### 7. Kya reseller, OEM ki behalf par warranty service de sakta hai?
Haan. GeM contracts me Reseller aur OEM dono joint-liability share karte hain. Reseller apni local service team deploy karke ticket resolve kar sakta hai aur resolution sheet update kar sakta hai.

### 8. Agar warranty fail hone par buyer refund chahta hai, toh iska rule kya hai?
Agar delivery accept hone ke baad standard warranty fail hoti hai aur alternate repair/replacement nahi hota, to buyer remaining payment hold kar sakta hai aur security deposit (ePBG) forfeit karke loss recover kar sakta hai.

### 9. Warranty ticket raised status me payment rules par kya impact padta hai?
Agar order payment fully clear nahi hui hai (jaise balance 10% payment pending ho), to warranty claim active hone par buyer payment release process ko block/hold kar sakta hai jab tak resolution issue update na ho.

### 10. Performance Bank Guarantee (ePBG) ko warranty breach par kaise forfeit kiya jata hai?
Buyer portal par warranty failure incident raise karega. Default verify hone par, buyer department apne bank ko directive bhejta hai ki ePBG amount departmental account me transfer kar diya jaye.

### 11. Warranty SLA time limit exceed hone par penalty kaise calculate hoti hai?
Contract key terms ke mutabik delay period ke liye liquid damage (LD) charges impose ho sakte hain. General rules me per day delay price deduction default parameters me apply ki jati hai.

### 12. Warranty ticket close kaise hota hai?
Product repair ya replace hone ke baad, OEM/Seller ticket par status update karta hai aur dispatch details dalta hai. Buyer resolution check karke ticket ko manually close or accept mark karta hai.

### 13. Kya de-registered products par bhi warranty validity bani rehti hai?
Haan, agar koi OEM ya product model portal se suspend/remove bhi ho jaye, tab bhi custom order and contract dates ke anusaar warranty period valid rehta hai aur vendor contractually support dene ke liye bound hai.

### 14. Kya service warranty claims and product replacement claims alag hote hain?
Product warranty me physical hardware and components replace hote hain, jabki service contracts me AMC/SLA (Annual Maintenance Contract) performance guidelines execute hoti hain jisme service response engineer visit delay penalties involve hoti hain.

### 15. Agar user ke misuse ke karan failure hua hai, toh OEM ticket kaise reject kare?
OEM ticket response panel me inspection sheet upload kar sakta hai jisme physical misuse (water logs, broken display) clear prove ho. Validation ke baad GeM support ticket ko reject status me set kar deta hai.
