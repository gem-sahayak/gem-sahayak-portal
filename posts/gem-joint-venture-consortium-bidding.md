# GeM Portal Joint Venture & Consortium Bidding Rules: Complete Guide 2026

Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par bad tenders aur complex services ke liye bidding rules kafi detailed hote hain. Jab koi single company ya vendor kisi bad bid ki financial ya technical eligibility criteria akele meet nahi kar paata, tab **Joint Venture (JV)** aur **Consortium** bidding ka options kaafi helpful sabit hota hai.

Lekin GeM portal par JV aur Consortium ke rules strictly regulate hote hain. GFR 2017 Rules aur GeM General Terms and Conditions (GTC) ke guidelines ko follow kiye bina bid submit karne par aapki bid bina kisi warning ke technical stage par reject ho sakti hai. Is article me hum is process ke har ek aspect ko aasan Hinglish me samjhenge.

---

## ⚡ Quick Answer: JV/Consortium Bidding Rules
> [!NOTE]
> **⚡ Quick Answer:** GeM portal par Joint Venture (JV) ya Consortium bidding tabhi possible hai jab Tender Document ke **Additional Terms & Conditions (ATC)** me iski specific permission di gayi ho. Ise participate karne ke liye ek **Lead Member** decide kiya jata hai, jiske profile se bid submit hoti hai. Ek registered **JV/Consortium Agreement (MOU)** aur **Power of Attorney (PoA)** submit karna mandatory hota hai. Joint and several liability ke rules sabhi partners par apply hote hain.

## Introduction: GeM Par JV aur Consortium Bidding Kya Hai?

Indian Public Procurement me bade tenders aur projects ki requirements kafi high hoti hain. Kai baar ek single seller ya company ke paas sabhi credentials nahi hote—jaise turnover requirement bohot zyada ho ya specific technical experience ki kami ho. Aise situations me, companies aapas me haath milati hain aur **Joint Venture (JV)** ya **Consortium** banakar bidding karti hain.

Government e-Marketplace (GeM) portal par bhi buyers ko yah option milta hai ki woh bad projects ke liye JV aur Consortium bidding allow karein. Lekin GeM portal par iske rules kafi strict hain. GFR 2017 ke provisions aur GeM General Terms and Conditions (GTC) ke mutabik, agar aap rules ko follow nahi karenge toh aapka bid technical evaluation stage par hi reject ho jayega. 

Is detailed guide me hum samjhenge ki JV aur Consortium bidding ke rules kya hain, documents kaun se chahiye, and system par step-by-step process kaise follow hoti hai.

## Prerequisites: Bidding Start Karne Se Pehle Ki Tayyari

GeM par JV/Consortium bid submit karne se pehle in basic requirements ko poora karna zaroori hai:

1.  **Lead Partner Registration:** Lead Member ka GeM portal par active seller account hona zaroori hai. Bidding lead partner ke profile se hi execute hogi.
2.  **Tender Verification:** Sabse pehle Bid Document (ATC) download karke check karein ki kya "Joint Venture" or "Consortium" bidding allowed hai.
3.  **Active Partnership Deed / MoU:** Bidding ki dates se pehle hi valid legal agreement tayyar hona chahiye. Backdated agreements allowed nahi hote.
4.  **PAN & GST Compliance:** Lead member aur sabhi partner members ke paas valid PAN and GST registrations hone chahiye.

---

## Required Documents Checklist

JV/Consortium bidding ke liye aapko niche diye gaye documents submit karne hote hain. Inhe carefully scan aur verify karke upload karein:

- [ ] **JV / Consortium Agreement (MoU):** ₹100 ya usse zyada ke Non-Judicial Stamp Paper par drafted aur notarized agreement. Isme clear shareholding, profit/loss ratio, aur roles & responsibilities likhi honi chahiye.
- [ ] **Power of Attorney (PoA):** Sabhi partners dwara sign kiya gaya ek authorization document jo Lead Partner ke designated representative ko bid submit karne aur legally bind karne ka power deta hai.
- [ ] **Board Resolution:** Agar koi partner Private Limited ya LLP company hai, toh unke board of directors ka resolution sheet jo JV me enter karne aur PoA issue karne ki permission deta hai.
- [ ] **Financial Audited Reports:** Sabhi participating members ki pichle 3 saal ki CA-certified Balance Sheets aur Profit & Loss statements.
- [ ] **Technical Credentials:** Members ke past experience certificates (Work Completion Certificates, CRAC, or Invoice details) jo project work-experience meet karte hon.
- [ ] **Border Sharing Compliance Declaration:** Under Rule 144(xi) of GFR 2017, sabhi partners ka land border sharing declaration form.

## Eligibility Criteria Table

Tender bid evaluate karte waqt criteria kaise check kiya jata hai, uska description niche table me hai:

| Criterion | Rule for Lead Partner | Rule for Other Partners | Evaluation Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Financial Turnover** | Usually must meet minimum 40% to 50% of the total tender turnover requirement. | Must meet at least 20% to 25% of the turnover requirement. | Combined turnover of all members is aggregated to meet the 100% tender requirement. |
| **Technical Experience** | Must have executed at least one or two works of similar nature matching a specific percentage (e.g. 50%). | Can contribute supporting experience, depending on the role specified in the MoU. | Checked against the scope of work allocated to each partner in the JV Agreement. |
| **Net Worth** | Must be positive in the last three financial years. | Must be positive in the last three financial years. | Checked individually for each partner to ensure financial stability. |
| **Blacklisting Status** | Must not be blacklisted or suspended by any Govt entity. | Must not be blacklisted or suspended by any Govt entity. | Even if one partner is blacklisted, the entire JV bid will be disqualified. |

## Step-by-Step Guide: GeM Par JV/Consortium Bid Kaise Submit Karein

Niche diye gaye lifecycle chart aur steps ko follow karein:



### Step 1: Tender Document (ATC) Review Karein
Sabse pehle GeM portal par target tender search karein. Uska PDF download karein aur **Additional Terms and Conditions (ATC)** section me check karein ki kya Joint Venture or Consortium bidding permitted hai. Agar permission nahi hai, toh aap as a JV apply nahi kar sakte.

### Step 2: Legal Agreements Tayyar Karein
Agar permission hai, toh sabhi partners milkar ek **Joint Venture Agreement/MoU** draft karein. Is agreement me:
*   Kaun Lead Partner hoga.
*   Kaun sa partner kaun sa kaam (scope of work) karega.
*   Financial share (equity ratio) kya hoga.
Iske sath hi ek **Power of Attorney (PoA)** banayein jo Lead Partner ko legal rights de. Dono documents ko legal stamp paper par sign aur notarize karwayein.

### Step 3: Lead Partner Ka GeM Profile Update Karein
Lead Partner apne registered GeM account me login karein. Profile section me details double-check karein. Ensure karein ki Lead Partner ke profile me generic requirements aur updates complete hon.

### Step 4: GeM Portal Par Bid Initiation
Bid participation page par jayein. Jab aap technical bid details fill karna start karenge, toh system aapko select karne ko puchega ki kya aap as a "JV/Consortium" bid karna chahte hain (agar buyer ne backend se yeh configure kiya hai). Select **Yes**.

### Step 5: Document Uploads & Verification
System par designated slots me:
1.  JV Agreement / MoU upload karein.
2.  Power of Attorney upload karein.
3.  Lead partner aur other partners ke individual financial certificates and experience certificates compile karke ek single PDF me ya separate specified folders me upload karein.

### Step 6: EMD Submission & Final E-sign
Tender requirements ke mutabik EMD (Earnest Money Deposit) submit karein. Agar EMD exemption claim kar rahe hain, toh check karein ki kya aap eligible hain. Sabhi documents upload karne ke baad bid ko digital signature (DSC) ya OTP ke zariye verify karke submit karein.

## Important Warnings for MSMEs & Sellers

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ Critical Warnings for JV/Consortium Bidders:**
> *   **MSE Benefit Rules:** Agar aap ek MSME (MSE) hain aur aapne kisi non-MSME company ke sath JV banaya hai, toh aapko MSE status ke benefits (jaise EMD exemption ya L1+15% price purchase preference) **nahi milenge**. Bidding rules ke mutabik, benefits tabhi milte hain jab JV ke saare partners MSEs hon ya JV firm khud register ho.
> *   **Joint & Several Liability:** Contract sign karne ke baad agar aapka partner beech me project chhod deta hai, toh lead partner poore project ko complete karne ke liye legally bound hota hai. Buyer lead partner se hi recovery aur penal actions (blacklisting/forfeiture) leva.
> *   **Land Border Clause:** Sabhi partner companies ko GFR Rule 144(xi) land border sharing rules comply karna hoga. Agar kisi ek partner ka connection restriction zones se hai bina government clearance ke, toh bid cancel ho jayegi.

## Comparison: Joint Venture vs. Consortium Bidding

Sellers aksar in dono concepts ke beech me confuse ho jaate hain. Niche di gayi comparison table se inka farq samjhein:

| Parameter | Joint Venture (JV) Bidding | Consortium Bidding |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Entity Setup** | Partners aapas me milkar ek temporary new legal relationship (new firm/entity structure) format karte hain. | Entities alag-alag rehti hain, bas ek common agreement (MOU) ke tehat project ke liye sath aati hain. |
| **Primary Registration** | Bidding ke liye new entity ya Lead Member ke credentials use ho sakte hain (depending on state/federal rules). | Hamesha Lead Member ke profile se bid submit hoti hai. |
| **Risk & Liability Sharing** | Risk aur profits pre-defined equity ratio me directly share hote hain. | Har partner sirf apne scope of work ke liye primary response hold karta hai, par liability buyer ke prati joint hoti hai. |
| **Evaluation Base** | Combined credentials ko single corporate identity ki tarah evaluate kiya jata hai. | Har partner ke credentials aur specific work role ko separately verify kiya jata hai. |
| **Best Suited For** | Large Infrastructure and Civil Engineering projects. | Supply of diverse goods, IT integration, and multi-disciplinary services. |

## Structured FAQs (15 Detailed Q&As)

### 1. Kya GeM portal par har tender me Joint Venture (JV) allowed hota hai?
Nahi, JV/Consortium participation tabhi allowed hota hai jab buyer ne bid documents/Additional Terms and Conditions (ATC) me isko explicitly allow kiya ho. Agar explicitly mention nahi hai, toh aap JV bid nahi daal sakte.

### 2. MoU/JV Agreement banate waqt stamp paper ki value kya honi chahiye?
Minimum ₹100 ke non-judicial stamp paper par JV Agreement banaya jata hai. Halaanki, state stamp laws ke according iski requirements change ho sakti hain, isliye tender document ke instructions ko dhyan se padhein.

### 3. Kya Lead Partner ka hona mandatory hai?
Haan, bilkul. Consortium ya JV bidding me ek designated Lead Partner (Lead Member) ka hona zaroori hai. Bidding process se lekar payment collection tak ki primary legal responsibility Lead Member ki hi hoti hai.

### 4. Kya individual partners ko apna GeM account banana hoga?
Sabhi partners ka separate GeM account hona mandatory nahi hai, lekin Lead Member ka account active aur verified hona chahiye kyuki bid unhi ke login portal se submit ki jayegi. Kuch cases me verification ke liye dusre members ke PAN/GST details maange ja sakte hain.

### 5. Turnover evaluation JV me kaise calculate hoti hai?
Generally, sabhi partners ka turnover add karke total evaluated turnover banaya jata hai. Par condition hoti hai ki Lead Partner kam se kam 40% ya 50% criteria akele meet kare, aur other partners 20-25% meet karein.

### 6. Kya JV bidding me MSME/MSE preference and benefits milte hain?
Agar JV/Consortium ke saare members registered MSEs hain, tabhi EMD exemption aur pricing preferences milte hain. Agar ek bhi partner non-MSE hai, toh preference benefits nahi diye jaate.

### 7. Power of Attorney (PoA) kisko issue ki jani chahiye?
PoA sabhi partners dwara signature hokar Lead Partner ke designated authorized signatory (jaise Director ya Partner) ke naam par issue honi chahiye, taaki woh bid aur contracts par sign kar sakein.

### 8. Agar lead partner change karna ho bid submission ke baad, toh kya rules hain?
Bid submission aur contract award hone ke baad Lead Partner ko change karne ki permission nahi di jaati. Aisa karne par contract terminate ho sakta hai aur bid security forfeit ho sakti hai.

### 9. Bidding ke time EMD (Earnest Money Deposit) kis ke naam par banni chahiye?
EMD hamesha JV/Consortium ke naam par ya fir Lead Partner ke naam par bank guarantee/DD/online payment ke roop me submit honi chahiye, jaisa bid document me specify kiya gaya ho.

### 10. Kya separate PAN and GST registration lena padta hai JV ke liye?
Agar aap as an 'Unincorporated JV/Consortium' bid kar rahe hain, toh separate PAN/GST ki zaroori nahi hoti, Lead Partner ke GST/PAN credentials par billing hoti hai. Lekin agar project award hone ke baad 'Incorporated JV' banayi jati hai, toh new PAN/GST register karna padega.

### 11. Land Border Sharing restrictions (Rule 144(xi) GFR) JV par kaise apply hoti hain?
Agar JV/Consortium ka koi bhi ek member land border sharing restriction country se belong karta hai aur unke paas competent authority ka registration nahi hai, toh puri JV disqualify ho jayegi.

### 12. Bidding phase ke dauran technical representation kaise submit karein agar buyer ne galat disqualification kiya ho?
Lead Partner apne GeM dashboard se login karke 'Technical Evaluation' stage ke dauran 48 hours ke andar representation click karke formal appeal document aur clarifications upload kar sakta hai.

### 13. Kya JV partners ka profit-sharing ratio tender documents me mention hona chahiye?
Haan, JV/Consortium Agreement ya MoU me profit-sharing, equity ratio, aur responsibilities clear terms me written honi chahiye. Buyer is agreement ko verify karta hai.

### 14. Agar JV project execution ke dauran default karti hai toh penalty kis par lagegi?
Penalty sabhi partners par apply hogi. Because of 'Joint and Several Liability', buyer kisi bhi ek partner se ya sabhi se complete penalty amount recover kar sakta hai aur sabhi ko blacklisting process me daal sakta hai.

### 15. Kya OEM Authorization Certificate (MAF) sabhi partners ke naam par hona chahiye?
OEM authorization/MAF us partner ke naam par ho sakta hai jo specific product supply kar raha hai ya fir designated Lead Partner ke naam par. Ise MoU me clear define kiya jana chahiye.
