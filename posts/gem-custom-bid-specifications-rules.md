# GeM Custom Bid Specifications Rules: Challenging Restrictive Clauses & Submission Guide

Government e-Marketplace (GeM Portal) par standard catalogs ke alawa, buyers ke paas specialized items khareedne ke liye **Custom Bids** create karne ka provision hota hai. Lekin kai baar sarkari departments custom parameters ka galat use karke aisi technical specifications daal dete hain jo sirf kisi ek specific brand ya supplier ko favor karti hain. Ise **Restrictive Specification Clause** kaha jata hai jo ki public procurement rules ke tehat poori tarah illegal hai.

Is detailed guide me hum samjhenge ki GeM Custom Bids ke specifications rules kya hain, restrictive clauses ko kaise pehchanein, portal par representation kaise submit karein aur GFR 2017 ke rules se buyer ko specs badalne par kaise majboor karein.

---

## ⚡ Quick Answer: GeM Custom Bid Rules Kya Hain?

> **Quick Answer:** GeM Custom Bid Rules ke mutabik, agar kisi government department ne custom bid me koi aisi technical specification daali hai jo kisi ek particular manufacturer ya OEM ko favor karti hai (Anti-Competitive/Restrictive Clause), toh sellers ke paas use challenge karne ka pura haq hai. GFR 2017 Rule 144(i)(b) aur CVC guidelines ke tehat technical criteria generic hona chahiye. Aap bid details page ke **Representations** tab par jaakar, bid publication ke 4 dino ke andar generic specifications, standards (jaise ISO/BIS), aur GFR compliance ke proof ke sath online challenge submit kar sakte hain.

## 💡 Introduction

Government e-Marketplace (GeM) par public procurement ko transparent aur competitive banane ke liye design kiya gaya hai. Jab kisi buyer department ko aisi specialized ya non-standard machinery, custom software development, ya proprietary tools purchase karne hote hain jo standard category list me available nahi hote, tab **Custom Bid** run ki jaati hai.

Lekin is process me ek loophole ka use karke kuch buyers "restrictive spec tailoring" karte hain. Aise specific dimensional criteria, specialized testing reports, ya proprietary licenses demand kiye jaate hain jo doosre equivalent manufacturers ke paas na ho. GFR 2017 aur Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) directives ke tehat ye anti-competitive practices strictly prohibited hain. Supplier ke paas right hota hai ki wo technical specifications ke biased hone par official challenge kare.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Guide with flow diagrams/mermaid blocks

Sarkari custom bid specifications ke restrictive clauses ko challenge karne ke liye is 6-step online process ko follow karein:

### Step 1: Scan and Highlight Restrictive Clauses
Bid PDF document download karein aur technical spec panel par focus karein. Agar wahan kisi specific dimension, safety criteria, certification, ya design requirement ko restrictive banaya hai, to use exact quote karein.

### Step 2: Prepare the Specification Matrix
Ek excel file me compare karein:
* *Buyer Clause Specification:* Jo buyer ne restrictively manga hai.
* *Alternative / Equivalent Specification:* Jo aam taur par standard marketplace me standard manufacturers ke paas hota hai.
* *Your Justification:* GFR policy aur common logic ke sath detailed text.

### Step 3: Login to GeM Portal and Locate the Bid
GeM Official portal (`gem.gov.in`) par login karein. Dashboard par **Bids** menu se **List of Bids** par click karein aur apna Bid Number search bar me daalein.

### Step 4: Click on 'Representations' Button
Bid box ke actions panel me **Representations/Clarification** option select karein. Is tab me jaakar click karein **"Create Technical Representation"** par.

### Step 5: Draft Detail Text & Upload PDF
* **Select Clause Category:** Select karein 'Technical Specifications'.
* **Draft Response:** Point-wise objection enter karein.
* **Upload Document:** Technical Comparison PDF aur equivalent certifications attach karein (Max 2MB). Click **Submit**.

### Step 6: Follow-up and Escalation (If needed)
Tender rules ke mutabik system technical evaluation/financial opening block kar deta hai javascript buyer har ek representation ko respond na kare. Agar response arbitary aata hai, to is complaint copy ko Nodal Officer aur Administrative Ministry ke pass email par direct send karein.

## ❓ Structured FAQs

### 1. GeM Custom Bid specifications rules kya hain?
Custom bids tab open ki jaati hain jab koi special category standard product list me nahi hoti. GeM rules ke tehat buyer ko specifications bilkul generic aur neutral rakhni chahiye taaki healthy competition promote ho aur kisi ek specific OEM ko advantage na mile.

### 2. Restrictive Technical Clause kya hota hai?
Jab buyer specifications me kisi particular model ke exact dimensions, patent standards ya non-reputed proprietary certifications ko mandatory bana deta hai jisse baaki standard manufacturers disqualify ho jayein, to use restrictive clause kaha jata hai.

### 3. Restrictive specifications ko challenge karne ki timeframe kya hai?
Bid publish hone ke baad maximum **4 din (96 hours)** ke andar hi representations submit karni hoti hain. Bid end date pass aane par yeh option system automatically disable kar deta hai.

### 4. GFR Rule 144(i)(b) isme kaise madad karta hai?
General Financial Rules (GFR) Rule 144(i)(b) explicitly states ki procurement specifications should be generic, clear, and must not be formulated to suit a specific brand, vendor or trade name. Aap is rule ka reference dekar buyer ki demand ko challenge kar sakte hain.

### 5. Kya representation resolution ke bina buyer financial bid open kar sakta hai?
Nahi. GeM system control ke tehat, technical evaluation finish karne aur sabhi online representations ka reply portal par submit karne se pehle financial bid open karne ki security permission buyer ko nahi hoti.

### 6. Kya reseller bhi representation file kar sakta hai ya sirf OEM hi kar sakta hai?
Dono kar sakte hain. Agar aap reseller hain aur product parameters generic hain, to aap valid details ke sath appeal kar sakte hain. Agar proprietary product hai, to OEM ke authorization ke sath appeal karna zaroori hai.

### 7. Agar buyer meri representation reject kar de to next step kya hai?
Agar buyer valid technical reason ke bina portal representation ko reject kar deta hai, to aap us department ke Senior Nodal Officer, Ministry Administrative Head, ya direct Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) portal par email compliance draft kar sakte hain.

### 8. Kya Startup aur MSMEs ke liye guidelines alag hain?
Custom bids me qualification criteria jaise Experience aur Turnover requirements me relaxation ka option buyer ko select karna padta hai. Agar relaxations nahi diye gaye hain, to aap ise challenge kar sakte hain.

### 9. CVC Guidelines restrictive specs ke khilaf kya action suggest karti hain?
CVC guidelines ke tehat specification tailoring ya restrictive parameters banana administrative misconduct/corruption ke equivalent mana jata hai. Aisa karne par tender cancel kiya ja sakta hai aur guilty officers par disciplinary action liya ja sakta hai.

### 10. Kya custom service bids me restrictive conditions ko challenge kar sakte hain?
Haan, standard custom service bids me turnover limit high rakhna, specific state experience mangna ya aisi certifications mangna jo standard service providers ke paas nahi hoti, challenge kiya ja sakta hai.

### 11. Frivolous representations submit karne se kya nuksan hota hai?
Agar koi seller sirf bid delay karne ya malicious intent se bina technical backing ke representation submit karta hai, to GeM unhe warning letter issue kar sakta hai aur incident management rule ke tehat portal se suspend bhi kiya ja sakta hai.

### 12. Kya challenge process direct pre-bid meeting me discuss kiya ja sakta hai?
Haan. Pre-bid meeting me hardcopy representation aur email verification submit karna sabse best secondary option hota hai.

### 13. Kya equivalent specification proof accept karna buyer ke liye mandatory hai?
Yes, agar proof validated standards (jaise NABL report, ISO/BIS standard) ke sath match hota hai, to buyer use reject nahi kar sakta unless unke paas solid technical defense ho.

### 14. Custom Bid specification doc kahan milega?
Custom Bid document download karne par usme specific performance sheet page par Generic Specifications and Parameters table rehti hai, wahan se criteria note karein.

### 15. Representation status kahan track karein?
Seller dashboard par 'Bids' section me jaiye, bid number search karein aur wahan active representations status (Pending, Answered, Rejected) dekh sakte hain.
