# GeM Bid Participation Fees Guide: EMD & PBG Rules Explained

> [!TIP]
> **Quick Answer:** GeM par bids participate karne ke liye aksar EMD (Earnest Money Deposit) dena hota hai. MSME aur Startup vendors ko typically EMD se exemption milti hai.

## At a Glance Dashboard
| Fee Type | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| EMD | Up to 5% of estimated bid value |
| Exemption | Micro/Small Enterprises, Startups |
| Refund | Auto-refund to unsuccessful bidders |
| Validity | Bid validity + 45 days |



## 1. Introduction
Government bidding mein non-serious players ko filter karne ke liye EMD aur ePBG ka system hai. Is article mein hum dekhenge ki GeM par Bid Participation Fees kaise kaam karti hai.

## 2. Who Should Read This Guide
- Bidders new to GeM
- MSME & Startup owners looking for exemptions
- Financial officers in bidding companies

## 3. Prerequisites
- Udyam Registration (for exemption)
- Bank Account linked with GeM / Advisory Bank

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- EMD Bank Guarantee / FDR
- Udyam Certificate (for exemption)
- Startup India Certificate (for exemption)

## 5. Eligibility Criteria Table
| Category | EMD Exemption Status |
| :--- | :--- |
| Micro & Small Enterprise (MSE) | Yes (Exempted) |
| Startup (DPIIT recognized) | Yes (Exempted) |
| Medium Enterprise & Others | No (Required) |

**Q1: How is EMD calculated?**

A: Yes, for unsuccessful bidders, it is refunded automatically.
