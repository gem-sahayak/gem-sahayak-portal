Partnership firms require specific legal documentation to activate their seller profile. Ensure you have these ready to prevent verification delays.

## Mandatory Documents Checklist

### 1. Partnership Deed
Your Partnership Deed must be registered with the Registrar of Firms. Ensure the scanned copy is clear and complete.

### 2. Authorization Letter
An Authorization Letter signed by all partners designating a primary bidding user is required. This delegates portal actions to the main account admin.

### 3. PAN and GST Cards
The PAN Card and registered GST Certificate must match the partnership firm's official name exactly.

Always upload clear, scanned PDF copies of all certificates to avoid technical evaluation rejections.
